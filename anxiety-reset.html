The four-step practice

01Recognise

Pause. Name the trigger with precision. Specificity dissolves the fog and interrupts the unconscious survival response.

02Regulate

Hand on chest. Breathe into the heart. The heart holds what the mind cannot resolve.

03Release

Acknowledge without judgment. Forgive yourself. Release resistance — it holds activation in place.

04Rise

Thank your heart. Notice what changed and what that change says about who you are becoming. Name the specific identity you choose in relation to this pattern, state it as a decision rather than a hope, and take one small action that gives that identity evidence. Each cycle expands your baseline capacity — the learning compounds.

The foundation every protocol is built on. Master the four steps here — then apply the same sequence to any specific state through the ten precision protocols in Personal Transformation.
