Settle in

The Clearing

Ten guided minutes to quiet the noise and bring you into a state where the work can land. Nothing is required of you but to listen.

Logged to your record when it finishes. You can stop at any point and it still counts.

Cue CardFour lines. Always here.

In the moment

You do not have to remember anything. Read the four lines and follow them.

  1. 01Name it. Out loud if you can. Not “I’m anxious” — finish the sentence. I am afraid that ______.
  2. 02Hand to heart. In for four. Out for six. Six times. The out-breath is the part that works.
  3. 03Stop fighting it. Let it be in the room with you. You are not agreeing with it. You are stopping the second fight.
  4. 04One breath as the person on the other side. Not a plan. One breath.

Then stop. Nothing else is required of you today. Log it later if you want to — it still counts.
