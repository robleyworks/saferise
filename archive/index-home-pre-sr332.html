01

On my own · what makes this different

Understanding it has never once stopped it.

You can explain the pattern. You know roughly where it came from. None of that turns up in the moment your reaction gets decided — because that is not a knowledge problem. It is a state problem, and a state does not respond to an argument.

Almost everything available to you works on the story. This works on the moment the story is running.

Where to begin

The one you open is the one the day calls for.

Fear, anger, overwhelm, grief, insecurity, shutdown. You are not choosing from a menu while you are in it — you already know which one it is.

02

With someone · what makes this different

You can only do your half. That turns out to be enough.

Most relationship work needs both people, in the same room, on the same day, both willing. Which is exactly why it mostly does not happen.

The pattern between you runs through your nervous system too. That is the half you can always reach — and changing it changes what the other half is responding to.

Where to begin

Pursuit, withdrawal, rupture, repair.

The argument is rarely where it begins. Each protocol works one pattern, from the side of it you can actually reach.

03

At work · what makes this different

This is not about getting better at the job.

Pressure does not reveal a gap in your ability. It reveals which version of you arrives when the stakes rise — and that was decided a long time before the meeting.

Work on the version that arrives, and the rest follows. Work on the room instead, and you are managing the symptom.

Where to begin

Visibility, conflict, belonging, burnout.

The moments where your ability matters most are the ones where something else is running you. Each protocol works one of them.
