Common Questions

Before You Begin

Is this therapy or clinical treatment?

No. The SafeRise Protocol is a self-regulation methodology — not therapy, not clinical treatment, and not a replacement for professional mental health support. It is a practical nervous system tool you can use in daily life, alongside any other support you are receiving.

Do I need a diagnosis or any prior experience?

No diagnosis required. No prior experience with breathwork, meditation, or therapy needed. You simply need to know what you are feeling right now. The protocols are designed to meet you exactly where you are.

How is this different from meditation apps?

Meditation apps teach you to maintain a state. The SafeRise Protocol is designed to shift you from one state to another — specifically when you are in activation, distress, or overwhelm. It works in the moments meditation is hardest to access.

I am already in therapy. Can I still use this?

Yes — and many people find the two work well together. Therapy builds long-term insight. The SafeRise Protocol gives you a tool for the moments between sessions when a trigger arrives and you need something right now.

What if I buy a module and it is not right for me?

We offer a 30-day satisfaction guarantee. If you have genuinely engaged with the content and do not feel it delivered value, contact us and we will make it right. No complicated process.
