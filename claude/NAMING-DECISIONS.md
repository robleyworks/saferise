# Naming — founder rulings

30 September 2026. These are settled. Any document or surface using an older
name is superseded, not in conflict.

---

## N1 · "My Records"

The member's records hub is **My Records**. One name, no subtitle.

**"The Lab" is not used anywhere** — not as a name, not as a subtitle, not as
a section heading, not in nav, not in copy, not in a file or class name. It was
considered and rejected: a lab is somewhere you *do* something, and the page
lets a member read, edit and delete. The name over-promised.

Five tabs: All Sessions · The Chosen Self · Decisions · Journal · Saved.
Full specification in `MY-RECORDS-HUB.md`.

The route follows the name: `/records`, with redirects from the live
`/record/chosen-self` and `/record/decisions`.

---

## N2 · "AI feedback"

The Sovereign AI feature is called **AI feedback** throughout the site —
every Sovereign featured section, the plans page, tier lists, marketing pages,
the dashboard, the protocol page and the B2B material.

**Do not introduce a second name.** "AI reading", "the reading", "synthesis"
and "summary" are not used on any surface. Where they appear in commercial or
product copy, change them.

Internal specification documents may keep their own working language —
`SOVEREIGN-FRAMEWORK-READING-SPEC.md` describes the mechanism, not the label
a member sees. Leave those and report them rather than editing.

### What AI feedback is, in one line for copy

> An AI reading of your own words: what you returned to, what shifted, and how
> you moved through Recognise, Regulate, Release and Rise.

### What it is never called

Not a summary. Not a report. Not an analysis. Not insights. Not coaching.

---

## N3 · "SafeRise Out Loud"

Founder-ruled, 30 September 2026 (recorded from `DASHBOARD-BANNERS.md`, built as
SR-488). **SafeRise Out Loud is the name for all of it: the podcast, articles
and short form.** It is not three pages. It is one destination with three sections
inside it: the podcast, Articles, Shorter form.

- The rail carries one entry, **Out Loud**. "Article" and "The SafeRise podcast"
  are retired as names and as rail entries.
- The route is `/out-loud`. `/writing` and `/podcast` 301 to their sections.
- A section keeps its own banner and copy, shown as a shorter band inside the
  destination, so it reads as moving within one place rather than arriving
  somewhere new.

---

## Why this matters more than it looks

Two names for one thing is the failure mode both of these rulings exist to
prevent. A member who reads "AI feedback" on the plans page and then meets
"your reading" in the product has to work out they are the same feature. A
member who reads "The Lab" in nav and "Your Saved Records" as a page title has
the same problem.

One thing, one name, everywhere.
