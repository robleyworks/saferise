# VIDEO — ART DIRECTION AND CALL SHEET

24 September 2026. Granular direction for every setup across the three films —
casting, wardrobe, environment, props, light, time of day, attitude and the
single activity each clip carries.

Use with `claude/VIDEO-GENERATION-SPEC.md` (the prompts) and
`claude/VIDEO-FOOTAGE-MANIFEST-THREE-FILMS.md` (what each film owes).

## PART ONE · GLOBAL DIRECTION

### 1 · The palette, and the one rule that protects it

The films live in the site's palette: near-black ground `#0A0A0F`, bone text
`#F5EDD8`, gold accent `#D4A843`, with sage, slate and a muted red as
secondaries.

> **Gold belongs to the light. Never to the wardrobe.**

Every warm highlight in these films — a lamp, low sun through a slat, a screen
glow — reads as the brand accent. The moment somebody wears gold, ochre or
mustard, the accent stops meaning anything. Keep clothing in the neutrals and
let the light carry the colour.

### 2 · Wardrobe — the permitted range

| | |
|---|---|
| **Yes** | charcoal · near-black · bone · oatmeal · stone · deep olive · muted slate blue · faded rust, small areas only |
| **Fabric** | matte and absorbent — cotton, linen, wool, brushed knit, unwashed denim |
| **Fit** | slightly lived-in. A collar that has been worn all day, a sleeve pushed up |

**Never on camera, and each for a reason:**

- **Pure white** — blows out under a single warm source and the grade cannot
  recover it. Use bone or oatmeal, which read as white and hold detail.
- **Saturated blue or red** — fights the gold accent and drags the grade cold.
- **Fine stripes, checks, herringbone** — the classic generation artefact.
  Repeating high-frequency pattern is where these models visibly fail.
- **Logos, lanyards, ID badges, branded uniform** — the no-branding rule, and
  generated text is always garbled.
- **Shiny fabric, satin, leather sheen** — specular highlights read as a stock
  photo and blow under a warm key.
- **Glasses** — reflections plus rims are two independent artefact sources.
  Drop them unless a character needs one, and none do.
- **Watches with a readable face, statement jewellery** — small detail the
  model renders as mush.
- **Anything freshly pressed.** These people are at the end of something.

### 3 · Grooming — found, not cast

Ordinary. Unstyled hair that has been through a day. **No visible makeup**,
no contouring, no gloss. Stubble where it is natural. A little shine on the
skin is correct — matte perfection reads as an advertisement, and the whole
film is an argument against advertisement.

**Faces should look like people you would not turn to look at.** The
moment somebody reads as a model, the viewer stops believing the story.

### 4 · Light — one source, hard falloff

| | |
|---|---|
| **Sources** | one practical — a window, a lamp, a doorway. A second only as a weak fill |
| **Direction** | side or three-quarter back. **Never flat frontal** |
| **Falloff** | steep. Most of the frame should sit in shadow |
| **Colour** | warm interior, or cool daylight in the workplace scenes. Never mixed in the same frame |

**Never: overhead fluorescent.** It flattens the face, greens the skin, kills
the shadow the grade is built on, and it is the single most common failure in
workplace stock. Name it in the negative prompt every time.

### 5 · Camera

| | |
|---|---|
| **Height** | eye level, seated subjects included. Never looking down on anyone |
| **Distance** | middle to close-middle. Head and shoulders plus room |
| **Lens feel** | 35–50mm. Shallow but not a portrait blur — the room must stay legible |
| **Movement** | locked, or the faintest handheld drift. **No push, no crane, no rack focus** |
| **Framing** | subject centred and upright, headroom above, **nothing load-bearing in the outer thirds** — every panel becomes a 384 × 1080 slice |

### 6 · The universal never list

Readable text of any kind · screens showing anything · phones and laptops in
the strain scenes · earbuds and headphones · crowds · mirrors and reflective
surfaces · anyone crossing in front of the lead · anybody smiling unless the
script asks · crying · hands over the face · head in hands · slow motion ·
children's faces in focus · anyone looking into the lens.

## P1 · OVERWHELM
**B2C 02** · 23.5s · **B2B 03** · 9.6s · 5 clips

### Model
Black Caribbean man, **late thirties to early forties**. Solid build, a little
heavier than he was. Short hair, close beard with grey starting at the chin.
Reads as somebody competent who has been carrying it a while. **Not a
executive-poster physique** — a middle manager who used to be the best
technician in the room.

### Wardrobe
Charcoal open-collar shirt, sleeves pushed to the forearm, no tie, top button
undone. A dark knit over the back of the chair, not worn. Plain dark trousers.
**Nothing corporate-crisp** — this shirt has been on since seven.

### Environment
A **small side office off a working floor** — not a corner office, not an open
desk. Partition glass on one side onto a corridor and a meeting room beyond.
Blinds half open. A pinboard with paper on it, nothing readable. A dead plant
or one that needs water.

**Scale matters:** the room should feel slightly too small for his seniority.

### Props
- A **stack of unopened folders**, squared off, untouched
- A **second chair on the visitor side, empty**, angled as if last used days ago
- A desk phone, dark
- A mug, gone cold, ring marks on the desk

**Never in frame:** an open laptop · a whiteboard with writing · anything
legible on the pinboard.

### Time of day
**Late afternoon into early evening.** The floor is emptying. Low sun through
the blinds on one side, the overheads not yet on.

### Light
Warm low sun through half-open blinds, camera left, laying slats across the
desk. The meeting room behind him lit cooler and brighter — **he is in the
warm dark, the decision is in the cold light.**

### Attitude
**Stalled, not defeated.** He is not sad. He is a competent person who cannot
find the first move, and is embarrassed about it. Jaw set. Eyes open and
unfocused. The tell is in the shoulders — held, not slumped.

⚠ **Do not play depression.** The line is *I cannot figure out how to get
started*, which is a jam, not a collapse.

### Activity, clip by clip
| | |
|---|---|
| **1** | Sits. One slow breath. Shapes behind the glass shift. **He does not turn** |
| **2** | The desk phone lights and goes dark, unanswered. One glance at it |
| **3** | The meeting-room light goes out behind him. He is still facing forward |
| **4** | One hand goes flat on the stack of folders and stays |
| **5** | The floor dark, his lamp on. Jaw set. **Freeze on the last frame** |

### The freeze
Hand still on the folders, looking at nothing, the dark office behind him.

## P2 · INSECURITY
**B2C 05** · 34.6s · **B2B 02** · 14.3s · 7 clips

### Model
Latino or mixed-heritage man, **early thirties**. Slim, neat, slightly boyish
for the room he is in. Recently promoted and dressed for it. **He looks like he
belongs there, which is the point** — the gap is entirely internal.

### Wardrobe
A good shirt, stone or pale oatmeal, sleeves down and buttoned. **Newer and
better-pressed than anyone else's in the film** — he is trying. A dark knit tie
or none. Dark trousers.

⚠ He is the only character permitted to look pressed. It reads as effort, and
effort is his whole condition.

### Environment
A **glass-walled meeting room** inside a modern office. Long table, more chairs
than people. The wider floor beyond the glass, soft and out of focus. Neutral,
corporate, slightly cold — a room he did not choose.

### Props
- A **printed document, several pages**, squared in front of him
- A **good pen**, the kind given rather than bought
- Empty chairs either side
- A carafe and two untouched glasses

**Never in frame:** anything readable on the document · a laptop · a screen on
the wall.

### Time of day
**Mid-morning.** Bright, ordinary, the working day fully underway. This is the
only panel not shot in fading light — **his crisis happens in broad daylight
while everything looks fine**, which is the horror of it.

### Light
Cool even daylight through the glass wall, camera right. A warm fill from the
room's own lamps on the shadow side so he is not clinically lit. Bright, but
the corners still fall away.

### Attitude
**Attentive on the surface, entirely absent underneath.** He nods at the right
moments. He is not panicking and not bored — he stopped following some time ago
and has decided not to say so.

The tell is **the reset**: the face does something social, then drops the
instant the room turns away. That drop is the performance.

⚠ **He must not look worried.** Visible anxiety would mean he is engaged.

### Activity, clip by clip
| | |
|---|---|
| **1** | Looks at the page. **The eyes do not track along the lines** |
| **2** | Signs. The pen moves; the eyes stay where they were |
| **3** | **A hand enters frame** with a second page. He signs that too |
| **4** | The hand takes the pages. He gives a brief courteous nod off-frame — **the face resets the instant they turn.** The beat of the panel |
| **5** | Alone. Empty chairs either side |
| **6** | Looks toward the door they left by |
| **7** | Straight ahead, absent. **Freeze on the last frame** |

### On the hand
Forearm and hand only, entering from frame edge. **A shirt cuff, no watch, no
ring.** It must not become a person — no shoulder, no hair, no second face.

### The freeze
Seated alone in the glass room, papers gone, looking at nothing.

## P3 · ANGER
**B2C 03** · 25.7s · **B2B 01** · 9.1s · 5 clips

### Model
White European man, **late thirties to forties**. Physically solid, someone who
takes up room without trying. Short hair, a day of stubble. **He should read as
a decent man**, not a villain — the whole line is that his daughter has learned
to read a face that he does not know he is making.

### Wardrobe
Dark olive or charcoal long-sleeve tee or work shirt, sleeves pushed up.
Forearms visible. Jeans. **He has been home an hour and has not changed.**

### Environment
A **family kitchen, evening.** Lived in, not styled. Dishes done or half done.
A school bag against a chair leg. Fridge with paper on it, nothing readable. A
counter with the day's debris on it.

**The doorway matters more than the room** — it must be deep in frame, clearly
an exit, and backlit from a hallway.

### Props
- A **child's crayon drawing** on the counter near his hands. **This is the
  most important prop in the film** — it is what she came to show him
- A glass of water, untouched
- The school bag, background

**Never in frame:** alcohol of any kind · a raised hand · anything broken ·
a phone.

⚠ **No bottle, no glass of wine.** The instant alcohol appears the film is
about something else, and the script is explicit that these are life
experiences, not diagnoses.

### Time of day
**Evening, after dinner, lamps on.** Dark outside the window. The house is in
its wind-down and he is the only thing in it that is not.

### Light
One warm practical — an under-cabinet strip or a single pendant — from camera
left, low. **The doorway behind him lit cooler from the hall**, so the small
figure appearing there is a silhouette, not a face.

### Attitude
**Contained, and radiating.** Nothing has happened. Nothing is going to happen.
He is standing still with his jaw set, and the whole room has changed
temperature because of it.

The tell is **stillness that is too still** — hands flat and pressing, weight
forward, breathing through the nose.

⚠ **He never raises his voice, never turns, never gestures.** The entire
violence of the scene is that a nine-year-old reads his back and leaves.

### Activity, clip by clip
| | |
|---|---|
| **1** | Both hands flat on the counter. The small shape appears in the doorway behind him and stops |
| **2** | He turns his head a few degrees — **not far enough to see her.** She does not come closer |
| **3** | **The shape steps back out.** The doorway is empty. He has not moved |
| **4** | He looks down at the drawing. One long breath out. **Freeze on the last frame** |
| **5** | *B2B cut* · same man, workplace corridor, mid-stride, saying something off-frame. Direct, unbothered, **certain** |

### The daughter
**Never in focus. Never a face. Never closer than the doorway.** A small
backlit silhouette, roughly nine years old, holding something. She appears,
waits, and goes. If the model resolves her face, retake it.

### The B2B cut
Same man, same day, workplace: cool corridor daylight, shirt instead of the
tee, mid-stride. **Not angry — certain**, which is worse and is exactly what
the line says.

### The freeze
His back and shoulders, the drawing on the counter, the empty doorway behind.

## P4 · ANXIETY
**B2C 01** · 25.7s · 4 clips

### Model
Black Caribbean woman, **early thirties**. Natural hair, worn simply. Composed,
capable, the person others rely on. **She must not look fragile** — the story
is that a competent woman lied about her back because the truth was not sayable,
and lost her job for it.

### Wardrobe
Bone or oatmeal blouse, plain, buttoned. A dark cardigan or blazer over it,
kept on. Simple studs. **Dressed carefully for this meeting**, which is its own
small heartbreak.

### Environment
A **small office on the visitor side of a desk.** Not HR-glossy — a real room
with a filing cabinet, a window with a blind, a door standing open onto an
empty corridor. Institutional and unremarkable.

**The open door matters.** It says this is nearly over and there is nothing
left to discuss.

### Props
- **A single printed letter**, on the desk, **facing her**
- **The empty chair opposite, pushed back** — the manager has gone
- A box of tissues on the desk, unopened. **Someone put it there in advance**
- A closed folder with her name on it, not readable

**Never in frame:** a second person · a computer screen · anything readable on
the letter.

### Time of day
**Late morning.** These meetings happen at eleven. Flat neutral daylight,
nothing atmospheric — **the ordinariness is the cruelty.**

### Light
Cool daylight from a window behind and to one side, blind half drawn. No warm
source at all. **This is the only panel with no warmth in it**, and it should
feel that way next to the other four.

### Attitude
**Upright, contained, finished.** She is not crying and will not. She is being
professional in a room where being professional no longer buys her anything.

The tell is **the stillness of someone waiting to be allowed to leave.**

⚠ **No tears, no trembling, no hand to the mouth.** She has already absorbed
it. What we are watching is the minute afterwards.

### Activity, clip by clip
| | |
|---|---|
| **1** | Upright, hands in her lap. **She is not looking at the letter.** One breath, slightly too shallow |
| **2** | Hands tighten in her lap and release |
| **3** | She looks at the letter for the first time. **She does not pick it up** |
| **4** | Still looking at it. **Freeze on the last frame** |

### The freeze
Seated alone, the letter facing her, the empty chair opposite, the open door
behind.

## P5 · SHUTDOWN
**B2C 04** · 25.7s · 4 clips

### Model
East Asian or mixed-heritage woman, **early thirties**. Hair loose and
unstyled. **She should look well** — that is the point. Nothing about her says
crisis, which is why nobody has asked.

### Wardrobe
An oversized bone or stone knit, sleeves past the wrist. Soft dark trousers or
leggings. Socks, no shoes. **Clothes she has been in all day and possibly
yesterday.**

### Environment
A **living room at night, one lamp on across the room.** Comfortable, decent,
slightly under-used — cushions undisturbed, a blanket folded rather than
thrown. **The room is tidy because nothing has happened in it.**

### Props
- **A phone face up on the low table**, screen lit but **unreadable** — no
  name, no interface, no text
- A mug on the table, cold, not moved
- A book face down, unopened for days
- A television, off, dark

**Never in frame:** a readable screen · a second person · anything suggesting a
visitor.

### Time of day
**Mid-evening, dark outside.** The hour when a call from family is normal.

### Light
A single warm lamp across the room, camera right, so she is lit from the far
side and most of her sits in shadow. **The phone's own glow is the second
source** and the only thing in the frame that changes.

### Attitude
**Flat, not sad.** No grief, no self-pity. Present in the room and absent from
it. Eyes open, focused at middle distance on nothing in particular.

The tell is **the absence of reaction**. Something happens — the phone — and
nothing in her answers it.

⚠ **Do not play sadness.** A sad performance makes it a mood. The line is *I
feel nothing most days*, and nothing is harder to act and more accurate.

### Activity, clip by clip
| | |
|---|---|
| **1** | She sits. **The phone lights.** She does not look at it |
| **2** | It keeps going. Her eyes move to it once, and away |
| **3** | **The screen goes dark.** The room is quieter than before it started |
| **4** | Eyes open, focused on nothing. **Freeze on the last frame** |

### The freeze
On the sofa, the dark phone on the table, the room in shadow around her.

## PART THREE · B2B NARRATOR SETUPS

No lead performer in any of these. They are rooms, objects and aftermaths.

### 00 · Before anyone arrives · 2 stills
**Environment** A hotel back-of-house corridor, a ward station, or a kitchen
pass. **Sector-specific, not generic open-plan** — the first market is
hospitality, healthcare and aviation.
**Props** Everything clean, stacked, in its place. Trolleys parked. Equipment
charging.
**Time** **First light, 05:30–06:30.** Lights not yet on, daylight arriving.
**Light** Low warm daylight from one end of the corridor. Cool shadow elsewhere.
⚠ **Early versus abandoned is entirely in the light.** If the overheads are on
and the daylight is gone, the beat inverts and the film opens on decline.

### 01b · The chain · 1 clip + 3 stills
**a · the silence.** Two workstations side by side, both occupied, **the two
people angled fractionally away from each other.** Middle distance, faces not
legible. Mid-morning, flat light. *(clip)*
**b · the empty desk.** Same pair of desks, one cleared — no monitor, no
personal object, chair squared in. The other still in use. *(still)*
**c · one person covering.** A service counter or nurses' station with one
person working and a second position dark behind them. *(still)*
**d · the aftermath.** **A phone face down on a counter beside a cold cup**,
end of shift. The review is never shown. *(still)*

### 02b · The performance conversation · 1 clip + 1 still
**Environment** A small side room, two chairs, a low table. Institutional.
**Wardrobe** Both parties workplace-neutral, mid-range.
**Time** Mid-afternoon, cool daylight.
**Attitude** Both composed. **Neither comfortable.** No raised voices, no
pointing.
**The still** The same room after: two chairs, one pushed back, the door open.

### 03b · The notice in the drafts folder · 2 stills
**a** A desk at the end of a day. Work finished and stacked. Chair empty. One
lamp still on. Everything else dark.
**b** A corridor with a light on in one room and the rest unlit. **Somebody is
still here, and it is late.**

### 04b · The strain trio · 1 cast frame + 2 clips
**a · the handover** — **254790848**, already cast. Black woman, white coat,
head back against the chair.
**b · the feedback.** Two at a desk, one mid-sentence, the other **arms in and
turned a few degrees away.** The moment listening stopped is a posture, not an
expression.
**c · the decision.** One person alone at a desk late, signing or deciding,
depleted. **The decision is not visible. The state that produced it is.**

### 08 · The four steps · 2 clips
**01 Recognise** — a person catching themselves. Mid-action, stopped. One hand
just come to rest. Eyes down and inward, not distressed. *(generate)*
**02 Regulate** — **V1**, in hand. Hand to chest, eyes closed.
**03 Release** — the shoulders coming down. A held breath going out. Same
person, same room as Recognise, so the four read as one sequence. *(generate)*
**04 Rise** — **V5**, in hand. Woman by a window, composed, turning into the
day.

⚠ **Recognise and Release must match V1 and V5 in grade and register** or the
four steps will not read as one capability. Generate them warm, domestic,
single-source — the same world those two clips live in.

## PART FOUR · DASHBOARD FILM

Plainest of the three. **Instruction, not persuasion.** No atmosphere, no
strain, nobody in difficulty. A member is already inside.

| slot | direction |
|---|---|
| 0:00–0:16 · ordinary places | A kitchen counter · a tram window · a stairwell. **Empty of people.** Held long. Method-page arena set — in hand |
| 0:16–0:26 · the cue | Hands, a phone face down. **Same phone still as B2B 01b** — one asset, two films |
| 0:26–0:42 · the same room twice | **The only shot that must show change.** One room, dim and lamplit, then the same frame in daylight. Same angle, same furniture, **nothing moved.** The change is entirely light |
| 0:42–1:00 · the close | A doorway, morning, light coming in. Nobody in it |

**Wardrobe and casting: not applicable.** Nobody appears in this film except
hands.

⚠ The five product details — meditation, cue, insights, resources, journal —
are **cropped captures, never full-screen**, and are blocked on the player.

## PART FIVE · THE ONE-PAGE CHECK

Before accepting any generated clip:

1. **Is anybody smiling who should not be?**
2. **Is there overhead fluorescent?**
3. **Is any text, logo or screen readable?**
4. **Does the wardrobe carry gold, white, or a fine pattern?**
5. **Is the face flat-lit or looking into the lens?**
6. **Does the subject survive a 384-wide vertical crop?**
7. **Does anything cross in front of the lead?**
8. **Does the person look cast rather than found?**

Any yes on 1–7 is a retake. A yes on 8 is a recast, and that is a still, not a
clip — go back and fix it for 2 credits.
