## 0 · The assumption this is built on

**The speaking characters do not talk on camera. The voice is interior
monologue over someone not talking.**

Nothing in either script requires lip movement, it keeps the no-face rule
intact, it keeps the existing corpus usable, and it makes casting a voice
decision rather than a shoot. **Overturn it in one line if it is wrong** — it
changes the five B2C panels and the four B2B employee beats, and nothing else.

### The constraint that decides the format

Each B2C panel needs **roughly 25 seconds** of usable footage of one person,
full frame, then a held freeze. Five of them is **125 seconds of a single
continuous human presence**.

A still with a motion pass does not hold 25 seconds. Neither does a single
generated clip. So each panel is one of three things, and they cost very
differently:

1. **Stock video** — the cheapest. Documentary-register clips of one person
   under strain, long enough to cut 25 seconds from. Adobe's video library is
   thinner than its stills and this needs testing before it is assumed.
2. **A still series** — three or four frames of the *same* person, cross-faded,
   ending on the freeze. Works only where a stock set covers one subject across
   several frames. Continuity is the risk.
3. **A shoot** — five performers, controlled, everything else solved. The only
   option that reliably delivers, and the only one with a real budget line.

**B2B does not have this problem.** Its employee beats run 6.1 to 14.3 seconds,
which a still with a slow move covers comfortably. The 25-second panel is a
B2C-only constraint, created by the accumulation staging.

### What the five panels must each do

Every one has to read **full frame** and again as a **384 × 1080 vertical
slice**, because that is where it spends most of the film. That rules out wide
establishing shots and anything where the subject is small or off-centre. The
subject sits centre, vertical, with headroom.

Each also needs **one held frame** — the freeze the panel keeps for the rest of
the film. It should be the moment of most distress, not the most movement.

⚠ The manifest's 31 eligible frames serve **none** of these. They are
landscapes, interiors and abstracts. The character panels are new work.

⚠ **Mobile is unresolved.** Five panels at 384px each is unreadable on a phone,
and the homepage is mobile-first. Flagged in the script and still open.
