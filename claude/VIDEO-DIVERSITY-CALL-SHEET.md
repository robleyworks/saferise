# VIDEO — DIVERSITY CALL SHEET

23 September. Casting options for every people slot, African American and
European, to choose from. All Adobe Stock **free-tier** — `pricing: "free"`,
no payment. Every frame licensed and **reviewed at size**, not by caption.

Reference: `claude/VIDEO-SHOT-BRIEF-BY-BEAT.md` for what each picture must mean.

---

## How to use this

Each slot lists options by casting with a verdict. Reply with the slot and the
asset ID — e.g. *"B2B-03 take 254790848"*. Nothing is committed.

Every asset clears the 1080p floor by a wide margin; the smallest here is
5000x3333, against a 1920x1080 requirement.

## Two things worth noticing

**The strongest people frames in this set are African and African American** —
254790848 and 545625176 are the two best, and it is not close. That is the
opposite of the first pass, which skewed white because Adobe's default ranking
was being accepted rather than directed. Naming the casting in the query changed
the result, not just the diversity of it.

**The European side is genuinely thinner for the strain beats.** B2B-03 has no
European option that passes, and B2B-08's European frame has an exposure
problem. This is not an artefact of effort — Adobe's documentary-register
coverage of people under strain is weak in both directions, and its Black-cast
results skew overwhelmingly toward *happy* and *smiling* captions, which the
strain beats cannot use.

**Still open:** B2B-01 (workplace before anyone arrives) and a stronger B2C-08.
Both are empty-room slots, which this source handles well — they need another
search round, not a different source.
