# Organisations explorer — copy assessment against the HR-buyer strategy

Assessed 22 September, against the brief: every line speaks to an HR decision-maker
in pain-point → solution form, in the language of budget, departmental coverage,
team capacity and return on spend — never abstract, never feature description.

Three failures were found and corrected. All three were **headers**.

---

## The test applied

A line passes when it does all three:

1. **Names a cost the HR lead already carries** — utilisation, vendor sprawl, team
   capacity, capability they have paid for and are not getting.
2. **Answers it with what SafeRise supplies** — not with an outcome we cannot evidence.
3. **Stays concrete.** A named person in a named situation beats a category noun.

## The three header rewrites

**§1** — was *Eight tracks, every employee, from day one*
→ **The expertise is already paid for. These eight keep it reachable.**
The pain is sunk cost not returning. The answer is the eight. It also promotes
the strongest sentence in the section — *the training is not missing, access to
it is* — from buried in paragraph four to the thing on the wall.

**§2** — was *Then two that match the job they actually do*
→ **Support that fits everyone fits no one, and goes unused**
The pain is spend on a benefit nobody opens — which every HR lead has witnessed.
The solution follows in the body.

**§3** — was *Thirty protocols, matched to who they are and where they work*
→ **Answer every department without buying a vendor for each one**
The pain is procurement and budget arithmetic. The lede now leads with
*one library, one invoice, nothing for your team to administer* rather than
with a protocol count.

---

## Length

All three section ledes ran five to six sentences. Each is now **two sentences
visible**, with the rest behind a `Read more` control that toggles `aria-expanded`
and swaps its own label. The visible opener always carries the concrete image;
the reveal carries the reasoning. Nothing was deleted.

---

## What is deliberately still absent

No retention, absence, turnover, productivity or engagement figure appears
anywhere, and no multiplier. Every claim is about what is **supplied** — who can
reach it, how many invoices, what the team has to run — or about a cost the buyer
already recognises. The moment a number is wanted on this page it needs a source
we are willing to cite.

"Single pane of glass" was also rejected: correct concept, wrong register beside
sleep, food and grief. It appears as *one place instead of several* and *one
library, one invoice*.
