24 September 2026. Written to `claude/COPY-CHANGE-STANDARD.md`.

**Supersedes** the cost-of-inaction sketch in `SafeRise_Market_Intel_Ledger.md`
§17.3, which opened *"Think about what this is costing. Not in money."* That

## 0 · The register, and the standard it is written to

These are **life experiences, not diagnoses.** Every line describes something a
person did, or something that happened to them, and none of it names a
condition, a disorder or a mechanism. Nothing here claims SafeRise would have
prevented any of it. The turn at the end says what changes is the state, and
stops there.

That distinction is what lets this material carry numbers — forty thousand a
year, six weeks, four years, two glasses — without becoming a clinical claim.
A number attached to a lived consequence is reporting. A number attached to a
condition is a claim. Keep every line on the first side of that.

## 1 · The section — track pages and `method.html`

**Headline** · What it is costing.

**Standfirst** · Not in theory. In the week you have just had.

Then the six domains. These map one-to-one onto the "What This Protocol Heals"
meter that already exists inside the platform, so the section reads as the
public face of an asset the product already owns.

### BODY

> You have been running at this level so long that you stopped noticing it was
> a level. The sleep went first. Then the weight. Then the number your doctor
> mentioned twice before you actually heard it.

### REST

> The day does not close. You are in bed at eleven and awake at one, running a
> conversation from March that you cannot change and cannot stop, and the alarm
> is going off in four hours whether you sleep or not.

### MIND

> Every task opens six more and none of them close, so you work late, badly, on
> the wrong things. Your team stopped bringing you decisions because you could
> not make them, and started making them without you.

### RELATIONSHIPS

> Four years ago there was an argument neither of you can now remember the
> subject of. Everything since has been about that argument. Your children have
> learned to leave the room at a particular volume.

### LIFE & WORK

> You stopped putting yourself forward, because putting yourself forward means
> being looked at. Three people you trained have been promoted past you, and
> the gap between what you earn and what you are worth is now about forty
> thousand a year.

### IDENTITY

> You have started describing your own life in the past tense.

### The close, under all six

> None of that is a character problem. Every one of those decisions was made in
> a state that could not carry it — and the state is the one thing here that
> can change.

## 3 · The organisational version — `organisations.html` only if wanted

Same device, costs instead of domains. Written from the hotel scenario.

> Two people on the same team stopped speaking properly in March. Nobody
> escalated it, because there was nothing to escalate. No incident. No policy
> broken. By May the rota was being built around them. By July one had resigned
> and the other was on a second period of sick leave, and replacing the first
> took eleven weeks and a recruitment fee. In August a guest got the end of that
> chain and left the review that is still the first thing anyone reads about
> you. Nobody in the building could tell you where it started.

Headed **What one unregulated hour costs, eleven months later.**

⚠ This is the strongest B2B copy in the corpus and it is also the most
quantified. It should ship on the page only after the same close as §1 — that
the state is what changes, not the outcome.

## 4 · What this retires

**`claude/COPY-BANK-COMMERCIAL.md` §2.3** and the `plans.html` entry in
**`claude/COPY-DEPLOYMENT-MAP.md` §2.4** both carry:

> One session will not rearrange your life. It will change the state you are in,
> which changes what you can do next. That is a smaller promise and it is one we
> can keep.

**Retired 24 September.** It leads with a negation of a claim nobody made, and
it denies the wrong axis — the film's argument is about state, not
life-rearrangement, so denying life-rearrangement concedes ground for nothing.

**Replacement, for `plans.html` and anywhere else that construction was
scheduled:**

> One session is where it begins. It changes the state you are in, which changes
> what you can do next.

The job of the original — lowering the promise before purchase — is still done.
*Where it begins* promises a beginning and nothing more. It simply does it
forward instead of backward.

The line was found in only one place on disk, `claude/VIDEO-SCRIPTS-THREE-SURFACES-V2.md`,
and has been corrected there. It had not yet shipped to `plans.html`.
