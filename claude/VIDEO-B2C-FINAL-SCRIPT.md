## Staging

Story one opens full frame with its label bottom-left. On its last word it
slides left and compresses to the first fifth of the screen, where it **freezes**
on a held moment of distress — the title stays under it. Story two enters and
fills the remaining four fifths. On its last word it settles into the second
fifth and freezes. And so on, until five frozen figures sit side by side with
every label visible. The turn lands on all five.

Nothing keeps running after its story ends. Each panel needs roughly 25 seconds
of usable footage and one held frame.

## Notes on the final pass

**The register split is deliberate.** Characters speak with contractions
because people do. Beats 09 to 14 keep the house register. Beats 00 and 06–08
sit between the two because Andre narrated them — that is intentional, not a
slip.

**Beat 01 ends in a firing.** The lie about the back is the cause. It is the
hardest consequence in the film.

**Beats 07 and 08 address the five, not the viewer.** An earlier version turned
to the second person — *you have had good judgement before*. It now stays on
the people on screen, and the film does not point at the viewer until 10b.

**The carousel introduction is four words** — *Named for the moment, not the
method* — per `claude/COPY-DEPLOYMENT-MAP.md`, which is emphatic that the
protocol names do the recognition themselves. No count is stated anywhere in
the film.

**The close is the offer, not the beta.** *The first track is free with an
account — the whole track, not a trial.* Beta urgency belongs on the page around
the film, not in the voiceover, so it can change without a re-record.
