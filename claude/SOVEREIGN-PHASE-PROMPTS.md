# Sovereign phase prompts — the library

1 October 2026. The on-screen prompts for a Sovereign session, one set per
protocol. Method, placement and rulings: `SOVEREIGN-SESSION-SCREEN-SPEC.md`.

**Source: `SafeRise_AllTracks_RecordingScripts.docx`.** Not
`SafeRise_SpokenTextOnly.md`, which strips `RED/ACTION` cues by design and so
loses the entire somatic layer.

**T0-00 Clearing has no Sovereign prompts.** Founder-ruled 1 October: the
Clearing remains a guided practice. Thirty protocols carry prompts — Tracks 01,
02 and 03.

---

## The four moves, and what varies

Every set carries the same architecture, in the founder's voice:

**Recognise** — body, then the feeling on it, then the thoughts around it, then
one word for the root. **Reversed in the numb states** (T1-07, T1-10, T2-08,
T2-10, T3-08, T3-09), where the scripts say plainly *we're not going hunting —
not finding it belongs to the state, not to any failure of yours*. Asking those
members to scan is asking for the one thing the state has taken.

**Regulate** — four in, six out, longer out than in; a hand at the centre of the
chest; and **thank your heart**. In the numb states, **contact comes before
counting** and the count is optional — *longer out than in is the only part that
matters*. There the gratitude is offered, never asked for, or it becomes one
more thing to fail at.

**Release** — two things are running. The state stays and is not argued with.
The expensive one is put down. Then the question swaps: never *what's wrong with
me*, always **what was this for?** — and the member is given explicit permission
to set down what doesn't belong to this moment.

**Rise** — step outside and speak to yourself as *you*; **the Chosen Self**; what
you're building, said out loud; one good thing already here. **The Chosen Self
scales to the state** — in Shutdown Recovery the scripts refuse the
back-to-yourself scene outright, so there the Chosen Self is someone who stands
up and fills a glass, not someone who meets a trigger with sovereignty.

Every somatic cue is **offered, never instructed**, as the scripts' own cues say.

# Track 01 · Personal Transformation

## T1-01 · Anxiety Reset

**Recognise** — Start right where you are, in your body. Notice your chest, your
throat, your stomach, behind the eyes — wherever the signal is loudest. There's
no correct answer here, and nothing is being marked. Name what you find,
quietly, without needing to change it.
Now look one layer deeper: the feeling sitting on those sensations, and the
thoughts spinning around them. Beneath the noise is what's actually asking for
you. Give it one simple word — not the story. The state.

**Regulate** — Nothing to solve right now. This is just breath.
Inhale softly through your nose for four. Let it out, slow and complete, for six
— always a longer exhale than inhale. Don't reach for a big one; a big breath
asks for effort, and effort is pointing the wrong way. Small is fine. Long is
what matters.
Rest a hand over the centre of your chest, if you'd like one there. Feel the
steady rhythm telling your body it can soften.
Stay here a while. This is the part that does the most, and the part people cut
short. Take a moment to thank your heart for keeping you grounded.

**Release** — There's a second thing running underneath the state. *Not now. Not
again. What is wrong with me.*
That one costs more than the state itself, and it's the only one of the two you
can put down. The state arrived — you're not being asked to want it, or approve
of it. Only to stop spending yourself on the fact that it came.
That brace was an instinct trying to keep you safe. Gently name what it's been
standing ready for.
Now swap the question. Not *what's wrong with me* — **what was this for?** Your
body runs fast because fast is useful; it goes ahead of thought. Leave the
question open, and give yourself full permission to put down what doesn't belong
to this moment.

**Rise** — Step outside it. Picture yourself from across the room — not close
up, across. Someone over there, breathing four and six. Speak to that person as
*you*. *You're having a fast day. You've had these before.*
Now envision The Chosen Self — the version of you who meets this next time. See
how they stand, how they breathe, how they answer with calm sovereignty instead
of defence.
Then turn to what you're building — not the distant goal, the work right in
front of you today. Make it a scene, with detail; a general hope does nothing.
Where are your feet, what's in your hands, what's your very next move? Speak it
out loud.
And one good thing that's already here with you now. Small beats large. The
chair. The light. That you stopped and did this.

## T1-02 · Anger Alchemy

**Recognise** — Your body registered it before you'd finished deciding what you
thought. So start there. Notice your jaw, your hands, the back of your neck, the
heat across your face — wherever it's holding. Name it quietly, without needing
to change a thing.
Now one layer deeper: the feeling on those sensations, and the thoughts circling
them. Give the root one simple word.
And notice what that word left out. It left out them — their name, their face,
what they said, and the whole argument you have assembled and ready. Not because
the argument is wrong. It will be just as right afterwards, intact.

**Regulate** — Nothing to solve right now. This is just breath.
Four counts in through the nose. Six out, slow and complete — longer out than in.
Your breath wants to go short and sharp. Let it want that. Go the other way
anyway.
Your hands are closed. Let them open — fingers wide, then loose.
Attention into the middle of your chest. Not the jaw; the jaw will hold on to
it. It'll come up before it goes down, and that's normal here.
Thank your heart for staying with you while it rises.

**Release** — Now the conversation in your head. Notice something about it: you
win it. Every single time. And every run puts your body back where it started.
You've been re-lighting yourself all afternoon.
The anger stays. It's yours, it's earned, nobody's taking it. Put down the rerun.
Only the rerun.
Gently name what your system has been standing ready for.
And swap the question. Not *what's wrong with me* — **what was this for?** Anger
goes toward things. Fear backs away; this walks at it. It turns up where
something is being crossed. You have full permission to set down the rehearsal
and keep every bit of the reason.

**Rise** — Step back from it. Somebody across the room, jaw set, running a
conversation for the sixth time. Speak to that person as *you*. *You are angry.
You have a reason.* Both halves stay. Neither takes anything off the other.
Now The Chosen Self — the version of you who meets this next time. How they
stand. How they breathe. How they say the true thing once, steadily, instead of
winning it five times in private.
Then something later today. Not the confrontation where you win; you've had that
one, and it cost you the afternoon. The one where you're steady and it still
matters just as much. Where are your feet, what do your hands do while you say
it, what are the actual words? Speak it out loud.
And one good thing already true, and small, and nothing to do with any of this.

## T1-03 · Overwhelm Threshold

**Recognise** — Start in your body, before any of the words about it. Notice
where it's holding — chest, shoulders, the pit of your stomach. Name it quietly.
Now one layer deeper, and then one sorting question that takes a second. **Too
much of what?** Too much to do — the list, the deadlines, the people waiting.
Too much coming in — noise, light, people, screens. Or too much at once inside
you — angry and frightened and sad, all live, with no way to pick one.
And if the honest answer is *I don't know, I just can't tell what's wrong with
me today* — that's a real answer, and it's the third one. Give it one simple
word.

**Regulate** — Nothing to solve right now. This is just breath.
Four counts in. Six out, slow and complete — longer out than in. Overwhelm
breathes high and fast, at the top of the chest. We're going the other way.
Rest a hand over the centre of your chest if you'd like one there. Attention
into the chest, not the list.
Press your feet into the floor. Feel the floor push back.
There's nothing you could do about any of it in the next minute anyway. Thank
your heart for carrying the load this far.

**Release** — Two things are running, and only one of them is the load.
Too much to do: you're going round the list, touching everything and starting
nothing. Too much coming in: you're pushing through, holding a normal face on,
spending everything on looking fine. Too much at once inside: you're sorting,
trying to decide which feeling comes first, as though there were an order.
Let the amount be exactly what it is. Put down the circling. Gently name what
your system has been bracing against.
And swap the question. Not *what's wrong with me* — **what was this for?** You
have full permission to set the sorting down for the next few minutes.

**Rise** — Step outside it. Someone a little way off with a great deal on,
breathing four and six. Speak to them as *you*. *You have a lot on. It is a lot.
And you are steadier now than when you sat down.*
Now The Chosen Self — the version of you who meets a day like this next time.
How they stand. How they breathe. How they do one thing instead of all of it.
Then forward, and small. Not the version where it's all done — that scene isn't
available and building it would be a lie. One thing. **The next thing.** Not the
most important one. Not the one you're most behind on. Where are your feet,
what's in front of you, what's the first movement you make? Speak it out loud.
And one good thing already handled.

## T1-04 · Abandonment Wound

**Recognise** — Start in your body. It usually sits low — a drop through the
stomach, a pull across the chest, the throat closing. Sometimes a hum through
all of you that won't hold still. Name it quietly, without needing to change it.
Now one layer deeper: the feeling on it, the thoughts circling. One simple word
for the root.
The state is here in the room, and you're the one who noticed it was here —
you're already standing slightly outside it. There's a question with no answer
yet, and your body has gone ahead and answered it. That's what it does with
silence: it fills it fast, so it isn't standing in an unknown.

**Regulate** — Nothing to solve right now. This is just breath.
Four counts in. Six out, slow and complete — longer out than in. Attention into
the middle of your chest.
Rest a hand at your sternum if you want one there. Warmth at that spot does
something on its own that nothing you think will match.
You'll feel the pull toward the phone. Toward checking. Toward saying the thing
that would settle it right now. Let it pull, and stay.
Thank your heart for reaching, even now.

**Release** — There's the state, and there's the searching. Every pass finds
something, and everything it finds could mean two things, so you go round again.
That isn't investigation. **That's a circuit.**
Let the state stay — it's here and nobody's arguing with it. Put down the
searching. Just that.
Gently name what your system has been standing ready for.
And swap the question. Not *what's wrong with me that I'm like this about people*
— you've asked that a hundred times and it has never once given you anything.
**What was this for?** Something in you moves very fast when connection is in
doubt. Full permission to stop looking for the next few minutes.

**Rise** — Step outside it. Someone across the room, phone face down, breathing
four and six. Speak to them as *you*. *You don't know yet. And you're steady
enough to not know for a while.*
That's the whole sentence. Not that it's fine — that you can stand in the
not-knowing without the state deciding it on your behalf.
Now The Chosen Self — the version of you who meets this silence next time. How
they stand. How they breathe. How they wait without the waiting costing them the
day.
Then forward. Not the scene where they reply; that one isn't yours to build, and
rehearsing it is what we just put down. You, somewhere specific, later today,
with this still unresolved. Where are your feet, what's in your hands, what's
your very next move? Speak it out loud.
And one good thing that's already here with you now.

## T1-05 · Shame Dissolution

**Recognise** — Start in your body, before any of the words. Contained, not
diminished. Notice where it sits, and name it quietly.
Now one layer deeper: the feeling on it, the thoughts around it. One simple word.
Notice which one it is — *I did something bad*, or something closer to *I am the
thing that's wrong*. For now, only this: that's a state. Whatever else it is, it
is first a state, running in a body, with a name.

**Regulate** — Nothing to solve right now. This is just breath.
Four counts in. Six out, slow and complete — longer out than in. Attention into
the centre of your chest.
Rest a hand there if it helps. This state pulls the body inward, and warmth at
the chest is one of the few things it accepts.
Let the shoulders come back a little. Not straightening up. Just less curled.
Thank your heart for staying open while the rest of you closed.

**Release** — Two things are running. The state arrived, it's unpleasant, and it
isn't being argued with.
Set down the case. **Only the case** — not whatever real thing may be underneath
it. That stays, and you can look at it later, from somewhere steadier.
Gently name what your system has been bracing against.
And the question. You already know the other one: *what is wrong with me* — you
have asked it many times and it has never once returned anything you could use.
It only ever returns more of itself. **What was this for?** Something in you
learned to go small and quiet and hidden. Full permission to put the case down
for now.

**Rise** — Step outside it. See yourself from across the room. Someone sitting
there, shoulders forward, breathing four and six. Speak to them as *you*, not
*I*. *You are having a bad hour with yourself. That's what this is.*
Now The Chosen Self — the version of you who meets this next time. How they
stand. How they breathe. How they stay in view instead of going quiet.
Then something concrete. Later today, somewhere specific, doing something
ordinary. Not a scene where you've resolved anything about yourself. Where are
your feet. What's in your hands. **Who can see you.** Speak it out loud.
Ordinary is the point. This state says you should be hidden; the scene where
you're out, doing a normal thing, in view, is the answer to it.
And one good thing that's already here with you now.

## T1-06 · Grief Integration

**Recognise** — Start in your body. Where is it today? Chest, probably — a
weight, or a hollow. Some days both together, which makes no sense and is still
what happens. Throat. Behind the eyes. Or a tiredness with nothing to do with
sleep. Name it quietly.
Now one layer deeper, and one simple word. That's the accurate one.
This doesn't hold still — gone for a week, then back in a supermarket aisle over
nothing. It's here today. There's no backwards for it to be.

**Regulate** — Nothing to solve right now. This is just breath.
Four counts in. Six out. If it catches, let it catch — don't smooth it out.
Rest a hand on your chest if you'd like one there.
Attention into the middle of your chest. **Including the part that hurts.**
We're not going around it.
If crying arrives, it's arriving into the right room.
And thank your heart — not for being fine. For still being able to feel this.

**Release** — Two things here, and only one of them is the grief.
The first is the loss. That stays. It isn't going to be reframed or argued down.
The second is quieter and has been running a long time: bracing before it comes,
trying to have it at convenient hours, checking whether this is too much of it,
or too long, or the wrong shape. And underneath, usually — *this shouldn't have
happened*, said again and again to something that already did.
Which of those two is costing you more today? Gently name it, and put that one
down. Full permission — the loss keeps every inch of its weight.

**Rise** — Step back a moment. Someone sitting there, carrying something,
breathing four and six. Speak to them as *you*. *You're carrying something
heavy. Of course you are.* Nothing to fix in that. No second half where it gets
better.
Now The Chosen Self — not a version of you who's finished grieving. The version
who carries this and still goes out into the day. How they stand. How they
breathe.
Then later today. Not the day this is finished; that day isn't available and I
won't pretend it is. An ordinary hour, carried out while carrying this. Where
are your feet. What's in your hands. What's around you. **Both things at once.
Not instead of it. Alongside.** Speak it out loud.
And one good thing that's already here with you now.

## T1-07 · Shutdown Recovery

**Recognise** — One word, and we're not going hunting for it.
There's no need to find where it sits — in this state that's often hard, and not
finding it belongs to the state rather than to any failure of yours.
Numb isn't nothing, though. It's somewhere your body has moved to, and it moved
there for a reason. Something is happening, just at a level you don't have a
window onto. One simple word is the whole of this step.

**Regulate** — Contact, before any counting.
Rest a hand on your chest. Or hold your own arm — whichever is nearer. Let it
stay. Warmth and weight is what gets through when finer things don't.
Now the breath, and it doesn't have to be four and six today. **Longer out than
in. That's the only part that matters.** The count is there if you want it. If
counting is too much, leave it out.
Nothing to achieve here. And if any thanks is available for the heart that's
still running underneath all this — it's offered, not asked for.

**Release** — There's the shutdown, and there's something quieter running
underneath it. You know the one. *Pathetic. Lazy. Everyone else manages.*
Running all day, low, at a volume you've stopped hearing. It isn't gentler for
being quiet — it's the same thing the loud states do, in a lower voice.
The state can stay. It arrived, it isn't being argued with, and it isn't being
ranked against how you were last month. Put down the other one.
And swap the question. Not *what's wrong with me that I can't function* —
**what was this for?** Full permission to set the reproach down.

**Rise** — Step back a little. Gently. Someone sitting or lying there, hand on
their chest. Speak to them as *you*. *You're shut down today. And you did this
anyway.* Both halves.
Now The Chosen Self — and today that isn't someone meeting a trigger with
sovereignty. **Today it's someone who stands up and fills a glass of water.**
That's the version. How they move. How they breathe.
Then one thing. Standing up. A glass of water. Opening a window. Speak it out
loud.
**Small isn't a reduced version of this step. Today it's the whole of it.**
And before you come back — you did this. On a day like this one.

## T1-08 · Jealousy Release

**Recognise** — Start in your body. Stomach, usually — that turning over.
Sometimes heat up through the chest and into the face; sometimes a coldness
instead, which catches people out. Name it quietly.
Now one layer deeper: the feeling on it, and the thoughts circling it. One
simple word for the root.

**Regulate** — Nothing to solve right now. This is just breath.
Four in. Six out, slow and complete — longer out than in. Attention to the
middle of your chest.
A hand at your sternum if you want one, or the phone face down — whichever
helps. This one pulls hard toward looking. A phone, a profile, a room.
Thank your heart for wanting something. That's what this is made of.

**Release** — Now the comparing. It was always going to answer every round —
you're running it, and you know exactly which way it goes.
The state can stay. Put down the measuring.
Gently name what your system has been standing ready for.
And swap the question. Not *what's wrong with me that I'm this person*. You've
asked. It gave you nothing. **What was this for?** Jealousy points at something.
Often not at the person — past them, at something you want and haven't said out
loud. Sometimes haven't said to yourself. Full permission to stop measuring and
start listening to what it's pointing at.

**Rise** — Step back. Someone over there with their phone face down, breathing
four and six. Speak to them as *you*. *You want something. That's all this is so
far.*
Notice where your attention has been sitting. On them, mostly — which has never
once produced anything.
Now The Chosen Self — the version of you who meets this next time. How they
stand. How they breathe. How they go after the thing instead of watching someone
else have it.
Then something that's actually yours. Where are your feet. What's in your hands.
What are you making, or asking for, or starting — and one small thing you'll
actually do about it, today, feeling exactly like this, not on a better day.
Speak it out loud.
And one good thing already here. Yours. Not compared to anyone.

## T1-09 · Insecurity Anchor

**Recognise** — Start in your body, before any of the words. Upright. Not
falling. Notice where it sits and name it quietly.
Now one layer deeper, and one simple word.
*Not enough. Not really. Found out eventually.* We're not going to argue with it
— arguing gives it something to answer.
The opinion is in the room. You're the one who noticed it was in the room.
That's a different position, and you're already standing in it.

**Regulate** — Nothing to solve right now. This is just breath.
Four counts in. Six out, slow and complete — longer out than in. Attention into
the centre of your chest, a hand there if you'd like one.
Press your feet into the floor. Feel the floor push back. **This state is
unsteady and the floor is not.**
Nothing to be good at here. Nobody's watching this bit. Thank your heart for
steadying you where the thinking can't.

**Release** — Two things are running. The state arrived, it's uncomfortable, it
isn't being argued with.
Set down the auditing. **Only the auditing.**
Gently name what your system has been bracing against.
And the question: not *what's wrong with me* — that's the question the auditing
is already asking, on repeat, and it has never once returned an answer you could
use. **What was this for?** Watching yourself closely in a room is a skill. It
was learned somewhere, because at some point it mattered a great deal to know
exactly how you were landing. It's still running. Full permission to let it rest
for a few minutes.

**Rise** — Step outside it. See yourself from across the room. Someone sitting
there, feet on the floor, breathing four and six. Speak to them as *you*, not
*I*. *You are unsteady today. That's a state, and it's one you've been in
before.*
Notice where your attention is sitting. A room you were in last week. One you
haven't walked into yet. Neither has anything in it you can use. **Everything
you actually have is here.**
Now The Chosen Self — the version of you who walks in unsteady and does the work
anyway. How they stand. How they breathe. How they speak without checking the
room first.
Then today. Where are your feet, what's in your hands, what's your very next
move? Speak it out loud.
And one good thing that's already here with you now.

## T1-10 · Powerlessness & Despair

**Recognise** — One word. We're not going looking for it.
You don't have to find where it sits; in this state that's often hard, and not
finding it is part of what this is rather than a failure to look properly.
Two things are stacked here and they get treated as one. There's what can't be
changed. And there's the state you're in about it. Different things. **Only one
of them is within reach.**

**Regulate** — Contact first. The counting can wait.
Rest a hand on your chest, or hold your own arm — whichever is easier to
arrange. Let it stay there. Warmth and pressure is what this state will take
when it won't take much else.
Now breathe, and **longer out than in**. Count it if you can. Don't if you can't.
Nothing here to get right. And if thanks for your heart is available, it's
offered — not required.

**Release** — The situation is real. Whatever part of it genuinely can't be moved
— that's real, and nobody's going to tell you otherwise, or that you haven't
tried, or that there's an angle you've missed.
And then there's the other thing. Going back over how it should have gone. The
version where it was different, run again. **A quarrel with something that
already happened.**
That's the expensive one, and it's the only one of the two you can put down.
Which isn't the same as calling it acceptable. Full permission to set down the
quarrel and keep every bit of the objection.

**Rise** — Step back a little, if you can. Someone sitting there with a hand on
their chest, in the middle of something hard. Speak to them as *you*. *This is a
lot. It isn't your fault. And you're still here.* No correction in that. Nothing
being fixed.
Now — remember your fingers moving, at the start. Most of this is outside your
reach right now. **That was inside it.**
The Chosen Self today is someone who decides one small thing and does it. How
they move. How they breathe.
Then one thing later that's yours to decide. Small enough that you're certain of
it. Standing up. A glass of water. A window. Not a step toward fixing any of it.
Speak it out loud.
And one good thing that's already here with you now.

# Track 02 · Relationship Healing

## T2-01 · Speak

**Recognise** — Start in your body, before any of the words. Nothing behind it
yet. Notice where it's holding and name it quietly.
Now one layer deeper: the feeling on those sensations, the thoughts circling
them. One simple word for the root.
Not the conversation. **The state you'd be walking in with.**

**Regulate** — Nothing to solve right now. This is just breath.
Four counts in. Six out, slow and complete — longer out than in. Attention into
the centre of your chest, a hand there if it helps.
The throat is worth some attention here. This state closes it, and a closed
throat changes your voice before you've said anything. Let the jaw come apart
slightly. Let the tongue drop off the roof of the mouth.
Thank your heart for wanting to be understood.

**Release** — Two things are running. Some of it is preparation — genuinely. But
past the second version it stops being preparation and becomes an argument
rehearsed with someone who isn't in the room. And notice which way it goes: **in
every draft, you have the better lines.**
Put down the rehearsing. Gently name what your system has been bracing against.
And swap the question. Not *what's wrong with me that I can't just have a
conversation* — that returns nothing, and plenty of people find this hard for
reasons that make sense. **What was this for?** Full permission to stop drafting
and keep everything you actually mean.

**Rise** — Step outside it. See yourself from across the room. Someone sitting
there, about to say something difficult, breathing four and six. Speak to them
as *you*. *You have something to say. You are allowed to say it badly.*
Now The Chosen Self — the version of you who opens their mouth without the
rehearsal. How they stand. How they breathe. How they say the first true thing
and let it be imperfect.
Then something concrete. Not the whole conversation — you don't control most of
it, and rehearsing their half is what we just put down. **Only your first
sentence.** Where you'll be. What your hands are doing. The actual words you'll
open with. Speak it out loud.
And one good thing that's already here with you now.

## T2-02 · Repair

**Recognise** — Start in your body, before any of the words. The break has an
edge on both sides. Notice where it's sitting and name it quietly.
Now one layer deeper, and one simple word for the root.
They have an account too, and theirs will not match yours, and that on its own
is not evidence of anything. We're not touching either account. **Only the
state.**

**Regulate** — Nothing to solve right now. This is just breath.
Four counts in. Six out, slow and complete — longer out than in.
Attention into the centre of your chest — **including the part that aches.**
We're not going around it. A hand there if it helps.
This state pulls toward the phone. Toward sending something, or checking whether
they've sent something. Not yet.
Thank your heart for still wanting them in it.

**Release** — Two things are running. One of them feels like getting clear. What
it's actually doing is preparing a case — and **a case needs someone to lose.**
Put down the case. Gently name what your system moved to protect.
And swap the question. Not *what's wrong with me that this keeps happening* —
that returns nothing you can use. **What was this for?** Something in you moved
fast when the rupture happened, and it moved to protect something. Understanding
what doesn't excuse anyone and doesn't settle who was right. Leave the question
open, with full permission to stop building the case.

**Rise** — Step outside it. See yourself from across the room. Someone sitting
there with something unresolved, breathing four and six. Speak to them as *you*.
*Something broke, and you want it back. That's all this is so far.*
Now stay outside a moment longer, and stand somewhere else — **where they were
standing.** Same room, same evening, seen from their side. What landed, from
there. What they didn't know that you did. Not agreeing with them. Just standing
where they stood, briefly.
The Chosen Self is the version of you who can do that without losing your own
account.
Then one thing. Where are your feet, what's in your hands, what's your very next
move? Speak it out loud.
And one good thing that's already here with you now.

## T2-03 · Rebuild

**Recognise** — Start in your body. Chest and stomach, usually, at the same time
— something hot and something dropping. The jaw. A restlessness that has nowhere
to put itself. Name it quietly.
Now one layer deeper, and one simple word for the root.
