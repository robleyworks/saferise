# Sovereign phase prompts — the library

1 October 2026. The on-screen prompts for a Sovereign session, one set per
protocol. Method, placement and rulings: `SOVEREIGN-SESSION-SCREEN-SPEC.md`.

**Source: `SafeRise_AllTracks_RecordingScripts.docx`.** Not
`SafeRise_SpokenTextOnly.md`, which strips `RED/ACTION` cues by design and so
loses the entire somatic layer. Every line below is a translation of what the
guided voice says at that step — from *"here is what is happening to you"* into
*"here is what to say"* — in the founder's own language and order. No new
vocabulary.

**Status.** Complete. Thirty-one protocols — the Clearing, Track 01, Track 02 and
Track 03 — one hundred and twenty-four prompts.

---

## Rules that hold across every protocol

**Regulate is not uniform.** Most protocols open with the count. Shutdown states
(T1-07, T1-10) open with **contact** and make the counting optional — "longer
out than in" is the only part that matters, and the count is dropped if it is
too much. That adaptation is in the scripts and must survive into the prompts:
demanding a count from someone shut down is the failure the script was written
to avoid.

**Every somatic cue is offered, never instructed.** The scripts say so in the
cue itself. Prompts use *if a hand there helps*, *if you want one*, *whichever
is nearer* — never an imperative.

**Release always has the same shape, with different content.** Two things are
running. One is the state, which stays and is not argued with. The other is the
expensive one — the rerun, the searching, the auditing, the measuring, the case
— and it is the only one that can be put down. Then the question swaps: never
*what's wrong with me*, always **what was this for?**

**Rise always begins outside.** Someone over there, breathing four and six,
spoken to as *you*. That distancing is the mechanism, not a tone.

**Rise always ends with one thing already here.** Small beats large.

## T0-00 · Clearing

**Recognise** — Start right in your body. Notice your jaw, your shoulders, the
weight in the pit of your stomach — wherever you're holding on. Name what you
find, quietly, without needing to change a thing.
Now look one layer deeper: the emotion sitting on those sensations, and the
thoughts spinning around them. Beneath the surface noise is what actually needs
you. Give that root one simple word.

**Regulate** — Nothing to solve right now. This is just breath.
Inhale softly through your nose for four. Let it out, slow and complete, for
six. Always a longer exhale than inhale.
A hand over the centre of your chest, if you want one. Let your hands open —
fingers wide, then loose. Feet into the floor, and feel the floor push back.
Thank your heart for keeping you grounded here.

**Release** — The tension you're feeling is answering an old memory, not the
room you're actually sitting in.
That physical brace was just an instinct trying to keep you safe. Gently name
what your system has been bracing against — the old expectation, the fear, the
past outcome.
Now unhook today from that old backlog. You have full permission to drop what
doesn't belong to this moment.

**Rise** — Move your fingers. One hand. That's the whole ask — and it's yours.
Now step outside it. Picture yourself over there, not in trouble — and speak to
that person as you. Someone safe and capable.
The Chosen Self: the version of you who meets this trigger next time. How they
stand, how they breathe, how they answer with calm sovereignty instead of
defence.
Then what you're building — not the distant goal, the work right in front of
you today. Where are your feet, what's in your hands, what's your very next
move? Speak it out loud.
And one good thing that's already here with you now.

## T1-01 · Anxiety Reset

**Recognise** — Find the signal. Chest, throat, stomach, behind the eyes —
wherever it's loudest. There's no correct answer and you're not being marked.
Then one word for it. Not the story. The state.

**Regulate** — Four in, six out. Longer out than in, every time.
Don't pull for a big one. A big breath asks for effort and effort is pointing
the wrong way. Small is fine. Long is what matters.
Attention into the middle of your chest. A hand there if it helps.
Stay here a while. This is the part that does the most, and the part people cut
short.

**Release** — There's a second thing running underneath. *Not now.* *Not again.*
*What is wrong with me.*
That one costs more than the state, and it's the only one of the two you can put
down. The state turned up. You're not being asked to want it, or approve of it.
Only to stop spending yourself on the fact that it arrived.
Now swap the question. Not what's wrong with me — **what was this for?** Your
body runs a fast state because a fast state is useful. It goes ahead of thought.
Leave the question open.

**Rise** — Step outside it. Picture yourself from across the room — not close
up, across. Someone over there, breathing four and six. Talk to them as *you*.
*You're having a fast day. You've had these before.*
Now put your attention somewhere on purpose. That's what all of this is for.
Make it a scene, with detail — a general hope does nothing. Where are you,
what's in front of you.
And one thing already here. Small beats large. The chair. The light. That you
stopped and did this.

## T1-02 · Anger Alchemy

**Recognise** — Your body registered it before you'd finished deciding what you
thought. So — where is it. Jaw. Hands. The back of the neck. Heat across the
face. One word for the state.
Now notice what that word left out. It left out them — their name, their face,
what they said, and the whole argument you have assembled and ready. Not because
the argument is wrong. It will be just as right afterwards, intact.

**Regulate** — Four counts in. Six out. Longer out than in.
Your breath wants to go short and sharp. Let it want that. Go the other way
anyway.
Your hands are closed. Open them — fingers wide, then loose.
Attention into the middle of your chest. Not the jaw; the jaw will hold on to
it. It'll come up before it goes down. That's normal here.

**Release** — Now the conversation in your head. Notice something about it: you
win it. Every single time. And every run puts your body back where it started.
You've been re-lighting yourself all afternoon.
The anger stays. It's yours, it's earned, nobody's taking it. Put down the
rerun. Only the rerun.
And the question: not what's wrong with me — **what was this for?** Anger goes
toward things. Fear backs away; this walks at it. It turns up where something is
being crossed.

**Rise** — Step back from it. Somebody across the room, jaw set, running a
conversation for the sixth time. Say it to them as *you*. *You are angry. You
have a reason.* Both halves stay. Neither takes anything off the other.
Now something later today. Not the confrontation where you win — you've had that
one five times and it cost you the afternoon. The one where you're steady and it
still matters to you just as much. What do your hands do while you say it.
And one thing already true, and small, and nothing to do with any of this.

## T1-03 · Overwhelm Threshold

**Recognise** — Find where it is in your body, before any of the words about it.
Then name the state. One word.
Now one sorting question, and it takes a second. **Too much of what?** Too much
to do — the list, the deadlines, the people waiting. Too much coming in — noise,
light, people, screens. Or too much at once inside you — angry and frightened
and sad, all live, with no way to pick one.
And if the honest answer is *I don't know, I just can't tell what's wrong with
me today* — that's a real answer, and it's the third one.

**Regulate** — Four counts in. Six counts out. Longer out than in.
Overwhelm breathes high and fast, at the top of the chest. We're going the other
way.
Attention into the centre of your chest. Not the list.
Press your feet into the floor. Feel the floor push back.
There's nothing you could do about any of it in the next minute anyway.

**Release** — Two things are running, and only one of them is the load.
Too much to do: you're going round the list, touching everything and starting
nothing. Too much coming in: you're pushing through, holding a normal face on,
spending everything on looking fine. Too much at once inside: you're sorting,
trying to decide which feeling comes first, as though there were an order.
Let the amount be what it is. Put down the circling.
And the question: not what's wrong with me — **what was this for?**

**Rise** — Step outside it. Someone a little way off with a great deal on,
breathing four and six. Speak to them as *you*. *You have a lot on. It is a lot.
And you are steadier now than when you sat down.*
Now forward, and small. Not the version where it's all done — that scene isn't
available and building it would be a lie. One thing. The next thing. Where are
you, what's in front of you, what's the first movement you make. Not the most
important one. Not the one you're most behind on. The next one.
And one thing already handled.

## T1-04 · Abandonment Wound

**Recognise** — Where is it sitting? Usually low. A drop through the stomach.
Sometimes a pull across the chest, or the throat closing. Sometimes a hum
through all of you that won't hold still. One word for it.
The state is here in the room, and you're the one who noticed it was here —
you're already standing slightly outside it.
There's a question with no answer yet, and your body has gone ahead and answered
it. That's what it does with silence. It fills it fast, so it isn't standing in
an unknown.

**Regulate** — Four counts in. Six counts out. Attention into the middle of your
chest.
A hand at the sternum, if you want one. Warmth there does something on its own
that nothing you think will match.
You'll feel the pull toward the phone. Toward checking. Toward saying the thing
that would settle it right now.

**Release** — There's the state, and there's the searching. Every pass finds
something, and everything it finds could mean two things, so you go round again.
That's not investigation. That's a circuit.
Let the state stay — it's here and nobody's arguing with it. Put down the
searching. Just that.
And the question: not what's wrong with me that I'm like this about people —
you've asked that a hundred times and it has never once given you anything.
**What was this for?** Something in you moves very fast when connection is in
doubt.

**Rise** — Step outside it. Someone across the room, phone face down, breathing
four and six. Speak to them as *you*. *You don't know yet. And you're steady
enough to not know for a while.*
That's the whole sentence. Not that it's fine — that you can stand in the
not-knowing without the state deciding it on your behalf.
Now forward. Not the scene where they reply; that one isn't yours to build, and
rehearsing it is what we just put down. You, somewhere specific, later today,
with this still unresolved. What's in your hands.
And one thing already here.

## T1-05 · Shame Dissolution

**Recognise** — Find where it sits in your body, before any of the words.
Contained, not diminished. Now name the state. One word.
Notice which one it is: *I did something bad*, or something closer to *I am the
thing that's wrong*. For now, only this — that's a state. Whatever else it is,
it is first a state, running in a body, with a name.

**Regulate** — Four counts in. Six counts out. Longer out than in. Attention
into the centre of your chest.
If a hand there helps, put one there. This state pulls the body inward, and
warmth at the chest is one of the few things it accepts.
Let the shoulders come back a little. Not straightening up. Just less curled.

**Release** — Two things are running. The state arrived, it's unpleasant, and it
isn't being argued with.
Set down the case. Only the case — not whatever real thing may be underneath it.
That stays, and you can look at it later, from somewhere steadier.
And the question. You already know the other one: *what is wrong with me* — you
have asked it many times and it has never once returned anything you could use.
It only ever returns more of itself. **What was this for?** Something in you
learned to go small and quiet and hidden.

**Rise** — Step outside it. See yourself from across the room. Someone sitting
there, shoulders forward, breathing four and six. Speak to them as *you*, not
*I*. *You are having a bad hour with yourself. That's what this is.*
Now something concrete. Later today, somewhere specific, doing something
ordinary. Not a scene where you've resolved anything about yourself. Where are
you. What's in your hands. Who can see you.
Ordinary is the point. This state says you should be hidden. The scene where
you're out, doing a normal thing, in view, is the answer to it.
And one thing already here.

## T1-06 · Grief Integration

**Recognise** — Where is it today? Chest, probably. A weight, or a hollow. Some
days both together, which makes no sense and is still what happens. Throat.
Behind the eyes. Or a tiredness with nothing to do with sleep. One word.
That's the accurate one. This doesn't hold still — gone for a week, then back in
a supermarket aisle over nothing. It's here today. There's no backwards for it
to be.

**Regulate** — Four counts in. Six out. If it catches, let it catch. Don't
smooth it out.
A hand on your chest, if you'd like one there.
Attention into the middle of your chest. Including the part that hurts.
If crying arrives, it's arriving into the right room.

**Release** — Two things here, and only one of them is the grief.
The first is the loss. That stays. It isn't going to be reframed or argued down.
The second is quieter and has been running a long time: bracing before it comes,
trying to have it at convenient hours, checking whether this is too much of it,
or too long, or the wrong shape. And underneath, usually — *this shouldn't have
happened*, said again and again to something that already did.
Which of those two is costing you more today? Put that one down.

**Rise** — Step back a moment. Someone sitting there, carrying something,
breathing four and six. Say it to them as *you*. *You're carrying something
heavy. Of course you are.*
Nothing to fix in that. No second half where it gets better.
Now later today. Not the day this is finished — that day isn't available and I
won't pretend it is. An ordinary hour, carried out while carrying this. Where
are you. What's in your hands. What's around you. Both things at once. Not
instead of it. Alongside.
And one thing already here.

## T1-07 · Shutdown Recovery

**Recognise** — One word, and we're not going hunting. There's no need to find
where it sits — in this state that's often hard, and not finding it belongs to
the state rather than to any failure of yours.
Numb isn't nothing, though. It's somewhere your body has moved to, and it moved
there for a reason. Something is happening, just at a level you don't have a
window onto.

**Regulate** — Contact, before any counting.
A hand on your chest. Or hold your own arm — whichever is nearer. Let it stay.
Warmth and weight is what gets through when finer things don't.
Now the breath, and it doesn't have to be four and six today. **Longer out than
in. That's the only part that matters.** The count is there if you want it. If
counting is too much, leave it out.
Nothing to achieve here.

**Release** — There's the shutdown, and there's something quieter running
underneath it. You know the one. *Pathetic. Lazy. Everyone else manages.*
Running all day, low, at a volume you've stopped hearing. It isn't gentler for
being quiet — it's the same thing the loud states do, in a lower voice.
The state can stay. It arrived, it's not being argued with, and it isn't being
ranked against how you were last month. Put down the other one.
And the question: not what's wrong with me that I can't function — **what was
this for?**

**Rise** — Step back a little. Gently. Someone sitting or lying there, hand on
their chest. Say it to them as *you*. *You're shut down today. And you did this
anyway.* Both halves.
Now something very small. Not the day you're back to yourself — that scene isn't
available and I'm not going to build it for you. One thing. Standing up. A glass
of water. Opening a window.
**Small isn't a reduced version of this step. Today it's the whole of it.**
And before you come back — you did this. On a day like this one.

## T1-08 · Jealousy Release

**Recognise** — Where is it? Stomach, usually. That turning over. Sometimes heat
up through the chest and into the face — sometimes a coldness instead, which
catches people out. One word.

**Regulate** — Four in. Six out. Attention to the middle of your chest.
A hand at the sternum, or the phone face down — whichever helps. This one pulls
hard toward looking. A phone, a profile, a room.

**Release** — Now the comparing. It was always going to answer every round —
you're running it, and you know exactly which way it goes.
The state can stay. Put down the measuring.
And the question: not what's wrong with me that I'm this person. You've asked.
It gave you nothing. **What was this for?** Jealousy points at something. Often
not at the person — past them, at something you want and haven't said out loud.
Sometimes haven't said to yourself.

**Rise** — Step back. Someone over there with their phone face down, breathing
four and six. Say it to them as *you*. *You want something. That's all this is
so far.*
Notice where your attention has been sitting. On them, mostly — which has never
once produced anything.
Now something that's actually yours. Where are you. What's in your hands. What
are you making, or asking for, or starting. And one small thing you'll actually
do about it — today, feeling exactly like this, not on a better day.
And one thing already here. Yours. Not compared to anyone.

## T1-09 · Insecurity Anchor

**Recognise** — Find where it is in your body, before any of the words. Upright.
Not falling. Now name the state. One word.
*Not enough. Not really. Found out eventually.* We're not going to argue with
it — arguing gives it something to answer.
The opinion is in the room. You're the one who noticed it was in the room.
That's a different position, and you're already standing in it.

**Regulate** — Four counts in. Six counts out. Longer out than in. Attention
into the centre of your chest.
Press your feet into the floor. Feel the floor push back. This state is unsteady
and the floor is not.
Nothing to be good at here. Nobody's watching this bit.

**Release** — Two things are running. The state arrived, it's uncomfortable, it
isn't being argued with.
Set down the auditing. Only the auditing.
And the question: not what's wrong with me — that's the question the auditing is
already asking, on repeat, and it has never once returned an answer you could
use. **What was this for?** Watching yourself closely in a room is a skill. It
was learned somewhere, because at some point it was useful to know exactly how
you were landing. It's still running.

**Rise** — Step outside it. See yourself from across the room. Someone sitting
there, feet on the floor, breathing four and six. Speak to them as *you*, not
*I*. *You are unsteady today. That's a state, and it's one you've been in
before.*
Notice where your attention is sitting. A room you were in last week. One you
haven't walked into yet. Neither has anything in it you can use. **Everything
you actually have is here.** So put it somewhere by choice.
Where are you later today, unsteady, and doing it anyway. What's in your hands.
And one thing already here.

## T1-10 · Powerlessness & Despair

**Recognise** — One word. We're not going looking for it. You don't have to find
where it sits; in this state that's often hard, and not finding it is part of
what this is rather than a failure to look properly.
Two things are stacked here and they get treated as one. There's what can't be
changed. And there's the state you're in about it. Different things. Only one of
them is within reach.

**Regulate** — Contact first. The counting can wait.
A hand on your chest, or hold your own arm — whichever is easier to arrange. Let
it stay there. Warmth and pressure is what this state will take when it won't
take much else.
Now breathe, and **longer out than in**. Count it if you can. Don't if you can't.
Nothing here to get right.

**Release** — The situation is real. Whatever part of it genuinely can't be
moved — that's real, and nobody's going to tell you otherwise, or that you
haven't tried, or that there's an angle you've missed.
And then there's the other thing. Going back over how it should have gone. The
version where it was different, run again. A quarrel with something that already
happened.
That's the expensive one, and it's the only one of the two you can put down.
Which isn't the same as calling it acceptable.

**Rise** — Step back a little, if you can. Someone sitting there with a hand on
their chest, in the middle of something hard. Say it to them as *you*. *This is
a lot. It isn't your fault. And you're still here.* No correction in that.
Nothing being fixed.
Now — remember your fingers moving, at the start. Most of this is outside your
reach right now. **That was inside it.**
So — one thing later that's yours to decide. Small enough that you're certain of
it. Standing up. A glass of water. A window. Not a step toward fixing any of it.
And one thing already here.

# Track 02 · Relationship Healing

## T2-01 · Speak

**Recognise** — Find where it is in your body, before any of the words. Nothing
behind it yet. Now name the state. One word.
Not the conversation. The state you'd be walking in with.

**Regulate** — Four counts in. Six counts out. Longer out than in. Attention
into the centre of your chest. A hand there if it helps.
The throat is worth some attention here. This state closes it, and a closed
throat changes your voice before you've said anything. Let the jaw come apart
slightly. Let the tongue drop off the roof of the mouth.

**Release** — Two things are running. Some of it is preparation — genuinely. But
past the second version it stops being preparation and becomes an argument
rehearsed with someone who isn't in the room. And notice which way it goes: in
every draft, you have the better lines.
Put down the rehearsing.
And the question: not what's wrong with me that I can't just have a conversation
— that returns nothing, and plenty of people find this hard for reasons that
make sense. **What was this for?**

**Rise** — Step outside it. See yourself from across the room. Someone sitting
there, about to say something difficult, breathing four and six. Speak to them
as *you*. *You have something to say. You are allowed to say it badly.*
Now something concrete. Not the whole conversation — you don't control most of
it, and rehearsing their half is what we just put down. **Only your first
sentence.** Where you'll be. What you'll be doing with your hands. The actual
words you'll open with.
And one thing already here.

## T2-02 · Repair

**Recognise** — Find where it is in your body, before any of the words. The
break has an edge on both sides. Now name the state. One word.
They have an account too, and theirs will not match yours, and that on its own
is not evidence of anything. We're not touching either account. Only the state.

**Regulate** — Four counts in. Six counts out. Longer out than in.
Attention into the centre of your chest — including the part that aches. We're
not going around it. A hand there if it helps.
This state pulls toward the phone. Toward sending something, or checking whether
they've sent something. Not yet.

**Release** — Two things are running. One of them feels like getting clear. What
it's actually doing is preparing a case — and **a case needs someone to lose.**
Put down the case.
And the question: not what's wrong with me that this keeps happening — that
returns nothing you can use. **What was this for?** Something in you moved fast
when the rupture happened, and it moved to protect something. Understanding what
it was protecting doesn't excuse anyone and doesn't settle who was right. Leave
the question there.

**Rise** — Step outside it. See yourself from across the room. Someone sitting
there with something unresolved, breathing four and six. Speak to them as *you*.
*Something broke, and you want it back. That's all this is so far.*
Now stay outside a moment longer, and stand somewhere else — where they were
standing. Same room, same evening, seen from their side. What landed, from
there. What they didn't know that you did. Not agreeing with them. Just standing
where they stood, briefly.
And one thing already here.

## T2-03 · Rebuild

**Recognise** — Where is it sitting? Chest and stomach, usually, at the same
time. Something hot and something dropping. The jaw. A restlessness that has
nowhere to put itself. One word.
What happened, happened. This doesn't touch it.

**Regulate** — Four counts in. Six out. Longer out than in. Attention into the
middle of your chest. A hand there if it helps.
Sleep's probably gone. That's what this state does — it doesn't stand down at
night, because it's decided nights are when things get past you.

**Release** — Two things running, and the first one is not up for discussion.
The second is going back through everything else. Every conversation, every
trip, every ordinary Tuesday — re-read, looking for what you missed.
Does it ever come out clean? It doesn't. Every pass finds something, because
anything can be read two ways once you're reading for it. You could go through
five years and never reach the end.
The betrayal stays. Put down the re-reading. That's not deciding you were wrong.

**Rise** — Step outside it. Someone across the room, breathing four and six,
holding something heavy and holding it steadily. Speak to them as *you*. *You
were right about what happened. And nothing has to be settled today unless it
does.* Both halves — the second one is the one people skip.
Now forward, and small. Not the conversation where it's resolved; you don't
control that half, and it may not be available at all. Today. Somewhere
specific, doing something ordinary.
And one thing already here.

## T2-04 · Release

**Recognise** — Where does it sit? Chest, tight rather than hot. Jaw. Something
in the shoulders. A heaviness that's been there so long it stopped registering
as a feeling and started registering as a fact about your life. One word.
Nobody is auditing it, and nothing here says you've kept it unfairly.

**Regulate** — Four in. Six out. Attention into the middle of your chest.
Unclench the jaw. Open the hands if they've closed. This one holds quietly, and
it holds for years.

**Release** — So — two things. It doesn't come out level. It isn't built to. **A
tally you're keeping about someone who isn't keeping one back will never
balance**, and some part of you has been waiting for it to.
The grievance stays. Every item on it stays. Put down the keeping.
Which isn't forgiving anyone. Nobody's asking. It's stopping an accounting that
was never going to be settled by more accounting.
And the question: not what's wrong with me that I can't let this go — **what was
this for?**

**Rise** — Step back. Someone over there, breathing four and six, who has been
reasonable for a very long time. Speak to them as *you*. *You kept a fair
account. And it was never going to be paid.*
Now — you can see where your attention's been. On a ledger, mostly, for years.
So put it somewhere by choice. One thing you'll actually do: stop absorbing, or
decline. Small enough that you'll do it this week, feeling exactly as you do
now. Say it once to yourself.
And one thing already here that the list has nothing to do with.

## T2-05 · Open

**Recognise** — Where is it? Chest, often — not tight exactly. More like
something held slightly apart. Throat. A stillness through the middle of you
that isn't calm. One word.
You described a state, and something in you did the describing. Those aren't the
same thing.

**Regulate** — Four in. Six out. Attention into the middle of your chest.
A hand there if you want one. Your own warmth, at the place that's been held
apart.

**Release** — Two things going on. The second is the managing, and it's so
practised you've stopped noticing you're doing it.
The distance can stay exactly as it is. Put down the managing — just for the
next few minutes, in a room where nobody's coming closer.
And the question: not what's wrong with me that I can't just be open — that one
arrives from outside and it's never helped anyone. **What was this for?**
Something in you decided that a certain amount of closeness is where it stops
being safe.

**Rise** — Step back a little. Someone over there with a hand on their chest,
breathing four and six. Speak to them as *you*. *You're not cold. You're
guarded. Those are different things.*
Now one thing, and make it smaller than you think. Not opening up. Not the
conversation. Something almost invisible — staying in the room a minute longer.
Answering the actual question instead of the near one. Not making the joke.
Small enough that you'll actually do it, guarded.
And one thing already here.

## T2-06 · Level

**Recognise** — Where does it sit? Chest. Throat, sometimes — the place where
the thing you didn't say is. A tightness that comes up sharply when it happens
again. One word.
Being right and being settled are two separate projects. You can have both —
just not in the same minute.

**Regulate** — Four in. Six out. Attention into the middle of your chest.
Unclench the jaw. Let the shoulders come down.

**Release** — Two things. One of them you're winning. **A case that unanswerable
can't be raised without a fight, so it doesn't get raised. It just gets
bigger.**
