## The four rules this version is built on

**1 · Name the state outright.** No suggestion, no gesture, no abstraction.

**2 · No state appears twice.** Not within a film, and not across the three.

**3 · No contractions.** "You have", "it is", "do not".

**4 · Nothing unbuilt is presented as available** — and the words never say so
either. Forthcoming tracks, roles and sectors are carried by a first-access
offer and a visual treatment, not by an admission.

### State allocation

| Film | States |
|---|---|
| B2C homepage | anxious · dread · anger · shame · panic |
| B2B organisations | shaking · defensiveness · exhausted · resentful |
| Member orientation | overwhelm · irritability · shutdown · fear |

Thirteen distinct states, no overlap. Any edit introducing a state checks this
table first.

## The scope wall — beat 13

**Six seconds, no voice.** The viewer is reading names and finding themselves;
narration competes with the only thing you want them doing.

**Build, do not fade in.** A grid that fades in as a block reads as a catalogue
page; a wall that builds reads as range.

**Titles legible. No prices, no counts, no track category labels.** Nobody
thinks "I need Relationship Healing," they think "the abandonment one."

### Order is a persuasion decision

Open on the familiar and low-stigma so the viewer is already inside before the
heavier names land. Somebody who meets *Powerlessness and Despair* first decides
this is not for them; somebody who meets it tenth has recognised themselves
twice already.

| # | Cover | Why it is here |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Anxiety Reset | the most familiar entry point, and the least stigmatised |
| 2 | Burnout and Overload | universal, and nobody is ashamed of it |
| 3 | The Abandonment Wound | the most arresting name in the catalogue |
| 4 | Conscious Separation | specific enough to stop somebody mid-scroll |
| 5 | Shame Dissolution | names the thing people do not say out loud |
| 6 | Grief Integration | universal, and the one people arrive already looking for |
| 7 | Insecurity Anchor | the working day, quietly |
| 8 | Shutdown Recovery | intriguing rather than familiar — the viewer asks what it means |
| 9 | Belonging Gap | editing yourself to fit a room |
| 10 | Powerlessness and Despair | the heaviest, and it lands last for that reason |

All ten come from the three live tracks.

## First access — the beat after the wall

> **VO:** Get first access.
>
> **On screen:** forthcoming track names **as titles only, no cover art**, in
> the same typeface at a lighter weight.

### The six that appear

On the coming-soon page **and** in the build plan:

> Executive Presence · Embodied Nutrition · Strength & Return · Sleep & Recovery
> · Elevation Series · Sex & Intimacy

### Three that do not, and why

**Entrepreneur's, Money and Addiction Recovery** are live on the coming-soon page
and scheduled nowhere; Addiction Recovery additionally carries a clinical hold.
First access implies a date exists. They stay out until scheduled or removed
from the page.

**Sales & Quota** is in the next seven but has no band and no page presence. It
cannot appear in a film before it appears on the site.

### Why the words no longer say "in build"

"In build" is a confession. It tells the viewer the product is a construction
site at the moment the film is showing them its range, and it buys nothing —
*get first access* already carries the meaning, because nobody needs first
access to something they can already use.

**The honesty now sits in the design** and has to be built that way rather than
assumed: shipped in full, forthcoming as titles only, lighter weight, no art.
If it reads as too subtle once cut, the fallback is one small word above that
group: **Next**.

**The requirement, stated so a later edit does not lose it:** a viewer must not
finish the film believing those tracks are openable today.

⚠ **No number.** Not in the read, not on screen.

## The alignment section

**"Everyone gets the foundation. Then one built for what they do, and one built
for where they do it."** The 8 + 2 model in one sentence, without naming the
model, without a count, and without a grid.

**The three examples.** All catalogue-real: **I01 Healthcare**, **I07 Education**
("hold the room — capacity for the classroom, the staff room and the return
home") and **I03 Hospitality**, with the role layer where **R04 Frontline
Service** sits. Three is the limit — a fourth turns an argument into a list, and
the point is made by contrast, not by coverage.

They are also the right examples for the first market. The B2B register's casting
rules already say **Caribbean first market**; the earlier draft's "ward sister"
and "contact centre lead" were NHS and UK-outsourcing vocabulary describing
nobody the first buyers employ.

## The sector wall — the new silent beat

**Five seconds, no voice.** Same device as the B2C scope wall: the voice carries
the argument, the screen carries the range.

**Names only, no art** — which sidesteps the cover problem entirely, since the
thirty role and industry covers are still unallocated.

**Sectors, ordered so the first market sees itself first:**

> Hospitality · Healthcare · Aviation · Education · Foodservice · Government ·
> Financial Services · Retail · Emergency Response · Events · Security
> Operations · Real Estate · Legal Services · Technology · Manufacturing ·
> Sport Performance

**Roles, beneath and lighter still:**

> Frontline Service · People Leadership · Client Success · Operations Flow ·
> Teaching Impact · People Support · Financial Focus · Sales Growth · Business
> Ownership · Project Delivery · Legal Clarity · Tech Innovation · Protective
> Readiness · Athletic Performance

⚠ **This wall sits under the first-access line, not under "everyone gets the
foundation", and the placement is load-bearing.** The register records thirty
covers made for the role and industry protocols but does not record their
content as live. Under the first-access line their status is unambiguous. Under
the foundation line it would read as thirty things a buyer can have today.
**Confirm the build status of the role and industry protocols before this beat
is cut** — if they are live, the wall can move earlier and the film gets
stronger.

⚠ **No count of roles, sectors or combinations**, spoken or on screen. The
14 × 16 arithmetic is a good internal fact and a bad public one.

⚠ **"Everyone gets the foundation" depends on an open decision.** Sex & Intimacy
is in the next seven but is not a Foundation 8 member, and a track cannot be
both "one of the eight every employee receives" and a separate consumer track.

### Two things that cannot go in

- **"Better judgement and performance on display"** — a performance-improvement
  claim, the same family as "increases retention", implying observability that
  cuts against the seat-reporting commitment.
- **"Your augmented employee assistance programme"** — hands back the argument
  the film spends thirty seconds winning. The word *assistance* drifted into
  three successive drafts and is kept out on purpose.

⚠ **Gate:** the film raises the 3am counter and cannot close it in ninety-four
seconds. The organisations page must, before the film ships.
