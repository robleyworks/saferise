Plans

Build more capacity where life asks the most of you.

Regulation gives you back access to internal resources that become harder to reach when a triggered state is governing you.

With more internal resource, you have more command of your attention. With more command of attention, you can perceive more clearly, judge more soundly, and respond with greater authenticity to who you are, what you value, and what you genuinely desire.

Choose your scope

Choose the scope of life in which you want to build and apply that capacity.

Each plan builds on the same underlying work — nervous-system regulation, greater command of attention, sounder judgement and more deliberate response — then extends that capacity into a wider part of your life.

Inside every protocol

Each protocol is a complete guided session and support system of tools dedicated to the moment dysregulation is happening — plus what you need before it and after.

What you need changes depending on where you are in the moment. SafeRise gives you different forms of support for regulation, understanding, integration and real-life use — all built around the same specific state.

01

REGULATE

When the state has already taken hold.

Start where your system actually is. These resources help create enough regulation and internal resource for attention and reflection to become available again.

◎

Guided Experience

The full Recognise → Regulate → Release → Rise protocol.

Follow the complete process when you do not want to work out the next step yourself. The experience is built specifically around the state you opened.

≈

Breathwork

A direct way back into rhythm.

Use paced breathing with the full protocol or on its own when you need a simpler way to help the body settle and restore capacity.

↟

Somatic Release

For when thinking is not the place to begin.

Movement, pressure, weight and sensation give the body something practical to do with activation before asking it for reflection.

02

UNDERSTAND

When you need to know what just happened.

Regulation creates room. These resources help you use that room to examine the pattern, the meaning you made and what captured your attention.

◇

How This Works

Understand the mechanism, not just the instruction.

See what may be happening in the body and why the protocol moves in this order, so the method becomes something you understand rather than simply follow.

◫

Perspectives

More than one way to understand the same experience.

Explore psychological, philosophical and human-development lenses without being told that any single explanation has to be yours.

⌖

Attention Advisory

See what has your attention — and what it is costing.

Examine where attention tends to go in this state, what keeps pulling it there, and where more deliberate attention may become available.

03

INTEGRATE

When the moment has passed but the learning should not.

Turn an experience into something you can see, name and return to without turning your practice into a score or performance system.

▤

Progress Journal

Put the experience into your own words.

Protocol-specific prompts help you capture what happened, what became visible and what you want to carry forward. Your writing remains yours.

↕

Capacity Check

Notice what changed without grading yourself.

A simple before-and-after check helps you recognise whether more capacity became available during the session — without streaks, scores or targets.

04

USE IT IN REAL LIFE

When you need the work to leave the screen with you.

Condensed, practical supports help you return to the method when there is no time or attention for the full experience.

≡

Cue Card

The essential sequence when bandwidth is low.

A condensed version of the method for the moments when you have done the work before and need the order, not another explanation.

↔

Disclosure & Invitation

When regulation needs to become communication.

Language for explaining what is happening without making another person responsible for fixing it — and for asking clearly for what would help.

▣

Protocol Guide

Know what to use, when, and why.

A practical reference for returning to the protocol outside the guided experience and choosing the resource that fits the moment.

The value of the library

One state can require regulation in the body, understanding in the mind, reflection afterward and support in the next real conversation. The resources are designed to stay with that full arc.

How access works

One membership. More access as you move up.

Each level includes the tracks beneath it. Move up when you want a wider library; move down when you need less. Your own journal entries remain yours regardless of the plan you choose.
