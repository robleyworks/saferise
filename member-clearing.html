The Clearing

The session to use when you do not know which state you are in. It does not ask you to decide first.

Begin where you are

Headphones if you have them. Sitting or lying down both work.

There is no clock on this player and no progress bar. Watching time pass is the opposite of what the session is for. It ends when it ends.
