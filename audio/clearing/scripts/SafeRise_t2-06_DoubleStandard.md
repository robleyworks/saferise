## 2 · Invitation / third-party script

**The Double Standard Protocol**

*Staying regulated when the rules feel unequal*

**The Invitation Script**

*“I've noticed the rules between us haven't always felt equal, and I want to look at that honestly with you — including my own part in it. There's a short practice for this. Would you be willing to go through it with me?”*

**Why I'm Asking**

Because I'd rather understand where the imbalance actually comes from than keep having the same fairness argument over and over.

**What This Involves**

Each of us naming where things have felt unequal, and honestly checking whether we hold ourselves to the same standard we're asking of the other.

**If They're Not Ready Yet**

If this feels too raw to do together right now, I can start by just naming my own part in it first, and we can build from there.

**The Third-Party Script**

*“There's a real imbalance in how the rules seem to apply between us, and I think we need outside perspective to help us see it clearly — this has been hard for either of us to look at honestly alone.”*

**When To Use This**

Use this when the same fairness argument repeats without resolution, or when the imbalance involves something significant enough that self-honesty alone hasn't been sufficient.

**What To Ask For**

Ask for a mediator or therapist who can name patterns neutrally, without either person needing to win the argument about who's right.
