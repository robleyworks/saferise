**T0-00 · CLEARING · TRANSITION**

**Clearing**

Frameworks: HeartMath, Kross

*Open with the Pangolin Anchor, then continue.*

**PURPLE/MUSIC***   bed in from the first word. Lighter than the protocols. No turn — sustained throughout.*

**VOICE***   unhurried and level. Makes no assumption about how the member arrived. At ease. Closest register to a conversation.*

There's an engagement ahead of you.

**GOLD/PAUSE**

Not yet. In a minute. Right now you're on the way to it, and this is that part.

**GOLD/PAUSE***   long*

Most of the time nobody stops here. You move from one situation straight into the next and find out afterwards what you carried across.

**GOLD/PAUSE**

So — what's ahead. Name it, just to yourself. One line.

**GOLD/PAUSE***   very long*

Good. Leave it there. We'll come back to it.

**GOLD/PAUSE**

Four moves. Recognise, Regulate, Release, Rise. You're running them on the way in.

**Recognise**

Where are you, right now?

**GOLD/PAUSE***   very long*

Not why. Just what's here.

**BLUE/ILLUSTRATION***   a threshold, seen from inside it. Light ahead. The figure still, mid-step.*

**GOLD/PAUSE**

Start with the body, because it answers faster than anything else. Jaw. Shoulders. Hands. Stomach. Breath — where it's sitting, how far down it's going.

**GOLD/PAUSE***   long*

No verdict on any of it. Just what's there.

**GOLD/PAUSE***   very long*

Now attention. Where has it been circling?

**GOLD/PAUSE***   long*

A conversation that didn't finish. A task left open. A sentence you're still composing. Whatever it is, notice it's where attention keeps landing.

**GOLD/PAUSE***   very long*

One word for where you are. Whatever's true. It doesn't need to be interesting.

**GOLD/PAUSE***   long*

That's the skill, and it's the whole of this step. You just found your own state without anything forcing you to look.

**Regulate**

Four counts in.

**TEAL/BREATH***   in, four*

**TEAL/BREATH***   out, six*

Six out. Longer out than in.

**TEAL/BREATH***   in, four*

**TEAL/BREATH***   out, six*

Nothing to fix. You're not trying to feel different. You're arriving.

**TEAL/BREATH***   in, four*

**TEAL/BREATH***   out, six*

Attention to the middle of your chest.

**RED/ACTION***   hand to heart, flat, light. Offered, not instructed.*

**TEAL/BREATH***   in, four*

**TEAL/BREATH***   out, six*

**TEAL/BREATH***   in, four*

**TEAL/BREATH***   out, six*

Same count you'd use in the worst hour of a bad week. Nothing about it changes.

**GOLD/PAUSE***   extended*

**Release**

Some of what's here belongs to what's ahead. Most of it doesn't.

**GOLD/PAUSE***   long*

Nothing to get rid of. It simply doesn't need to come with you.

**GOLD/PAUSE**

That unfinished situation attention kept circling — it can stay where it is. It'll still be there afterwards. It doesn't need you right now.

**TEAL/BREATH***   in, four*

**TEAL/BREATH***   out, six*

**GOLD/PAUSE***   long*

And whatever the body's holding — let as much of it settle as wants to. Not all of it. Whatever goes.

**GOLD/PAUSE***   very long*

What's left, leave alone. You don't have to be clear of everything to walk into what's next.

**GOLD/PAUSE***   long*

And a question, while it's quiet.

*What**'**s running that you didn**'**t choose?*

**GOLD/PAUSE***   very long*

Not a fault. A pattern doing its job on old instructions — a way of reading a room, a habit of checking, a tone you arrive with before anyone's spoken.

**GOLD/PAUSE***   very long*

If an answer surfaced, leave it where it is. If none did, that's ordinary. This question answers slowly.

**TEAL/BREATH***   in, four*

**TEAL/BREATH***   out, six*

**Rise**

Step outside it.

**BLUE/ILLUSTRATION***   figure at middle distance, mid-threshold, room ahead. Unhurried.*

Someone on their way in. Not in trouble. Breathing four and six before they go.

**GOLD/PAUSE**

Speak to them as *you*.

*You know where you are. Go in with that.*

**GOLD/PAUSE***   long*

**TEAL/BREATH***   in, four*

**TEAL/BREATH***   out, six*

Now — the engagement you named at the start. Bring it back.

**GOLD/PAUSE***   long*

Not how you want it to go. That does nothing for you. Just the first move. Where you'll be. What you'll say first, or what you'll wait for.

**GOLD/PAUSE***   very long*

And notice you get to decide where attention sits going in. That's what this is for.

**TEAL/BREATH***   in, four*

**TEAL/BREATH***   out, six*

One detail already here, before you finish. Not gratitude in general. One specific detail, in the room, now.

**GOLD/PAUSE***   long*

Now put down everything except that.

**GOLD/PAUSE**

**PURPLE/MUSIC***   bed lifts, resolves without a turn.*

Go when you're ready.

You'll have noticed there wasn't much to it. That's the point. This is the one that fits on the way in.

Recognise. Regulate. Release. Rise.

Four moves, on the way through.
