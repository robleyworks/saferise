**SAFERISE**

**Guided Meditation · Recording Scripts**

All three tracks · thirty-one protocols

Word for word. Read as written; do not paraphrase during recording.

Every script runs the four steps in order — Recognition, Regulation, Release, Rise. There is no fifth step.

**MARKERS**

TEAL/BREATH   a breath, counted.   GOLD/PAUSE   silence.   PURPLE/MUSIC   bed cue.   RED/ACTION   a physical instruction, offered and never insisted on.   BLUE/ILLUSTRATION   visual cue for the video edit and the poster; not spoken.   VOICE   register note for the booth; not spoken.

**STANDING RULES FOR THE BOOTH**

The count is four in, six out. There is no hold — the uneven ratio is the mechanism.

Second person throughout. Never I. The distance is what the fourth step rests on.

No durations are stated anywhere, in the scripts or in the edit.

Step headings are the public names. The internal names appear in nothing a member hears.

Six protocols open with movement rather than attention — t1-07, t1-10, t2-08, t2-10, t3-08, t3-09. Those carry a Before we start section and the pace is slower throughout.
