Escalation criteria written by someone licensed before a word is drafted.

## 5 · What gates what

**One person unlocks the most.** A licensed clinician contracted to Kenor is the
single gate on both gendered tracks, Sex & Intimacy, Addiction Recovery, Embodied
Nutrition, clinic pilots, grant eligibility and the investor raise's clinical
summary. **Three names with licence numbers is the highest-leverage ask on the
board**, and it costs a contributor four hours.

| Track | Gate |
|---|---|
| Training & The Body | **None.** Ships on founder time alone |
| Elevation | Nothing external. Needs authoring |
| Executive Presence | Nothing external. Needs authoring |
| Sex & Intimacy | Clinical co-development |
| Women's Track | Clinician + autistic/ADHD reviewers |
| Men's counterpart | Clinician + parity with the women's track |
| Addiction Recovery | Clinical governance + crisis pathways |
| Embodied Nutrition | Clinical review + Training's guardrail pattern |

## 6 · Recommended sequence

1. **Training & The Body.** The only track that ships without anyone's signature.
2. **Elevation and Executive Presence**, in parallel — both prospectus-complete,
   both authoring-only.
3. **Women's Track scoping**, one page, now — free and unblocked, even though
   writing waits on the clinician.
4. **On clinician engagement:** escalation sheets, then Sex & Intimacy and the
   Women's Track.
5. **Then** Money Shift, the men's counterpart, Embodied Nutrition.
6. **Last** Addiction Recovery, once clinical governance is established rather
   than being built for it.

   departure from the neutral positioning and reads differently depending on how
