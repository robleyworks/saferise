# The substrate–capacity model

**Why nutrition and fitness belong in SafeRise, and how tracks map to industries.**
Draft, 13 September 2026.

---

## 1 · The correction

The founding claim said attention governance sits *upstream* of nutrition, fitness and
work. **That is physiologically wrong and it has to be replaced.**

Sleep deprivation reduces prefrontal–amygdala connectivity and sharply raises amygdala
reactivity. That is the regulatory circuit itself degraded — no attentional technique
restores it. Iron, B12 and thiamine deficiency impair attention and working memory
directly. Dehydration around 2% of body mass measurably degrades sustained attention.
Chronic undernutrition lowers heart rate variability, the same vagal tone the protocols
work with.

⚠ **Do not use the acute glucose–willpower model.** Ego-depletion has largely failed
replication. The deficiency evidence is solid; the moment-to-moment glucose story is not.

### The replacement

**Three layers, with the arrow running both ways.**

| Layer | What it is | What it determines |
|---|---|---|
| **Substrate** | Sleep, nutrition, hydration, training load, illness, chronic stress | Whether regulatory capacity is *physically available* |
| **Capacity** | Noticing the state, regulating it, recovering choice | Whether the person can *act rather than react* |
| **Application** | Relationships, work, money, parenting, intimacy | Where the choice actually lands |

**The loop is the point.** Poor sleep degrades capacity. Degraded capacity produces the
11pm scroll and the reach that is not hunger. Those degrade sleep and nutrition further.

**Attention governance is not above nutrition. It is where the loop can be interrupted by
a person rather than by circumstance.**

## 2 · Why this justifies the tracks

This is the argument that turns the roadmap from opportunistic expansion into
completeness.

> SafeRise is not adding nutrition and fitness because they are adjacent markets. It is
> adding them because **without substrate there is no capacity, and the platform would
> otherwise be teaching people to regulate on an empty tank.**

### Embodied Nutrition — substrate

Not a diet product. It works the states *around* eating — the reach that is not hunger,
the hour after, eating in company, the moment control starts to feel safer than appetite.

**Why it belongs:** eating behaviour is the most state-driven of all substrate inputs, and
it is the one where information is least effective. Nobody reaching at 11pm lacks
nutritional knowledge.

⚠ **Gates already on record and unchanged:** no numbers of any kind, disordered-eating
adjacency, clinical review is a hard gate. **New requirement from this model:** the track
must point outward when the problem is deficiency rather than state. Regulation is not a
treatment for anaemia.

### Strength & Return — substrate

Not a training programme. It works lapse shame, the mirror, being watched, the missed
week, rest guilt.

**Why it belongs:** training and rest are substrate inputs, and the barrier is almost never
the training itself — it is walking back in. Encouragement lands as pressure when the
state underneath is shame.

⚠ **On record:** no numbers, targets or programmes. States only.

### Sleep — the gap

**Sleep is the strongest substrate evidence and there is no track for it.**

The states are obvious and unserved: the mind that will not close, the 3am wake, the
dread of the alarm, revenge bedtime procrastination, the Sunday night that belongs to
Monday.

**Recommendation: sleep becomes its own track, or the substrate layer is incomplete.** It
is the single input with the clearest mechanistic link to regulatory capacity, and it
would be the most defensible track on the platform.

## 3 · The full architecture

```
SUBSTRATE          Sleep · Embodied Nutrition · Strength & Return
                   Is the capacity physically available?
                              ↓  ↑
CAPACITY           Personal Transformation
                   Can I notice the state and act rather than from it?
                              ↓  ↑
APPLICATION        Relationship Healing · Professional Performance
                   Executive Presence · Money · Intimacy · Parenting
                   Entrepreneur's Journey · Addiction Recovery
                   Where the recovered choice lands
```

**Elevation sits outside the stack** — it is what becomes available when survival is no
longer taking the whole budget. Keep it separate.

**Personal Transformation is the hinge.** It stays free because it is the layer everything
else on the platform requires. That is now an architectural reason rather than a marketing
one.

## 4 · Industry mapping

Different environments produce different stress signatures. The same thirty-plus protocols
recombine into different bundles, which is what makes an enterprise conversation specific
rather than generic.

### Healthcare and emergency services

**Signature:** shift-disrupted sleep, eating on the run, compassion fatigue, exposure to
outcomes, shame after error.
**Bundle:** Sleep · Embodied Nutrition · Shutdown Recovery · Shame Dissolution · Grief
Integration · Conflict Navigation
**Lead with:** substrate. Circadian disruption is the dominant variable and they know it.

### Finance, legal and professional services

**Signature:** sustained high-stakes exposure, decision density, imposter pressure,
adversarial conversation, long hours displacing training and rest.
**Bundle:** High-Stakes Presence · Decision Fatigue · Imposter Dissolution · Conflict
Navigation · Perfectionism Release · Strength & Return
**Lead with:** judgement arriving on time.

### Technology and engineering

**Signature:** context switching, on-call sleep disruption, long feedback loops,
comparison, burnout normalised as commitment.
**Bundle:** Burnout & Overload · Sleep · Creative Flow · Decision Fatigue · The Belonging
Gap · Career Transition
**Lead with:** attention as the scarce resource, which is a framing this audience accepts
immediately.

### Sales and client-facing

**Signature:** rejection cycling, number pressure, performance visibility, emotional labour.
**Bundle:** Performance Anxiety · Anxiety Reset · Anger Alchemy · Powerlessness & Despair ·
Money Shift
**Lead with:** recovery time between calls.

### Manufacturing, logistics and field operations

**Signature:** shift work, physical load, injury fear, low psychological safety, thin
support culture.
**Bundle:** Sleep · Strength & Return · Shutdown Recovery · Safe Conversation ·
Powerlessness & Despair
**Lead with:** substrate and safety. Avoid therapeutic vocabulary entirely.

### Leadership and people managers — cross-industry

**Signature:** holding others without support, isolation, decisions affecting livelihoods.
**Bundle:** Executive Presence · Carrying What They Told You · Conflict Navigation ·
Decision Fatigue · Burnout & Overload
**Lead with:** nobody is holding it for you.

## 5 · What this changes commercially

**The pitch stops being "wellbeing" and becomes an operating argument:**

> Your people are being asked to perform judgement work on degraded substrate. Shift
> patterns, eating at a desk, training that stopped in March. Then a hard conversation
> lands and there is nothing left to meet it with.
>
> We work all three layers — whether the capacity is physically there, whether they can
> reach it, and what they do with it. Most programmes work only the third.

**Three practical consequences:**

- **The bundle is the deliverable.** A healthcare contract and a finance contract are the
  same platform, different curriculum and different facilitated sessions
- **Substrate tracks are the easiest enterprise sell.** Sleep and eating are legible,
  non-stigmatised and measurable by proxy. They open doors that "emotional regulation"
  does not
- **It justifies the seat model.** Different people in the same organisation need
  different layers; you cannot know which in advance, so everyone gets everything

## 6 · Gates

- **Sleep track** — decision needed on whether it becomes a standalone track
- **Embodied Nutrition** — clinical review is a hard gate; must route outward on
  suspected deficiency
- **Strength & Return** — no numbers, targets or programmes, states only
- **All substrate tracks** — must not imply regulation substitutes for medical
  investigation
- **The founding sentence** — must be corrected wherever it claims attention sits above
  nutrition and fitness. It appears in the method page, the coming-soon page and the
  positioning material
- **Industry bundles** — describe the environment, never diagnose the workforce

## 7 · The sentence to use

> Nothing works well when you are not sleeping or eating. But people are rarely failing
> at nutrition or training for lack of information — they fail in states where the plan
> is unreachable. SafeRise works all three layers: whether the capacity is there, whether
> you can reach it, and what you do with it.

Defensible, narrower than the original, and it survives a physiologist.
