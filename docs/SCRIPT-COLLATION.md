# SafeRise Meditation Scripts — Naming and Collation

**Date:** 18 September 2026
**Purpose:** one canonical list of the 31 sessions, and how the parallel "recording scripts" set maps onto it.

---

## 1. There are two script libraries

**Library A — the recorded library.** The **31 scripts** in `SafeRise_Scripts_ReadingCopy.md` — 30 protocols plus T0-00 The Clearing, which was recovered and filed on 18 September. Reviewed line by line and voiced. Plain register, state-matched breath counts, four steps named as verbs.

**Library B — the "recording scripts" set.** 30 scripts delivered with a MANIFEST on the same day, in a heavier register with an added hum, a gratitude passage, and numbered step markers. **Not recorded.** Four of its files are hybrids carrying Library A text with inserted material.

Library A is canonical. Library B is an archive.

**Fourteen different descriptors** where the protocol has three. That alone makes the two libraries unmixable: the one-word naming is the mechanism of Recognise, and a member who hears *Agitated* in one session and *restricted or braced* in the next is being taught a different protocol each time.

## 3. Audio file naming

The recorded takes in Downloads are named by protocol, not by code. Rename to `T#-##_Name_Part-#.mp3` before assembly; unnamed and loosely named files are how a wrong take reaches a master.

**Still unidentified:** four files named `ElevenLabs_2026-09-18T…`, running 4:15, 4:51, 3:44 and 3:24. One is probably The Clearing.

**Junk to delete:** `shutdown recover vocal2.mp3` (6 seconds) and `Burnout & overload 2.mp3` (41 seconds). Both parent files are complete on their own.

**Two versions to choose between** — these are different voices, not alternative takes: Shame (male / female), Grief, Insecurity, Overwhelm (male / female), Abandonment (plus an "afro" version). Decide the library voice first, then pick takes within it.

**Worth a listen:** `Rupture.mp3` is 3:53 against 6:41 expected — 58% of length, the shortest ratio in the set. Every other single take falls between 70% and 100%. Check it didn't truncate.

## 4. Why Library B was not used

Recorded here for the file, since the two libraries will otherwise keep resurfacing.

- **Register.** "A boundary was crossed... something inside you surged to defend it" against the recorded "Something got crossed. Not a mood, not a bad day — a line, and something went over it." Library B reintroduces the abstraction the review pass removed: *physiological charge*, *sacred boundaries*, *your protective ego*, *your vital life force*.
- **The one-word descriptor breaks.** Fourteen variants across 30 scripts. See the table above.
- **Known defects present in it.** `Clinically,` appears in T3-10, which had been flagged for removal every time it occurred. A stage direction survives at the top of T3-09 Part 1: *(Spoken slowly, evenly, and with zero urgency...)*. `into your breath stream` appears in T1-02, T3-04, T2-03 and T1-09, where the preferred phrasing is *put that where your breath is*.
- **A broken opening line.** T1-06 Part 1 begins *"I'm that today it is so close."* — words missing. Later in the same file: *"Notice that the words simply name the physical state. they don't attempt..."*
- **Audible step numbers.** T1-07, T3-09 and T2-10 carry "Step 1: Recognize" through "Step 4: Rise" as spoken lines. The canonical form is the verbs, woven or standalone.
- **Two closing cues.** Some scripts end "Move your hands and feet," others "Roll your shoulders slowly, letting the tension in your neck loosen. Stretch your arms out." All 31 recorded takes use the first.
- **The Italian persona**, in UNCODED-07 only. It commits the library to an Italian-inflected guide or it sits as an anomaly in one session.
- **Added content that was never approved:** the hum, the gratitude-to-the-sentinel passage, and the evoked warm feeling. Adopting any of them means re-recording all 31.

**What is genuinely good in it, for the record.** T1-03 Overwhelm is the strongest thing in the set — *"Capacity isn't a character trait. It's a quantity. Yours is finite, the same as everyone's"* and *"You are not behind because you are weak. You are behind because there is more than fits."* The shake-the-hands-out discharge is a real somatic idea the recorded version doesn't have. If any part of Library B is ever revisited, start there.

**The one Library B idea already adopted:** naming the resistance in Release. It is in the recorded T1-02, T3-03 and T3-04. The other sessions were signed off without it.

## 5. Do not mix them

If any part of Library B is wanted later, it is a decision to re-record the whole library, not to swap individual sessions. A library where some sessions say *Agitated* and others say *Flooded*, where some hum and some don't, and where some close on "Move your hands and feet" and others on "Roll your shoulders slowly," reads as broken rather than varied — and members move between these in sequence.

## 6. The Clearing

Recovered 18 September and filed as the first entry in the reading copy. 790 words, 7:03 spoken, one block of 4,432 characters.

It sits outside the three-state structure by design — a doorway rather than a protocol. Its own imagery, the jar and the light, appears nowhere else in the library, and it closes by handing the member on: *choose the state you'd like to — Recognise. Regulate. Release. Rise.*

**One consequence for the build.** The proposed four-bar symmetric pattern for song 20 is wrong. The script cues four in and six out, and counts both aloud — *"One. Two. Three. Four… One. Two. Three. Four. Five. Six."* That is the Agitated pattern: five bars, ten-second window, exhale-led. A four-and-four bed would fight a breath the voice is counting out loud, which is the one place a listener cannot miss a mismatch.

Song 20 is 8:00 against a 7:03 vocal — 57 seconds spare, so head-trim rather than loop.
