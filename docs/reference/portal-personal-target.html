Common Questions

Before you start.

The things worth knowing, answered plainly.

Is this therapy?

No. SafeRise is a structured self-guided practice and coaching resource, not psychotherapy, counselling, or medical treatment. Andre is trained in Jungian shadow-work frameworks, somatic coaching, and meditation practice — he is not a licensed therapist or psychiatrist, and nothing here is a diagnosis.

Many people use it alongside therapy. It is designed to give you something practical to do in the moment a pattern shows up, between the sessions where you unpack why it exists.

What if I'm already working with a therapist?

That combination tends to work well, and it's worth telling them you're doing it. Each protocol includes a Disclosure & Support script partly for this — a short way to explain what the practice involves so they can tell you whether it fits what you're working on together.

If your therapist advises against it, follow their guidance. They know your situation; a website doesn't.

Do I need experience with meditation or somatic work?

None. Every protocol is fully guided from start to finish — you're told what to do, when, and for how long. If you've tried meditation and found that being left alone with your thoughts made things worse, this is deliberately not that.

How long does a session take, and what do I need?

Around ten minutes. Headphones if you have them, somewhere you won't be interrupted, and the ability to sit or lie down. Nothing else — no journal to prepare, no prior reading.

There's also a Crisis Card in every protocol: a four-line version for moments when ten minutes isn't realistic.

Which protocol should I start with?

The one that names what you're feeling right now. That's the whole design — you don't need to work out an underlying cause first, and there's no correct order.

If nothing obviously matches, start with the Foundation Protocol. It teaches the four steps in their simplest form, and every other protocol is a variation on it.

What actually comes with a protocol?

A guided audio session, a printable step guide, the science behind the pattern, a reference case, body-based practices for between sessions, journalling prompts, and a crisis card. Some protocols also include an Attention Advisory or an Invitation to Repair where another person is genuinely part of the pattern.

You are not expected to use all of it. Most people use two or three and ignore the rest.

Can I use this while I'm actually in the middle of it?

Yes — that's what the Crisis Card is for, and it's designed to be readable when concentration is poor. The full session is better done once things have settled slightly.

If you're experiencing severe physical symptoms, or symptoms you haven't had before, treat that as a medical question first and speak to a doctor.

Is any of this based on real science?

The core of it rests on peer-reviewed work: Stephen Porges's Polyvagal Theory, the HeartMath Institute's research on heart-rhythm coherence, and Gabor Maté's Compassionate Inquiry. Carl Jung's shadow and individuation frameworks and Joe Dispenza's work on the observer state are included as interpretive layers rather than clinical evidence, and we try to be clear about which is which.

SafeRise has not been through a clinical trial. It is a structured practice built on established frameworks, not a treatment with proven outcomes.

Is my journal private?

Your entries and progress data are yours. They aren't shared, sold, or used to target advertising. Full detail is in the Privacy Policy.

What if it doesn't work for me?

Cancel any time from your account — there's no contract and no cancellation call. If it isn't landing within the first month, we'd rather you stopped than kept paying out of obligation.

Regulation practices are not universal. Some nervous systems respond faster to movement, or to another person in the room, than to a guided sequence alone.

Can I use it with a partner?

Track 01 is intrinsic work — just you and your own system. Relationship Healing is the track built for two people practising together, and it's in development.

What if I'm in crisis right now?

SafeRise is not crisis support and shouldn't be used as a substitute for it. If you're in immediate danger, contact your local emergency number.

If you need to talk to someone now, findahelpline.com lists free, confidential helplines by country. Reaching out there is the right move, and it's not an overreaction.

Scope & Safety

SafeRise is a self-guided practice and coaching resource. It is not therapy, medical treatment, or crisis care, and it does not diagnose any condition. Andre Robley is trained in Jungian shadow-work frameworks, somatic coaching, and meditation practice, and is not a licensed therapist, psychologist, or physician. Nothing on this site replaces advice from a qualified clinician who knows your situation.

If you are in immediate danger, contact your local emergency number. If you are struggling and want to speak to someone now, findahelpline.com lists free, confidential helplines by country. If you are under the care of a doctor or therapist, please talk to them before starting, and follow their guidance over anything you read here.
