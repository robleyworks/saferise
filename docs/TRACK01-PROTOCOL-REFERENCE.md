# SafeRise — Clearing and Track 01 · Protocol Reference

Companion to `MEDITATION-PRODUCTION-HANDOVER.md`. Read this to understand **what each
session is for and how it should sound.** Every voice-register note below comes from the
script itself and is binding.

The bed is selected by **state**, not by protocol. Three beds cover Track 01; Clearing
uses the fourth.

---

## T0-00 · Clearing
**Label: Steady · State: Steady · Frameworks: HeartMath, Kross**

The foundation session, for the day nothing is wrong. That is precisely the point: what
gets built on a calm day is what is available on the day something *is* wrong. It runs
the same four moves as everything else on the platform, without a problem attached.

**Voice:** unhurried, warm, and entirely unworried. Nothing is wrong and the voice knows
it. The closest register to a conversation anywhere in the set.

**Bed:** lighter than the protocols, in from the first word. No breathing cycle —
sustained field.

---

## T1-01 · Anxiety Reset
**Label: Regulate · State: Agitated · Frameworks: Porges, HeartMath**

For anxiety that arrived before any explanation did. The session's premise is the
ordering: body first, explanation afterwards, with the explanation catching up rather
than leading.

**Voice:** steady, unhurried, unbothered. Someone who has seen this many times and is not
alarmed by it. **Explicitly not soothing.**

---

## T1-02 · Anger Alchemy
**Label: Transmute · State: Agitated · Frameworks: Porges, Maté**

For anger, treated as a boundary crossed rather than a mood. **This one does not begin
with settling** — anger does not settle on request, and asking it to is how these
sessions fail. The member is told to keep moving if they are moving.

**Bed:** in late, under the third line. Sits low throughout.

---

## T1-03 · Overwhelm Threshold
**Label: Reduce Load · State: Agitated · Frameworks: Porges, HeartMath**

For too much coming in at once. Opens by asking the member to reduce actual sensory
input before anything else — turn something off, go somewhere quieter. Counters the
state's own argument that there is no time for this.

---

## T1-04 · Abandonment Wound
**Label: Repair · State: Agitated · Frameworks: Porges, Maté**

For the unanswered message, the room that went quiet, the goodbye that landed wrong. The
session explicitly refuses to answer whether the person is being left — that question
stays open. It works on the state that arrived in the not-knowing.

**Voice:** warm and close, present throughout. *The tone is doing something the words are
not* — this register note matters more here than anywhere else in the track.

---

## T1-05 · Shame Dissolution
**Label: Dissolve · State: Unsteady · Frameworks: Maté, Jung**

For shame, whose defining property is that it does not want to be looked at. Goes slowly
and asks nothing to be said out loud, to anyone, ever.

⚠ **Longest script in the track by a wide margin (~1,738 words against a track average
near 850). A trim is pending — do not voice until resolved.**

---

## T1-06 · Grief Integration
**Label: Integrate · State: Unsteady · Frameworks: Maté, Watts**

For the day grief is close rather than distant. Gives explicit permission to stop
partway.

**Voice:** slow, sparse, unafraid of silence. Says less than it wants to. **Sits alongside
rather than comforts.** More pauses than any other protocol in the platform.

**Bed:** from the first word, very low, no turn until the close.

---

## T1-07 · Shutdown Recovery
**Label: Restore · State: Numb · Frameworks: Porges, HeartMath**

For collapse and shutdown. **One of the six movement-opening protocols** — carries a
*Before we start* section and runs slower throughout. Asks for very little; doing only
the first part is stated to be a complete use of it.

**Voice:** low, few words, generous pauses. Offers rather than instructs. Nothing at stake
in getting it right.

---

## T1-08 · Jealousy Release
**Label: Release · State: Unsteady · Frameworks: Jung, Maté**

For jealousy — named as the thing nobody will admit to. Refuses to adjudicate whether
the member is right about what is happening.

**Voice:** frank, unembarrassed, faintly wry. **Treating this as ordinary is the
intervention.** No gentleness, no pity — this is the sharpest register in the track and
softening it would remove the mechanism.

---

## T1-09 · Insecurity Anchor
**Label: Anchor · State: Unsteady · Frameworks: Jung, Kross**

For self-monitoring and the checking loop — am I doing this right, is it working yet.
Opens by taking that off the table. Deliberately withholds reassurance, on the grounds
that reassurance is something this state would argue with.

---

## T1-10 · Powerlessness & Despair
**Label: Reclaim · State: Numb · Frameworks: Porges, Watts**

For situations that will not be different afterwards. **States that plainly in the
opening line** rather than letting the member discover it at the end. Separates the
situation from the weight of carrying it, and works only on the second.

**One of the six movement-opening protocols.**

**Voice:** honest, level, **no brightness whatsoever**. The respect is in refusing to
pretend. Long pauses, asks little.

## What the registers have in common

Four of these sessions explicitly **refuse to resolve the member's actual situation** —
T1-04 (are you being left), T1-08 (are you right), T1-09 (reassurance), T1-10 (will
anything change). That refusal is the design, not a gap. Any production choice that
warms, softens or reassures past what the script says will remove the thing that makes
these work.

Three run **against the instinct of the category**: T1-01 is not soothing, T1-08 is wry
rather than gentle, T1-10 has no brightness at all. If a generated voice take sounds
kind where the note says level, the take is wrong.
