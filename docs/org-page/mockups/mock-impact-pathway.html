The impact pathway

From an internal shift to an organisational signal.

One thing here is trained directly. Everything to the right of it is something to evaluate, in that order, with decreasing certainty at every step.

What SafeRise trains

State recognition and regulation

This is the claim. It is the only part of the chain SafeRise acts on directly, and the only part it will stand behind.

01 · Input

Private, usable support

Available near the moment, without disclosure or completion pressure.

02 · Capability

More room to choose

Earlier recognition, regulation and recovery after pressure.

03 · Behaviour

Better access to skill

Pause, judgement, communication, repair and return.

04 · Signal

Workplace indicators

Patterns a team can assess ethically, at an aggregate level.

The hatching is not decoration. Stages three and four are areas to evaluate, not outcomes SafeRise claims, and the page should not let them look like the same kind of statement as stage one.

Where a signal would show up.

Five areas an organisation can assess without looking at any individual.
IMG · DECISION
Decision quality

Judgement under pressure

Can people create enough pause to tell urgency from importance, and respond rather than react?

  • Decision confidence
  • Avoidable rework
  • Escalation quality
IMG · SUPPORT
People experience

Sense of support

Does support feel private, relevant and usable before strain becomes a crisis?

  • Perceived support
  • Psychological safety
  • Help-seeking confidence
IMG · RECOVERY
Sustainable output

Performance and recovery

Can people regain useful attention after disruption and re-enter the work with more choice?

  • Recovery time
  • Focus quality
  • Error and rework patterns
IMG · STABILITY
Workforce stability

Capacity to stay

Does the environment become more supportable when pressure can be met without silence or stigma?

  • Intent to stay
  • Regretted attrition
  • Absence patterns
IMG · REPAIR
Relational health

Communication and repair

Can a person notice activation before it becomes the entire conversation?

  • Conflict recurrence
  • Manager confidence
  • Team climate

Measure the environment, not the individual. Use the aggregate people and performance measures already in place, optional anonymous pulse questions, and qualitative learning. Private protocol use does not become a productivity score, and named activity is never exposed.
