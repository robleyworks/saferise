1 · Data, scope & trust

Data, scope & trust

The boundary is part of the product.

A nervous-system resource cannot be credible at work if using it creates a second risk: being watched, scored or interpreted by the employer.

No individual reporting

No names, session history or activity supplied to leadership. Not their usage, not whether they opened anything. Not to you, and not on request.

Journals remain private

What somebody writes is not an organisational record.

No scores or streaks

No leaderboard, completion pressure or wellbeing metric.

No diagnosis

SafeRise does not screen, label or assess anybody.

Not treatment or crisis care

It sits alongside professional support and routes outward when needed.

No outcomes invented

Results are not claimed before they are measured.

Good fit

A recurring pressure, leadership willing to protect private use, and a live introduction people can return to.

Not a fit

A motivational keynote only, named reporting on behaviour, or a need for clinical or emergency support.

2 · The operating layer

The operating layer

Training only works when people can reach it.

Frameworks assume attention is available. SafeRise works one layer earlier: the state a person is in while the moment is happening.

01

Recognise

Name the state before it takes over.

02

Regulate

Breath, attention and somatic tools, matched to the state.

03

Recover choice

Return with room to judge what is really there.

Pressure doesn’t disappear. This is a repeatable way to meet it, without the most activated part of you making every decision.

3 · The impact pathway

The impact pathway

From an internal shift to an organisational signal.

SafeRise supports state recognition and regulation. What follows is a pathway to evaluate, not a promise of outcomes.

01 · Input

Private support

Near the moment, without disclosure or pressure.

02 · Capability

More room to choose

Earlier recognition, regulation and recovery.

03 · Behaviour

Better access to skill

Pause. Judge. Communicate. Repair. Return.

04 · Signal

Workplace patterns

Reviewed ethically, at an aggregate level.

Directly supported
Reasonably expected
Observed
Not promised
Areas teams explore

Decision quality

Can people pause and respond, rather than react?

Perceived support

Does support feel usable before strain becomes damage?

Recovery capacity

Do people return to steadiness between demands?

Workforce stability

Do patterns hold across a demanding period?

Relational health

Are repair and candour available under pressure?

Measure the environment, not the individual. Use existing aggregate people and performance measures, optional anonymous pulse questions and qualitative learning. Do not turn private protocol use into a productivity score or expose named activity.
