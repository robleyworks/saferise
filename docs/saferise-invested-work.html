What has gone in, and what it is worth.

A continuous record of the work invested in SafeRise Protocol, by lane, at market contractor rates. Hours are derived from measured output, not recalled from memory. Edit any figure and it saves to this browser.

How to read this. This measures replacement cost — what it would cost to commission this work from contractors today. It is not a company valuation, and it is not a claim about market value. Founder time carries no invoice; this is the number that would have appeared on one. Every rate is editable because every rate is arguable.
