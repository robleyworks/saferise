# SafeRise image production manifest

Production brief: 14 September 2026 image inventory plus founder constraints.

## Global casting exclusions

Do not repeat any approved subject's face, hairstyle, clothing, pose, or prominent prop.
Across the set: no coffee cups, books, beige linen, oatmeal clothing, generic suits,
lotus poses, yoga mats, candles, sunrise-over-water, beaches, or minimalist-wellness
styling. People should be 27–37, Caribbean and European across the set, lively,
contemporary, upper-middle-class in register. No more than two closed-eye subjects.
