# SafeRise — Resource Architecture, Three New Tracks

**8 September 2026.**

Covers Sex & Intimacy, Elevation Series, and the Professional Performance pair
(built here as Leadership — **provisional, not confirmed**).

---

## 1 · The principle

The **nine universal resources stay at nine** across every track, forever. They
are the platform's spine and the reason a member who learns one track can use
any other without relearning the furniture.

**Conditional resources grow per track.** This is already how the platform
works — Raising It exists in Track 03 only. Each new track may add conditionals
specific to its domain. Nothing about that breaks the manifest, because the
manifest was never a fixed twelve; it is nine plus whatever the domain needs.

Current conditionals: Proximity Guide (14), Invitation to Repair (12),
Raising It (10). Six are proposed below.

**A track's conditionals must earn their slot** by covering something no
universal resource does. If it could be a section inside How This Works or Safe
Practice, it is not a resource.

## 2 · Sex & Intimacy

Adopting the ten protocols in the working prospectus as written. Two
corrections to its resource section.

### Correction 1 — Safe Practice is missing and it is the one this track needs most

The prospectus's §13 sets out referral thresholds, "no cause claims", "medical
issues stay medical", and adults-only provisioning. **Safe Practice is the
resource that carries exactly that**, and it is absent from the prospectus's
architecture. It must be present on all ten protocols, and it does more work on
09 and 10 than anywhere else on the platform.

### Correction 2 — the renames are platform-wide, not track-local

The prospectus proposes Journal for Your Record, Cue Card – 4-Line Reset for
Cue Card, and treats The Chosen Self and Protocol Guide as resources. Your
Record and Cue Card exist in 30 and 31 protocols respectively; The Chosen Self
is a dashboard record item at `/record/chosen-self`, not a resource. Renaming
is legitimate but it is a 30-protocol change, and it should be decided as one
rather than arriving as a side effect of this track.

### New conditionals — both from the prospectus, adopted

**Desire Check.** Clarifies what is actually present before deciding what it
means — enjoying imagining it, curious about it, might try once, want it
ongoing, feel pressure to want it, once wanted it, don't yet understand it,
or uncertainty itself. Stops at clarity; does not decide, does not negotiate.

**Consent & Boundaries.** Willingness, limits, existing agreements, changing
one's mind, stopping midway, pressure versus willingness, power differences,
capacity, privacy, aftercare, revisiting afterwards. Answers *can this happen
responsibly*, where The Decision answers *do I want to stand behind it*.

## 3 · Elevation Series

Ten protocols. **Eligibility gating removed** — see §5 for what that changes.

### New conditionals

**Others in Your Material.** The ethical problem this track creates and nothing
else covers: a member documenting and translating lived experience is handling
other people's stories without them present. Covers whose story is yours to
tell, the difference between your experience of someone and an account of them,
anonymisation that actually anonymises, when asking is itself an imposition,
what changes on publication, and material involving someone who cannot consent.
Elevation's structural equivalent of Consent & Boundaries.

**Scope of Claim.** What you can honestly say you know against what you are
extrapolating from one life. Sample size of one, survivorship in your own
story, *this worked for me* versus *this works*, conditions you did not
control, and how to state a claim so it stays true. Not modesty training —
overclaiming and underclaiming are both scope failures. It is also SafeRise
practising its own doctrine: a platform that refuses outcome promises cannot
teach members to make them.

**Safe Practice on 01 is load-bearing.** Flatness after achievement and
clinical low mood present almost identically. That resource has to name the
difference and point outward.

**Proximity Guide has no purchase in this track.** No protocol needs it. That
is fine — conditionals are conditional.

## 4 · Leadership · provisional

The pair for Professional Performance: from managing your own state under
observation to being responsible for other people's. Grounded in Philosophy
§11 — a dysregulated parent affects a household, a fearful manager affects an
organisation.

Protocol list is a sketch pending your confirmation of the track itself.

### New conditionals

**The Hard Message.** Delivering something that costs the person hearing it —
feedback, a decision, a no. The structural mirror of Raising It: Raising It is
naming something to someone with power over you, this is naming it while
holding the power. The asymmetry runs the other way and so does the state.

**When It's Not Yours to Hold.** Recognising that someone you are responsible
for needs more than you can give, and routing them rather than absorbing them.
Referral thresholds, the limits of a manager's role, and what happens to your
own state when you carry someone else's.

This second one is the most safety-relevant resource proposed in this document.
A leadership track without it teaches people to take responsibility for others'
states with no boundary on that responsibility, which is how managers end up
doing untrained clinical work on their reports.

## 5 · What removing Elevation's eligibility gate changes

Removing it **resolves the blocker** I flagged: qualification had to be
computed from a member record that lives only on the member's device and never
reaches the platform. With no gate, nothing needs computing.

Four things follow and all are copy changes:

1. `saferise-plans-v1.html` — the button reading *Not yet available to you*,
   and the line *There is nothing to apply for — you will be told when you
   qualify*, both become untrue.
2. `saferise-dashboard-v41.html` — *Seven protocols for people who have
   finished the foundational work* is now wrong on both the count and the
   condition.
3. `tracks.js` — `LIFE_LAB.extension` carries the comment *Not shown on member
   surfaces before someone has run the first eight*. Decide whether that
   restriction survives; it is a display rule, not an access rule, so it can.
4. The positioning still needs to say Elevation is **later, not higher** —
   otherwise removing the gate turns a sequence into a hierarchy, and members
   who never open it are made to feel behind.

`visible:false` is unaffected. Ungated is not the same as launched.

## 6 · Resource count after all three tracks

Nine universal, unchanged and permanent.

Nine conditional: Proximity Guide, Invitation to Repair, Raising It, Desire
Check, Consent & Boundaries, Others in Your Material, Scope of Claim, The Hard
Message, When It's Not Yours to Hold.

No protocol carries all of them. Per-protocol counts land between nine and
twelve, which is what the existing store already does. **Derive the set from
the content, never from a typed map** — that rule is what makes adding six
conditionals cost nothing structurally.
