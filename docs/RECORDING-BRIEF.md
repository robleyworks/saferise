# Recording sprint — brief

**Eleven files · 10,155 words · 97 minutes of finished audio · ~6.4 hours all in**

Clearing plus Track 01. Read section 1 before you open the mic — four items there
are cheaper to fix now than to re-record.

---

## 1 · Fix before you record

### ⚠ Five scripts contain spoken durations

Durations are prohibited platform-wide. Every one of these is a re-record if it
goes to tape:

| Script | Line |
|---|---|
| `t0-00` Clearing | *"That readiness you found **a minute ago**"* |
| `t1-02` Anger Alchemy | *"how these things fail in the **first ten seconds**"* |
| `t1-03` Overwhelm | *"nothing you could do about any of it in the **next minute**"* |
| `t1-04` Abandonment | *"It'll still be there in **a few minutes**"* |
| `t1-05` Shame Dissolution | *"you did that **a few minutes ago**, at the start"* |

Replacements that keep the sense and lose the clock: *a moment ago* · *right at
the start* · *early on* · *in the first breath or two* · *it will still be there
afterwards*.

**Not every hit is a defect.** *"It takes a second"* and *"a second thing"* in
`t1-03`, `t1-04` and `t1-05` are idiomatic, not durations. Leave them.

### ⚠ `t0-00` uses "practising"

*"one thing worth **practising** here rather than in the middle of something."*
Prohibited as a product word. Suggested: *"one thing worth getting familiar with
here"*.

### ⚠ The loudness target is unresolved

`BED-RHYTHM-SPEC.md` flags it and it is still open. **−19 LUFS mono / −1 dBTP**
is the standing platform rule; the sprint chain says **−16**. They disagree by 3
LU. If the meditations are mono, one is wrong — and getting it wrong is eleven
re-renders. **Decide before the first master, not after.**

### ⚠ No script carries a single pause marker

Zero across all eleven. That means 97 minutes of pacing invented live, and it is
the largest single risk to consistency across the set. Two options:

- **Mark them first.** An hour with a pencil, notating the breath points and the
  long holds. Slower today, identical across eleven files.
- **Set pace by ear against a timer.** Faster, and the files will vary.

Recommendation: mark `t0-00` and `t1-01` only. Once the pace is in your body the
remaining nine follow, and you have two reference files to check the others
against.

**Three sessions, not one.** Vocal fatigue in this delivery shows as a pitch
drift you will not hear until playback.

**Session one — Clearing, Anxiety Reset, Anger Alchemy.** ~90 min. Clearing first
because it is shortest and least loaded; it is the warm-up that sets level and
pace before anything that matters.

**Session two, fresh — Shame Dissolution, Overwhelm, Insecurity, Abandonment.**
~2 hrs 15. Shame Dissolution first: at 1,580 words it is half again the next
longest and the hardest to hold tone through.

**Session three — Jealousy, Powerlessness, Shutdown, Grief.** ~80 min. The four
shortest, deliberately last.

## 3 · At the mic

**Room tone, thirty seconds, at the top of every session.** You need it for the
head and tail of each file and it has to match that room, that day. Three sessions
means three room-tone takes, not one.

**105 words a minute.** Noticeably slower than reading aloud. Time Clearing
against a stopwatch — if it lands under seven minutes you are rushing, and
correcting on file one is free.

**Five minutes between scripts.** Water, no talking. The gap is doing real work.

**Do not push through a bad take.** Restart the paragraph. A patched breath is
audible in a way a patched word is not.

## 4 · The beds

`BED-RHYTHM-SPEC.md` is still marked **proposal, awaiting your decision.** It
needs to become a rule before eleven files are built against it.

| State | Bars | Pattern | Rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Agitated | 5 | 4s in / 6s out | 6 breaths/min |
| Unsteady | 6 | 6s / 6s | 5 breaths/min |
| Numb | 4 | 4s / 4s | 7.5 breaths/min |

**Clearing has no bed.** Its state is Steady and the spec defines three beds for
Agitated, Unsteady and Numb. Either Steady takes the Unsteady bed, or it gets its
own. That is a decision, and Clearing is the first file you record.

**Generate, then measure.** ElevenLabs Music will not reliably hit a phrase
length on request. Import the candidate, read the amplitude envelope, confirm the
swell period against the target within half a second, and check the loop point
for an audible seam. **Reject and regenerate rather than adjusting the spec to
match what came back** — that is the whole reason the document exists.

**Mix:** bed sits roughly 18 dB under the voice, measured. Keep bed content clear
at 100–250 Hz where the voice fundamental lives, and soft at 2–4 kHz where
consonants are defined.

---

## 5 · After

**Master all eleven in one batch**, same chain, same session — so loudness across
the set is consistent rather than drifting file to file.

Two-pass EBU R128, target per section 1 once resolved, −1 dBTP ceiling.

**Then listen to all eleven back to back.** Not for defects — for whether they
sound like one person on one day. That is the only check that catches the thing
nobody notices until a member does.

---

## 6 · One thing to think about, not to act on

**The set runs 5 to 15 minutes — a threefold spread.** A member moving from Grief
Integration to Shame Dissolution meets a session three times longer with no
warning, and nothing on the page tells them.

Not a recording problem and not a reason to delay. But if the spread ever
narrows, `t1-05` is the one to look at, and it is easier to decide that before it
is recorded than after.
