## 1 · The one that matters most

**The Investor Teaser leads with the positioning your own strategy document
tells you to abandon.**

It opens:

> *"Coaching, available in the moment that matters. Subscription-based,
> on-demand coaching-as-a-service…"*

`docs/business/POSITIONING.md` §8 is unambiguous:

> **Take Position A. Keep the prices. Change the words.** You are not selling
> coaching. You are selling the layer that everything else assumes and nobody
> provides.

And §2 sets out precisely why the label fails:

- **Coaching implies a person.** The core is self-guided audio. A buyer hearing
  *coaching as a service* expects humans and finds a library.
- **The price contradicts the label.** The category runs €1,000–3,000 per seat
  per year because a person is doing hours of work. SafeRise is €180. *"A buyer
  who knows the category reads €15 a seat as: this is not actually coaching."*

Carolina, or anyone who knows the category, reads the Teaser and prices SafeRise
against BetterUp. **That is the worst possible first frame.**

The same word appears in the Personal Transformation one-pager —
*"a free, complete state-specific coaching experience"*.

**Fix:** Position A language throughout. *SafeRise is the regulation and
attention layer that makes everything else land.* The sentence that already
works for both audiences:

> When someone is in a triggered state, everything else you have given them is
> unreachable. SafeRise is what gets it back.
