A finished product in an unconsolidated market, looking for reach.

SafeRise teaches people to recognise the state they are in, regulate it, and recover the capacity to choose what happens next — on their own, in the moment, without a clinician in the room. Thirty protocols are built and the platform enters beta on 30 September 2026.

The method — one sequence, every protocol

RecogniseBring the experience into view, without diagnosis
RegulateEnough steadiness to regain command of attention
ReleaseLoosen struggle and the demand to resolve it now
RiseReturn to deliberate participation and caring action

The same four steps run in every protocol, on every track. That consistency is the product. Beneath it sits a three-layer model — capacity, the substrate it depends on (sleep, nutrition, training load), and the application where it lands. Most wellbeing spend touches only the third.

Why it survives scrutiny

  • Six frameworks, tiered by evidence. Porges, HeartMath and Kross lead and stand independently; Maté, Jung and Watts are labelled clinical-practice or interpretive and never presented as mechanism.
  • Clinical governance is a gate, not a footnote. Four tracks stay unbuilt until a licensed clinician is contracted.
  • Defined by refusal. Not therapy, not a course, not gamified self-improvement, not an outcome promise.
  • Privacy is structural. The organisation funds access and never sees what happens inside an account.
