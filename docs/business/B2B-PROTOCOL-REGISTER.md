# SafeRise B2B — protocol register, page-ready copy

**Single source for `/organisations`, the protocol catalogue and the B2B PDFs.**
Written 22 September from the approved handover and the v6 Executive Summary
and Programme Breakdown. Where the v6 documents already carry approved wording,
it is reproduced verbatim rather than rewritten.

Structure per protocol: **name · anchor · headline · who it serves · what the
work repeatedly asks**. The headline is the one line that carries the card; the
rest is the supporting copy beneath it.

---

## The rule that governs every entry

> If a resource still works almost unchanged after the role or industry name is
> replaced, it belongs in the Foundation 8. B2B content exists only where the
> work context genuinely changes the experience.

No Role or Industry protocol re-teaches anxiety, shame, conflict, sleep,
leadership, nutrition or burnout. It names the occupational pattern, gives
context-specific regulation, and routes the deeper work to the Foundation track
that already holds it.

**A note on what this replaces.** The live page currently offers each industry
"a focused route of three to five protocols" assembled from the B2C library.
That is the architecture the handover retires: one Industry protocol, one Role
protocol, eight Foundation tracks. The "three to five" framing must come out.

## Section copy — the 8 + 2 model

**Eyebrow** · THE 8 + 2 MODEL
**Headline** · One foundation. Two layers of context.

The person is not the job. The role is not the industry. SafeRise separates the
three so personalisation comes from context, not from duplicating the same
content under different names.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Role / function** | *What does this responsibility repeatedly ask me to carry?* Responsibility, cognitive load, authority, scrutiny, rejection, precision, vigilance, dependency, performance cycles and repeated interpersonal demand. |
| **Industry** | *What does this work environment repeatedly expose me to?* Operational rhythm, service pressure, public contact, emergency exposure, safety consciousness, sensory demand, shifts, disruption and competition. |

How it combines — four worked examples, kept short:

- Resort accountant · Financial Focus + Hospitality
- Audit-firm accountant · Financial Focus + Legal Services
- Professional athlete · Athletic Performance + Sport Performance
- Football coach · Teaching Impact + Sport Performance

The same role in two environments is two different jobs. That is the whole
argument for the second layer, and it is worth making explicitly on the page.

## What must not appear on the page

From the handover's positioning boundary, and worth holding hard because it is
the difference between a credible enterprise product and a liability:

SafeRise never implies that regulation solves structural problems. Not that
breathing fixes understaffing, that regulation fixes harassment, that resilience
removes unsafe conditions, or that self-guided practice replaces treatment. The
approved position is: **help organisations improve the system; help people
navigate themselves while they are inside it.**

Outcome language stays hedged — supports, may help, creates conditions for,
complements, improves access to. Never "increases retention" or "reduces
absence" without evidence behind it.

Privacy is a selling point and should be stated plainly: **the organisation
creates the conditions and funds access; the individual owns what happens inside
their account.** Employers see aggregate, never journal entries, personal
circumstances or individual protocol choices. SafeRise must not become emotional
surveillance.
