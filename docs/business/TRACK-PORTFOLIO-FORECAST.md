## The distinction that matters

| | **Industry track** | **Department track** |
|---|---|---|
| Sold to | An employer in that sector | A function inside any employer |
| Example | Contact Centre, Hospitality, Clinical | Sales, Engineering, Legal, Finance |
| Addressable base | Fewer companies, larger seat counts | **Every company has one** |
| Budget source | HR or Operations | **The department's own budget** |
| Buyer | HR Director, Site Director | VP Sales, VP Engineering, GC |

### Department tracks bypass HR entirely

This is the strategic point and it was under-explored until now.

**A VP Sales buys a Sales track out of enablement budget without ever touching
wellbeing procurement.** Sales enablement budgets are routinely larger than HR
wellbeing budgets, the buyer is a single decision-maker rather than a committee,
and the cycle is weeks rather than quarters.

It also creates a second, independent land-and-expand path: sell one department,
prove it, expand to the population licence through the same account.

## The three that should be built first

### 1 · Sales & Quota — the standout

**You have authority here that literally nobody else in wellbeing has.**

Quota pressure, rejection absorbed daily, public performance ranking, month-end,
the specific dread of a forecast call. You lived it for a decade and carried a
number the whole time. A wellness founder writing this track would get it wrong
in the first paragraph and every salesperson reading it would know.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Buyer** | VP Sales, CRO, Head of Enablement |
| **Budget** | Sales enablement — larger than HR wellbeing, and discretionary |
| **Cycle** | Weeks. One decision-maker |
| **Trigger** | Quota reset, missed quarter, high SDR attrition, new fiscal year |
| **Content overlap** | Performance Anxiety, Imposter Dissolution, Conflict Navigation, Belonging Gap already exist and transfer |

### 2 · Contact Centre — the largest seat counts

Attrition is measured monthly, costed per head, and already funded. Tens of
thousands of seats in Jamaica and the Dominican Republic within your own region.

### 3 · Executive Presence — already scoped, highest ACV

Prospectus exists, 65% content reuse, and leadership development budgets are the
largest and least questioned in any organisation. **Cheapest build in the top
three and the highest ACV.**

## HR & People Teams — build it for a different reason

Ranks seventh on revenue and it is not a revenue play.

**It is the buying team.** Give the People function their own track, free, before
any licence conversation. They experience the product on their own difficulty —
absorbing everyone else's distress with nobody supporting them, which is a real
and unaddressed condition — rather than evaluating it on someone else's behalf.

That is a better demo than any deck, and it costs €6,695 once.

**Recommend: build in Wave 3, deploy free to every live prospect's People team.**

## ⚠ The assumption everything rests on

**Portfolio payback of 1.5 months of mature revenue is a suspiciously good
number, and it should be treated as unproven.**

Every figure above depends on one untested claim: **that a vertical track
actually closes at €14,000–18,000.** No vertical track exists. No vertical
licence has been sold. The ACV uplift from €6,000 generic to €15,000 specific is
a reasoned estimate, not an observation.

**Build one. Sell it. Then build the wave.**

If Sales & Quota closes three licences at €14,000, the model holds and Wave 1
proceeds with confidence. If it closes at €7,000, every number here halves and
the build order should change — department tracks would still work, but the
portfolio would be a slower, smaller business than this table implies.

Committing €103,090 and two years of build capacity against an untested ACV is
the one genuine mistake available here.

## Content reuse — where the cost actually sits

| Reuse | Tracks | Implication |
|---|---|---|
| **65–70%** | Customer Support, Executive Presence, HR Teams | Adaptation of existing protocols. Fastest and cheapest |
| **50–60%** | Sales, Contact Centre, Hospitality, Legal, Finance | Substantial new writing on a familiar spine |
| **40–45%** | Engineering, Shift Work, Clinical, Field Service | Mostly new. Domain research required |
| **30%** | First Responders | Almost entirely new, plus clinical review |

**Build high-reuse tracks first where scores are close.** Executive Presence at
65% reuse and €18,000 ACV is the best cost-to-return ratio in the portfolio.

## Held behind clinical governance

**Clinical Staff** and **First Responders** both require a licensed clinician
before drafting begins, not before shipping.

First Responders in particular has the highest trauma exposure of any track in
the portfolio and the lowest content reuse. It ranks last on score partly for
that reason. Its resonance is real — but it is the track most likely to cause
harm if built under commercial pressure with an empty clinical layer.

○ Neither enters the build schedule until a licensed clinician is contracted.
