## What it is

Nervous-system regulation and attention infrastructure. Not an EAP, not coaching,
not training. The layer your training, your coaching panel and your assistance
programme all quietly assume people already have.

An organisation with existing support is not being asked to replace any of it.

## What one unregulated hour costs, eleven months later

Two people on the same team stopped speaking properly in March. Nobody escalated
it, because there was nothing to escalate. No incident. No policy broken.

By May the rota was being built around them. By July one had resigned and the
other was on a second period of sick leave, and replacing the first took eleven
weeks and a recruitment fee. In August a guest got the end of that chain and left
the review that is still the first thing anyone reads about you.

**Nobody in the building could tell you where it started.**

## Why existing provision does not reach it

An assistance programme waits for someone to make the request, then routes them
to a stranger. The solution arrives as exposure — a webinar, a portal, a library
nobody opens.

Adult nervous systems do not reorganise from exposure. They reorganise under
attention, repeated.

## What every employee receives — the 8 + 2 model

**The Foundation 8** — the human work that travels with a person from one role,
team or industry to another.

**Plus two context layers** — one protocol built for what they do, one for where
they do it. A nurse on a ward, a teacher at three in the afternoon and a
front-desk supervisor in a resort do not have the same bad hour, and should not be
handed the same session.

Every employee. From the first day. No referral, no waiting list, no opening
hours, and no limit on how many need it at once.

## The privacy position, stated plainly

> **The organisation creates the conditions and funds access. The individual owns
> what happens inside their account.**

Measure the environment, not the individual. Existing aggregate people and
performance data only — nothing inside a member's account is reportable, and
nothing is built to make it so.
