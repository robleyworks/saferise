# Gendered Track Programme — Working Model

**Draft for Andre Robley · 7 September 2026**
Covers the women's track (Track 09, working title), the collaboration boundary
with Kirsten Morris, and the minimum clinical provision required before either
gendered track ships.

*Placed in the knowledge base 8 September 2026.*

---

## 1 · The principle

The founder holds architecture and voice. The collaborator holds substantiation
and sourcing. The clinician holds safety.

These three do not overlap, and none can be done by the other two. Most of what
follows is an attempt to keep them separate while spending as little money as
possible.

Two constraints shape everything. **Cash is short**, so unpaid and asynchronous
work is preferred wherever it does not compromise safety. **Kirsten must never
sit on the critical path** of anything with a date attached.

---

## 2 · The four roles

### Founder — Andre
Roadmap, track themes, protocol selection, resource architecture, brand voice,
all drafting, final publication.

Everything upstream of substantiation stays here — the largest share of the work,
costing nothing but time, and the part that cannot be delegated without the track
ceasing to sound like SafeRise.

### Collaborator — Kirsten Morris
Research sourcing, clinician recruitment, population accuracy review, grant and
funding routes.

**Not:** clinical sign-off, contraindication authorship, safety review, or public
listing as a clinical figure. She is unlicensed and pre-licensure in Florida with
no registration number, and has scoped herself accurately as a therapeutic coach.
The model must not stretch her past that.

Strengths, in order of value:
1. **Access to a licensed network** — the shortest path to the clinician the
   programme is gated on.
2. **Nonprofit management master's** — grant strategy and eligibility.
3. **Live population knowledge** — she can tell you when copy will not land.

**Load ceiling: roughly four hours a month, asynchronous, nothing
time-critical.** If any task needs her within a fixed window, restructure it.

### Clinician — unfilled, and the gate on everything
Licensed, current, verifiable in a public register. Scope covering women's
health, perimenopause or trauma. Contracted directly to Kenor International B.V.
Paid, bounded, defined.

Writes and signs the escalation and exclusion criteria. Reviews the outline and
one written protocol. Signs a final scope statement. Nothing else.

**Contracted to Kenor, not to Kirsten.** If the relationship runs entirely
through her it dissolves the moment her health or priorities shift, and there is
no clinical layer again.

### Lived-experience readers — two per population
Unpaid or nominal. They read for whether it lands, not for safety. This is what
stops a track reading as written *about* a group rather than *for* them, and it
matters most for the neurodivergent material.

## 3 · The workflow

### Stage 1 — Scoping *(Andre, alone)*
Track theme, ten protocol titles, the state each addresses, the population each
serves. One page. No cost, no dependency.

### Stage 2 — Escalation sheet *(Andre drafts, clinician signs)*
**Before any protocol is written.** One page per protocol theme: which symptoms
mean stop and see a doctor, which conditions mean this protocol is wrong for
someone, what the copy must never imply.

The single most important change to the sequence. Safety boundaries change what a
protocol *is*. Discovered after a significant build they cause rewrites rather
than annotations — on ten protocols that is the difference between a fortnight
and a quarter.

The clinician's first paid task is reviewing ten pages, not 15,000 words. **The
cheapest possible way to buy a clinician's signature**, and it makes subsequent
asks smaller.

Known escalation territory: thyroid disease, cardiac symptoms, postpartum
psychosis, major depression, and any medical dismissal that could keep someone
from care they need.

### Stage 3 — Outline plus one *(Andre)*
The full ten-protocol outline, plus **one protocol written end to end** at
production depth — every resource, the full four steps, the meditation script.

Handover happens here, not at significant build. By the time material feels
substantial it is fixed. A reviewer arriving then can only approve or cause
expensive damage, and most choose approve. That yields sign-off without scrutiny,
which is worse than no review because it looks like the good outcome.

### Stage 4 — Substantiation *(Kirsten, asynchronous)*
- **Research base.** Peer-reviewed sources with dates and DOIs. Perimenopause
  autonomic literature is newer and thinner than polyvagal work and it moves.
  Anything without a source does not ship.
- **Clinician candidates.** Three names, with licence numbers.
- **Population read.** Does it land, or read as written by an outsider.

### Stage 5 — Clinical review *(clinician, paid)*
Reads the outline and the one written protocol against the escalation sheet they
already signed. Confirms or amends. Signs a scope statement Kenor can cite.

**Two to four hours of consultancy time.** The only unavoidable spend.

### Stage 6 — Build and ship *(Andre)*
Remaining nine protocols written against approved boundaries. Lived-experience
read on the finished set. Publication.

## 4 · What this costs

| Item | Cost | When |
|---|---|---|
| Stages 1, 3, 6 | Founder time only | Ongoing |
| Kirsten, Stage 4 | Unpaid or equity-deferred, ~4 hrs/month | Async |
| Escalation sheet review | 1–2 hrs clinical time | Stage 2 |
| Clinical review | 2–4 hrs clinical time | Stage 5 |
| Lived-experience readers | Free protocol access | Stage 6 |
| Legal review of gendered positioning | Fold into existing counsel | Before ship |

**Total unavoidable cash: 3–6 hours of licensed clinical time per track.**

The escalation-sheet ordering is what makes this possible — reviewing ten pages
costs a fraction of reviewing a finished track, and buys the same signature.

Women's health and neurodivergence both attract funding; a small grant would
cover the clinical time outright.

## 5 · The boundary document

Write this before she starts. One page, an hour's work.

**Andre decides:** track themes and roadmap · protocol selection · resource
architecture · brand voice and copy · what ships and when · all commercial terms.

**Kirsten delivers:** research sourcing with citations · clinician candidates ·
population accuracy review · grant and funding routes.

**Kirsten does not:** sign off clinically · author contraindications · appear as a
clinical figure on the site · hold the clinician relationship contractually.

**Named on the site as:** contributor and research lead. **BA Psychology, Clark
Atlanta University. MA Human Services and Nonprofit Management. Certified in
Functional Family Therapy.**

⚠ **Not "Functional Family Therapist" on any public surface.** The word is a
model designation, not a licence, and using it as a title for someone unlicensed
in Florida edges toward protected-title territory.

**Compensation:** revenue share on the track, deferred, or a grant-funded
stipend. Agreed in writing before work starts.

This exists because two people have already turned out to be scoped wrong. It is
not distrust; it is what stops a third.

## 6 · The male counterpart

The same structure, symmetrically. Dwight occupies the position Kirsten does —
contributor with real domain material, not clinical authority. Same boundary
document, same public-description discipline.

⚠ **The clinical standard must be identical on both sides.** Both tracks wait for
their clinician, or neither does.

Men's presenting states need their own scoping rather than a mirror: anger as the
permitted emotion, help-seeking suppression, status and provision threat,
physical symptom presentation, isolation after separation.

## 7 · Sequencing

Track 08 — Training and The Body — was identified as the next build precisely
because it has no clinical or external dependencies. This track is its inverse:
maximum clinical dependency, maximum contraindication load.

1. **Now.** Boundary document. Kirsten's brief. The single ask: three clinician
   names.
2. **Now, parallel.** Track 09 scoping, one page. Free, no dependency.
3. **While waiting on the clinician.** Build Track 08.
4. **On clinician engagement.** Escalation sheet, then outline plus one, then
   Stage 5.
5. **After the women's track is proven.** Male counterpart, same model.

The clinician is the gate on both gendered tracks, clinic pilots, grant
eligibility and the investor raise's clinical summary. **Recruiting one person
unlocks all of it.** The most valuable thing Kirsten could do this month is
produce three names — and that ask costs nothing and fits inside four hours.
