## What it is

A self-guided nervous-system regulation platform. Structured protocols, named for
the moment rather than the method, that a person opens when anxiety, anger,
overwhelm, conflict or pressure has started making the decisions.

It is not therapy, not an EAP, not a meditation app and not coaching. It is the
layer underneath all of them — the capability every one of those assumes a person
already has.

## What it is actually costing people

Not in theory. In the week they have just had.

- They stopped putting themselves forward, because putting yourself forward means
  being looked at. Three people they trained have been promoted past them.
- Four years ago there was an argument neither party can now remember the subject
  of. Everything since has been about that argument, and the children have learned
  to leave the room at a particular volume.
- Every task opens six more and none of them close, so the work goes out late,
  badly, on the wrong things.
- The sleep went first. Then the weight. Then the number a doctor mentioned twice
  before they heard it.
- They have started describing their own life in the past tense.

**None of that is a character problem.** Every one of those decisions was made in
a state that could not carry it — and the state is the one thing there that can
change.

## What it deliberately does not do

No streaks. No scores. No progress metrics. No timers or countdowns inside a
practice. No outcome promises — the frameworks are named, and their limits are
written down.

**One session is where it begins. It changes the state a person is in, which
changes what they can do next.**
