## 0 · What every track inherits

Non-negotiable, so it isn't restated per track:

- **Ten protocols.** Each names a **state**, not a topic. Every protocol
  regulates before it interprets — that is the line separating SafeRise from
  the label-dispensing content it defines itself against (`FOUNDER-COMMENTARY`
  §02).
- **Four steps, exactly four:** Recognise / Regulate / Release / Rise.
  "Before You Return" is the unnumbered post-protocol step.
- **The twelve-resource set**, nine universal and three conditional. Which
  conditionals apply is a per-protocol judgement, derived from content, never
  typed into a map.
- **Patterns are always described about the member**, never about an absent
  person. This is the hard boundary that keeps a track from becoming a
  diagnostic quiz about someone who cannot answer back.
- **No outcome promises. Retrospective framing only.** Prohibited vocabulary:
  quantum, frequency, manifest, rewire, spoken durations, fifth step,
  "practice" as a product word.

### How the twelve resources adapt across a new track

The nine universal resources keep their function and change their content:

| Resource | What changes in a new track |
|---|---|
| Guided Meditation | The full session. Longest asset, 800–1,500 words. Track-specific imagery and body focus. |
| Cue Card | The in-the-moment version, no preamble. Same four steps compressed to what someone can hold while activated. |
| How This Works | Mechanism plus sourcing. This is where framework citation lives — there is no separate sources resource. |
| Somatic Release Activities | Between-session physical work. Shortest asset, 120–270 words. |
| Safe Practice | When to proceed, when to stop, when this isn't the right tool. Carries the track's specific contraindications. |
| Disclosure & Support | A script for telling one person what you are doing. Track-specific because the disclosure risk differs sharply. |
| Your Record | Prompts tied to this protocol. Now also carries device-storage and export language, since nothing is retained server-side. |
| Accountability & Empathy | What the state does to people outside you. Where the track's ethics live. |
| The Decision | What takes over when you don't choose. The closing argument of each protocol. |

The three conditionals attach by pattern, not by track:

- **Proximity Guide** — where an ongoing external source is genuinely part of
  the pattern and distance is a live variable.
- **Invitation to Repair** — where reopening something with another person is a
  real available move.
- **Raising It** — where the pattern must be named to someone with power over
  you. Currently Track 03 only; Fitness and Intimacy both need it.

# Track · Sex & Intimacy

**Positioning.** The one area where dysregulation is most reliably misread as
identity — as brokenness, incompatibility, or a verdict about desire itself.
SafeRise's claim is narrow and defensible: the state you are in decides what is
available to you in intimacy, and the state is workable even when the story
about it is not.

**Hard boundary.** This track is psychoeducational and regulation-based. It is
not sex instruction, not technique, not therapy. It never depicts or describes
sexual acts. Every protocol is about the state around intimacy, not the act.

**Overlap to resolve before authoring.** `t2-05 Intimacy Barrier` already
exists in Relationship Healing. The division: Track 02's protocol is about the
relational barrier between two people; this track's material is about the
member's own state, and applies whether or not there is a partner. If that line
blurs, the track becomes a paid duplicate.

| 02 | The Body Exposure Protocol | Unsteady | Healing & Reconnection |

**What each one is actually about**

01 — Wanting differently from a partner, and the state that turns a difference
into a verdict about the relationship or about yourself.
02 — Being seen. The state that arrives before anything happens, and what
attention does when it turns inward at exactly the wrong moment.
03 — Anticipatory activation. Structurally the same mechanism as `t3-05`
Performance Anxiety, in the setting where it is least discussable.
04 — Freeze and numbing. Named carefully: shutdown is a protective response,
not a failure of desire. Highest contraindication load in the track.
05 — Saying what you want from a regulated state rather than a braced one.
06 — Declining without collapse, apology-spiral, or rupture.
07 — What you absorbed about sex before you had any say in it, and how it still
runs. Jung and Maté carry this one.
08 — After a no. The state that turns one refusal into a conclusion.
09 — Long-relationship flatness. Not a technique problem.
10 — Coming back after a rupture. Pairs with Invitation to Repair.

**Conditional resources:** Proximity Guide on 01, 07, 09. Invitation to Repair
on 05, 06, 08, 10. Raising It on 02, 04 — because these are the two where a
member may need to name something to a partner or a clinician.

**Safe Practice carries more weight here than in any existing track.** Protocol
04 in particular must name, without pathologising, that shutdown around
intimacy can follow trauma and that this platform is not the right tool for
that on its own. Dr. Bastien's review should be a gate on this track
specifically, whatever is decided about his site presence.

# Track · Elevation Series

**Positioning.** Not "advanced protocols." The problem this track addresses is
the one that arrives *after* the foundational work succeeds — the arrival
problem. You regulated, you got there, and the state that shows up is flatness
rather than the thing you expected.

Everything here draws on the Life Laboratory in `tracks.js` — eight stages
(LIVE, NOTICE, EXAMINE, LEARN, TEST, DOCUMENT, TRANSLATE, SERVE) and the
Elevation-only extension arc (CREATE VALUE, EARN, REINVEST, SERVE MORE) — plus
Philosophy §22–28: hidden intellectual property in ordinary lives, agency after
experience, human authorship, and wanting to be surpassed.

**The doctrine's own qualification must survive into every protocol:**
experience creates material, not wisdom. Something has to happen to the
material. That is the LEARN stage and it is the spine of this track.

**Ambition Recovery lands here.** It was withdrawn from Track 03 and re-scoped
as the arrival problem. Protocol 01 is that protocol under a name that says
what it does.

**What each one is actually about**

01 — You got what you were working toward and it is quiet. The state that
follows arrival, and why it is not ingratitude.
02 — Taking the principle out of the event. The state that makes you retell the
story instead of learning from it.
03 — Writing it down while it is still accurate, against the state that wants
to edit it into something more flattering or more damning.
04 — Putting it in language someone who has not lived it can use. The state
that hoards, and the state that over-explains.
05 — Testing what you believe you learned before building on it. Guards the
whole arc against confident nonsense.
06 — Giving without collapse. The state where service becomes a way of avoiding
your own material.
07 — What you know becoming something another person can hold. The state that
says it isn't worth anything because it came easily to you.
08 — Being paid for it is not a betrayal of it. Directly from the extension
arc, and the protocol most likely to meet resistance.
09 — Earnings buy reach. The state that treats reinvestment as risk rather than
as the mechanism.
10 — Wanting to be surpassed (§28). The state that holds on, and what it costs
the people it is held from.

**Structural note.** Elevation is a **one-time unlock with extended sessions**,
so its Guided Meditations should run materially longer than the three live
tracks and its resource set should skew toward Your Record and Accountability &
Empathy. Proximity Guide barely applies here; Raising It applies to 07, 08 and
09, where the member has to name something to a client, employer or partner.

**Eligibility.** Gated on completing foundational work, not applied for. The
plans page already says: *there is nothing to apply for — you will be told when
you qualify.* Whatever the qualification rule ends up being, it has to be
computable from the member's own record, and the record now lives on their
device. **That is an unresolved dependency and should be settled before the
track is authored, not after.**

# Track · Fitness — recommended third

**Why this one, and not the others.** `FOUNDER-COMMENTARY.md` §01 names the
trifecta — nutrition, nervous system regulation, fitness — as the three things
everything tracks back to, and its own "Opens" note says SafeRise owns one leg
of three and the positioning should not imply all three until they exist. That
is a documented mandate rather than a preference.

Against the alternatives on the coming-soon page:

- **Addiction Recovery** needs clinical governance, relapse-risk protocols and
  crisis pathways SafeRise does not have. Highest-harm track on the roadmap;
  it should not be third.
- **Embodied Nutrition** carries disordered-eating adjacency requiring
  clinical review of every asset and a hard prohibition on numeric targets. It
  is the natural *fourth*, once Fitness has established the guardrail pattern.
- **Money Shift** has no clinical dependency and is a strong state fit — scarcity,
  shame, avoidance are all regulation problems. It is the runner-up, and the
  argument for it is commercial rather than doctrinal.
- **Fitness** has no clinical dependency, closes a named gap in the founder's
  own stated model, and is the one track where the somatic resources already in
  the platform transfer almost directly.

**Suggested name:** *Training & The Body*. "Fitness" invites the comparison
this track exists to dismantle.

**What each one is actually about**

01 — Returning after a lapse, and the state that makes the first session feel
like a verdict on the gap.
02 — Looking, without the look becoming a judgement. Pairs closely with the
Intimacy track's protocol 02 but from a different entry point.
03 — Telling discomfort from harm. The state that overrides the signal, and the
state that mistakes every signal for a stop.
04 — The room. Other people's bodies, other people's progress.
05 — Movement after injury, and the state that keeps protecting long after the
tissue has healed.
06 — The week you missed, and what the state does with it — which is almost
never proportionate.
07 — Rest read as threat. The state where stopping feels like losing ground.
08 — Being observed while training. Structurally close to `t3-01` High-Stakes
Presence, in a physical setting.
09 — The body changed and the self-image didn't. The most under-served state in
the whole category.
10 — Training while life is heavy, and how to tell load-bearing from
self-harming.

**Guardrails, non-negotiable.** No numeric targets of any kind — no calories,
no weights, no rep schemes, no body measurements, no frequency prescriptions.
No before-and-after framing. No body-composition language. Nothing that could
function as a restriction or compensation instruction. Protocols 02, 06, 07 and
09 sit closest to disordered-eating territory and their Safe Practice resources
must say plainly that this platform is not the right tool if food or exercise
has become compulsive, and point outward.

**Conditional resources:** Proximity Guide on 04, 08. Raising It on 05, 08, 10
— naming a limit to a coach, a trainer, or a team.

## Sequencing recommendation

**Training & The Body first.** No clinical dependency, no external reviewer, no
crisis-pathway design. It also lets the guardrail pattern get built and tested
before Embodied Nutrition needs it.

**Elevation second**, but only once the eligibility rule is settled. It is the
smallest authoring job — extended sessions on an existing four-step spine — and
it is the only one with revenue already priced at €222.

**Sex & Intimacy third.** Highest authoring care, highest review burden, and
the one that most needs clinical sign-off before publication. It is also the
strongest differentiator on the roadmap, which is exactly why it should not be
rushed to be first.
