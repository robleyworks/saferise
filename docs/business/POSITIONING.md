# SafeRise — positioning

**Draft, 13 September 2026.** Written to be argued with.

---

## 1 · The thing underneath

Everything rests on one claim about ordering:

> The capacity to govern attention sits upstream of nutrition, fitness, education,
> professional capability and relationships. All of them run on it.

That makes SafeRise domain-agnostic by design. It is not a wellness product that happens
to cover several areas. It is one capability, applied wherever a state is currently
deciding what a person does.

**Calm is the entry point, not the function.** The function is the recovery of conscious
participation — room to observe rather than automatically obey.

---

## 2 · The category question

You proposed **coaching as a service**, B2B and B2C. The architecture supports it. The
label does not, at the current price.

### Where it holds

You already have a coaching stack in everything but name — self-guided protocols, group
workshops, one-to-one, retreats, organisational programmes. Four levels of support around
one method is the shape of a coaching service.

### Where it breaks

**Coaching implies a person.** Your core is self-guided audio. A buyer hearing "coaching
as a service" expects humans and finds a library. That gap is most expensive in the
enterprise sale, where someone has to defend the purchase internally.

**And the price contradicts the label.** Coaching-as-a-service — BetterUp, CoachHub,
Sounding Board — runs roughly €1,000–3,000 per seat per year, because a person is doing
hours of work. SafeRise is €180. A buyer who knows the category reads €15 a seat as
*this is not actually coaching*.

⚠ **Pick one.** Either the label moves to match the price, or the price moves to match
the label. Leaving both is the version that loses deals slowly and never tells you why.

---

## 3 · Two coherent positions

### A · The layer underneath — recommended

> **SafeRise is the regulation and attention layer that makes everything else land.**

Not coaching. The capability coaching, training, therapy, nutrition programmes and
performance work all quietly assume people already have.

**Why this is the stronger position:**

- **It explains the price.** €15 a seat is cheap for coaching and entirely reasonable for
  infrastructure. The number stops being an objection and becomes evidence.
- **It sells alongside rather than against.** An organisation with an existing EAP,
  coaching panel or L&D programme is not being asked to replace any of it.
- **It matches what is actually built.** Self-guided at the core, with human support as
  levels above it rather than as the product.
- **It is the honest version of the upstream claim.** You already say attention sits
  beneath nutrition, fitness and work. This is that sentence, sold.
- **It scales without staff.** Seats cost nothing to serve. Coaching costs hours.

**The cost:** "infrastructure" is a colder word than "coaching", and it wins fewer
conversations on first contact. It wins more of the ones that get to a second.

### B · Coaching as a service

> **SafeRise coaches people through regulation, attention and response — across their
> personal life, their relationships and their work.**

Warmer, more legible, and a category buyers already have budget for.

**What it would require:**

- **€900–1,500 per seat per year**, not €180
- **Real facilitated contact in every seat** — a group session a month at minimum
- **Delivery capacity you do not have.** At roughly four hours a month of facilitator
  time, this is capped at a handful of organisations
- **The clinical gate closed**, because live contact at scale is a different exposure

**This is a different business**, and it is the one that hits a wall fastest.

## 4 · What Position A looks like in market

| | |
|---|---|
| **Category** | Nervous-system regulation and attention infrastructure |
| **For** | People and organisations who already have support, and still lose the moment |
| **Against** | Not therapy, not an EAP, not a meditation app, not coaching |
| **Proof** | Named frameworks with written limits. No outcome promises. No streaks, scores or progress metrics |
| **B2C wedge** | A whole track free with an account — not a trial |
| **B2B wedge** | A delivered pilot that leaves the platform behind |

### The sentence

> When someone is in a triggered state, everything else you have given them is
> unreachable. SafeRise is what gets it back.

That works for a consumer and for an HR director, which very few sentences do.

## 5 · The domains — sequencing, not scope

The upstream claim licenses every domain. **The product does not cover them yet.**

| Domain | State |
|---|---|
| Personal | Live |
| Relationships | Live |
| Professional performance | Live |
| Embodied Nutrition | Not built |
| Strength & Return | Not built |
| Money, intimacy, recovery, parenting, elevation | Not built |

⚠ **Do not sell "your nutrition, your fitness goals" today.** The record already states
that SafeRise owns one leg of the named trifecta and the positioning must not imply
otherwise until the others exist.

**How to talk about it without overclaiming:**

> The same capability applies wherever a state is making the decision. We have built it
> for three areas of life so far, and we are building it for the rest.

That is true, it is ambitious, and it survives a sceptic.

## 6 · Pricing coherence

The current book is coherent **under Position A** and incoherent under Position B.

| | Price | Reads as |
|---|---|---|
| Track 01 | Free with an account | The wedge |
| Membership | €19/mo · €190/yr | Infrastructure |
| Workshops | €39 pp | Facilitated, occasional |
| One-to-one | €129 · three for €349 | The human option, priced as one |
| Pilot programme | €1,800 | A delivered event that leaves something behind |
| Seats | €15 → €11 | Infrastructure at volume |

**The one-to-one is the only item priced like coaching, and it should stay that way** —
it is the thing a person actually delivers.

### The gap worth naming

**€1,800 for a pilot, then €15 a seat.** An organisation that runs the pilot and buys 100
seats spends €18,000 in year one. That is a real contract, and it reaches it without
anyone being called a coach.

**What is missing between them is a recurring delivered layer** — the thing that would
make it a service rather than a licence. A facilitated session a quarter, included above a
seat threshold, would close that gap without requiring the full coaching build.

⚠ **Delivery capacity is the constraint, not demand.** Roughly four hours a month exists.
Anything recurring has to be sized against that number, not against the market.

---

## 7 · What has to be true before this ships

- **Clinical gate.** Escalation criteria written and agreed before any live layer is sold
- **Seat reporting.** The quarterly seats-in-use commitment must be verified as
  deliverable — nothing currently produces that figure
- **The trifecta claim.** Corrected wherever it implies nutrition and fitness exist
- **Price consistency.** €29 was live on a track page as of this morning

---

## 8 · The recommendation

**Take Position A. Keep the prices. Change the words.**

You are not selling coaching. You are selling the layer that everything else assumes and
nobody provides — and that is a rarer thing to own than another coaching brand.

Revisit Position B when there is a clinician, a facilitator bench, and evidence that
organisations will pay four figures a seat. None of those exist yet, and building the
words before the business is how a company ends up unable to deliver what its own site
promises.
