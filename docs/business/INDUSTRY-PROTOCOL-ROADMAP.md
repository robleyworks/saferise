# Industry protocol roadmap

**Where the distress actually is, what is missing, and what to build in what order.**
Draft, 13 September 2026.

---

## 1 · Method

Sectors ranked by three things a B2B buyer already knows about their own workforce:

1. **Dysregulation prevalence** — how routinely people are operating in a triggered,
   braced or shut-down state
2. **Substrate degradation** — shift work, disrupted sleep, eating conditions, training
   displaced by hours
3. **Buyability** — whether a budget, a mandate and a named owner exist

⚠ **Prevalence and buyability are not the same thing.** The sectors with the most distress
are often the ones least able to buy. Both matter; the roadmap follows the intersection.

---

## 2 · Sector assessment

### Tier A — high distress, high substrate degradation, real budget

| Sector | Signature | Substrate | Buyability |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Healthcare & emergency** | Shift-disrupted sleep, eating on the run, exposure to outcomes, shame after error, compassion fatigue | **Severe** | Strong — mandated wellbeing spend, named owner, retention crisis |
| **Finance, legal, professional services** | Decision density, adversarial exposure, imposter pressure, hours displacing rest and training | **High** | Strong — high per-head budget, attrition is expensive |
| **Technology & engineering** | Context switching, on-call sleep disruption, burnout normalised, comparison | **Moderate–high** | Strong — established wellbeing budgets, receptive vocabulary |

### Tier B — very high distress, harder sale

| Sector | Signature | Substrate | Buyability |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Education** | Emotional labour, no recovery window, public performance, term-cycle exhaustion | High | **Weak** — budget-constrained, long cycles |
| **Hospitality & retail** | Shift work, abuse from public, low control, thin support | **Severe** | **Weak** — margin-thin, high churn |
| **Manufacturing, logistics, field ops** | Shift work, physical load, injury fear, low psychological safety | **Severe** | Moderate — safety budget is the door, not wellbeing |
| **Social care & frontline support** | Secondary trauma, chronic underfunding, moral injury | **Severe** | **Weak** |

### Tier C — distinct, narrow, high-value

| Sector | Signature | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Sales organisations** | Rejection cycling, number pressure, visible performance | Buys quickly, measures everything |
| **Leadership & people managers** | Holding others without support, isolation, livelihood decisions | Cross-industry; the highest-margin bundle |
| **Founders & entrepreneurs** | No structure, no salary, isolation, identity fusion | B2C, not B2B. Already scoped as a track |

## 3 · What already exists

**Live:** Personal Transformation (10) · Relationship Healing (10) · Professional
Performance (10)

**Written, not shipped:** Elevation · Sex & Intimacy · Executive Presence · Strength &
Return · Embodied Nutrition · Entrepreneur's Journey · Money Shift · Addiction Recovery

**Professional Performance already covers**, cross-industry: high-stakes presence,
conflict, imposter, perfectionism, performance anxiety, belonging, career transition,
decision fatigue, burnout, creative flow.

**Executive Presence already covers** the leadership layer: self-disqualification, first
hire, peer to boss, losing the craft, the promised number, asking for more, letting
someone go, carrying what they told you, cost at home, role drift.

**Assessment:** the *application* layer is well served. **The substrate layer is nearly
empty, and the sector-specific states are entirely unserved.**

### Gap 4 — Substrate completion

**Strength & Return** and **Embodied Nutrition** are written but unshipped.

**Priority: high.** With Sleep, they complete the substrate layer and make the
three-layer model true rather than aspirational.

⚠ **Existing gates stand:** nutrition has disordered-eating adjacency and a clinical
review gate; fitness is states only, no numbers, targets or programmes. **New from the
substrate model:** both must route outward when the problem is deficiency or injury
rather than state.

## 6 · Build sequence

### Phase 1 — complete the substrate layer

**Ship Strength & Return and Embodied Nutrition. Build Sleep & Recovery.**

Rationale: the three-layer model is the whole positioning argument. Until substrate
exists, it is a claim rather than a product. Sleep unlocks the most sectors per unit of
work.

*Output: three tracks, 30 protocols. Substrate layer complete.*

### Phase 2 — open healthcare

**Ship Executive Presence. Build Exposure & Aftermath under clinical governance.**

Rationale: Tier A, highest budget, clearest need, and Executive Presence is already
written so it is cheap to ship alongside.

⚠ **Exposure & Aftermath does not ship without a clinician.** If that is unresolved,
Phase 2 is Executive Presence alone and healthcare waits.

*Output: healthcare and leadership bundles sellable.*

### Phase 3 — broaden the application layer

**Ship Money Shift, Sex & Intimacy, Addiction Recovery, Entrepreneur's Journey.**

Rationale: written already, mostly B2C depth, strengthens the consumer offer while the
enterprise pipeline matures.

⚠ Addiction Recovery requires clinical governance and crisis routing before shipping.

### Phase 4 — Frontline & Public-Facing

Build when a Tier A contract funds it. Large population, weak buyability, and it should
not consume runway before healthcare or finance is closed.

### Ongoing — Elevation

Outside the stack. It is what becomes available when survival is not taking the whole
budget. Ship when ready; it does not gate anything.

## 8 · Gates, consolidated

- **Clinician engaged and insured** before Exposure & Aftermath or Addiction Recovery
- **Escalation criteria agreed** before any live organisational session is sold
- **Embodied Nutrition** — clinical review; no numbers; routes outward on deficiency
- **Strength & Return** — states only; routes outward on injury
- **Sleep & Recovery** — routes outward on apnoea, insomnia disorder, medication effects
- **Frontline** — describes the environment, never diagnoses the workforce
- **Never** state a protocol or track count in marketing. The numbers keep moving

---

## 9 · The one judgement call

**Sleep or Exposure first?**

**Sleep** is unblocked, opens more sectors, and needs no clinician. **Exposure** is what
makes healthcare buy, and healthcare is the best-funded Tier A sector.

**Recommendation: Sleep first.** It ships without waiting on anyone, it strengthens every
bundle including healthcare's, and it buys the time to get clinical governance right
rather than fast.
