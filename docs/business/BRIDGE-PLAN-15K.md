# The $15,000 bridge

**Months 1–3: $5,000 to a full B2C rollout.
Months 4–6: $10,000 to B2B pilot-ready.**

What this buys, what it deliberately does not, and what you do yourself.

---

## The principle

**Spend only on things that cannot be done by hand.**

At this budget, every euro goes to one of three places: something that unblocks revenue,
something that is legally required, or something that produces audio you cannot voice
yourself. Everything else waits for the raise.

⚠ **The largest saving available:** a pilot does not need the enterprise platform. Twenty-five
seats for ninety days is a spreadsheet, a coupon code and an email. Building organisations,
seats, SSO and admin console before a single pilot is sold spends €27,000 to solve a problem
you can solve for free until roughly the fifth customer.

## Why the AI budget is not overhead

At $15,000 total, the temptation is to cut tooling first. That is backwards.

**What $2,010 of AI tooling substitutes for across six months:**

| Would otherwise cost | Approx. | What replaces it |
|---|---|---|
| Contract developer — platform, passes, fixes | €12,000–18,000 | Claude Code against written pass briefs |
| Designer — mockups, decks, collateral | €4,000–6,000 | Claude + ChatGPT, reviewed by you |
| Video production — film, banners | €2,000–4,000 | Runway |
| Copywriter — pages, briefs, documents | €3,000–5,000 | Already how this project is written |

**The constraint this budget is actually buying its way out of is your own time.** You are
building this alongside a full-time role. Every hour a tool removes is an hour available for
the two things nothing can do for you: selling, and delivering the pilots.

⚠ **One honest caveat.** These tools compress construction, not judgement. The passes still
need briefs, the output still needs verification against the live tree, and this session has
produced several corrections where an unverified assumption went into a brief and came back
as a defect. Budget review time, not just subscription.

## What this proves, and why it changes the raise

At the end of month six you hold three things you do not have today:

1. **A real conversion rate** — the single most leveraged assumption in every forecast
2. **Two written pilot findings reports** — the evidence gap closed
3. **One named reference customer** — the credibility gap closed

⚠ **That is exactly the Path A month-six milestone.** Doing it on $15,000 of your own money
rather than on someone else's changes the conversation entirely: you are no longer raising
to find out whether people pay. You are raising to scale something that already does.

**A backer funding €500,000 into an untested conversion rate is taking a risk. A backer
funding €500,000 into a proven one is buying growth.** The second is a much easier
conversation, and it is worth considerably more than $15,000 of dilution avoided.

## The honest risks in this plan

**Paddle approval is outside your control.** Free tier launches regardless; paid revenue
does not. If it slips past month three, the conversion number slips with it.

**Fifty protocols of audio in three months is real production work.** If the tone-break
problem across passage seams is unresolved, this is where it bites. Budget the time, not
just the subscription.

**Running two pilots while employed full-time is the binding constraint, not the money.**
Each is a half-day delivered plus follow-up. Book them deliberately.

**Insurance may be required earlier than month four** if a paid 1:1 is delivered in month
three. Check before the first session, not after.
