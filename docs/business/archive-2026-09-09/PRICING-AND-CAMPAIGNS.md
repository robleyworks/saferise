# PART ONE · PRICING

## The two constraints that shape every number

**1 · No outcome claims.** Standard wellbeing pricing is justified by promised
ROI. That justification is unavailable. Price must therefore be anchored to
**format, duration and access** — things that are demonstrably delivered.

**2 · The calendar is the ceiling.** Anything Andre personally delivers cannot
scale. Price live delivery to be genuinely scarce; price access to be genuinely
easy.

---

## Consumer workshops — remote

Current: €29 personal, €39 relationship per couple. Both correct as an entry
price, both wrong as the whole ladder.

| Format | Duration | Price | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Open session** | 60 min | **Free** | Monthly. Top of funnel. Fills the list and the 1:1 slots |
| **Protocol workshop — Personal** | 60 min | **€29** | Entry paid. Unchanged |
| **Protocol workshop — Relationship** | 60 min | **€39/couple** | Unchanged |
| **Protocol Intensive** | 90 min, capped at 12 | **€69** | New. Small room, direct work, sells out |
| **Four-week series** | 4 × 60 min | **€149** | New. The retention product — four weeks builds a habit, one hour does not |

**Why add the top two.** At €29 the workshop is a funnel, not a business. The
Intensive and the series give people who want more somewhere to go that is not a
€129 private session — and the series is the only workshop format that produces
the repeated contact retention actually needs.

**Capacity rule.** One free session and one paid session per month maximum.
Adding a third is how the calendar quietly becomes the job.

---

## Corporate workshops and retreats

**⚠ The current structure prices the wrong variable.** €1,800 for up to 50 and
€3,500 over 50 charges by headcount — but delivering a remote session to 30 or 60
people is identical work. Headcount only matters when there is a room, catering
and travel.

**Price on format and location instead.**

| Offer | Delivery | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lunch-and-learn** | Remote, 45 min | **Free** | The entry. Needs no track record |
| **Team session** | Remote, 90 min | **€1,200** | Single team or department |
| **Half-day programme** | Remote | **€2,400** | Multi-session, one day |
| **Half-day, in person** | On site | **€3,500** + travel | Travel and time billed separately |
| **Full-day retreat** | On site | **€6,500** + travel | Off-site, catering excluded |
| **Conference or festival** | On site | **Custom, from €4,000** | Highest visibility, best lead source |

**Remote is cheaper and more profitable.** No travel, no lost days, no venue
risk. Make it the recommended option rather than the compromise.

**Never discount the day rate.** If a buyer needs a lower number, reduce scope —
a 90-minute session instead of a half-day — not the rate. A discounted day rate
becomes the new rate permanently.

## Licence packages

| Package | Headcount | Annual | Per employee | Included |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Pilot** | One department, capped | **€2,500 / 3 months** | — | Full access, 1 session, fixed end date |
| **Foundation** | Under 100 | **€4,500** | €45+ | Full library, 4 sessions/year, launch kit |
| **Growth** | 100–500 | **€12,000** | €24–120 | + named contact, quarterly review |
| **Scale** | 500–2,000 | **€28,000** | €14–56 | + 2 additional sessions, multi-site rollout |
| **Enterprise** | 2,000+ | **Custom** | Negotiated | + co-designed programme |

**Benchmark:** employee assistance programmes run roughly €15–40 per employee per
year for a service with famously low utilisation. These bands sit inside that
range while delivering 30 protocols and 308 resources rather than a phone number.

### Packaging levers

| Lever | Terms | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Founding logo programme** | First 6 organisations, **40% off year one** | Bought in exchange for a named, quotable reference. The reference is worth more than the discount |
| **Two-year term** | **15% off**, paid annually | Locks revenue, removes a renewal conversation |
| **Retreat bundle** | Licence + full-day retreat, **€2,000 off** the retreat | Attaches live delivery to the recurring line |
| **Multi-site** | Second site at **60%** of band price | Expansion without a new sale |

**Discount discipline.** Never discount a licence band outside the founding
programme. If price is the objection, move them down a band or into the pilot.
The band price is the anchor for every renewal and every future customer.

### Anchoring order

Present in this sequence, always:

1. **Full-day retreat — €6,500.** Establishes what a day of your time costs.
2. **Licence — €12,000 for 500 people.** Now reads as obvious value.
3. **Pilot — €2,500.** Now reads as trivially small.

Leading with the licence makes it the expensive thing. Leading with the retreat
makes it the reasonable one.

# PART TWO · CAMPAIGN STRATEGY

## The messaging constraint, solved

**You cannot write:** improve productivity · reduce burnout · boost performance ·
increase retention · optimise · maximise · any promised outcome.

That removes roughly 90% of standard wellbeing outbound copy. What remains is
stronger, because nobody else is using it.

**Four angles that work inside the rules**

| Angle | The line | Why it lands |
|---|---|---|
| **The failed rollout** | "What did activation look like at week six?" | Every HR leader has lived this. It is a question, not a claim |
| **The disclosure problem** | "Everything you have bought before required someone to raise their hand." | Names the mechanism nobody else names |
| **The short security review** | "Your InfoSec review will be unusually quick, and here is why." | Concrete, verifiable, and a genuine procurement benefit |
| **The refusal** | "No streaks, no scores, no data to leadership." | Differentiation stated as a limitation, which reads as honest |

**Never lead with the founder story.** It converts on the second call, not the
first.

## Channel 1 · LinkedIn — organic

**The personal profile is the asset.** A company page will get negligible reach
and is not worth the effort at this stage.

### The content moat

An ex-enterprise seller who now builds nervous-system regulation can say things
about why corporate wellbeing fails that a wellness founder cannot credibly say,
and that an HR consultant will not say. **That is the entire content strategy.**

### Pillars — three posts per week

| Pillar | Cadence | Example angle |
|---|---|---|
| **The refusal** | Weekly | Why the product has no streaks, and what streaks are actually for |
| **Mechanism, no claims** | Weekly | What a state does to judgement, described without promising anything |
| **Artefact** | Weekly | A protocol cover, a page, a design decision, a rejected image and why |

**Highest-performing format:** a specific decision, the reasoning, and the cost of
it. "We removed the completion tracker. Here is what it cost us." Build-in-public
without the self-congratulation.

### Profile as landing page

- ○ Headline reframed to the buyer, not the seller
- ○ Featured section: licence one-pager, method page, a workshop replay
- ○ About section carries the free track link
- ○ Creator mode on, so the follow button precedes connection

## Channel 2 · Sales Navigator

### Search architecture

| Filter | Setting |
|---|---|
| **Titles** | CHRO · People Director · Head of People · Head of Talent · Head of L&D · Wellbeing Lead · People Operations Lead |
| **Headcount** | 100–2,000 (the licence sweet spot) |
| **Geography** | Netherlands · UK · DACH · Nordics first |
| **Industry** | Software & IT first — your language, your network, your credibility |
| **Seniority** | Director and above |

### Saved searches with alerts — the trigger engine

| Saved search | Why it is a trigger |
|---|---|
| **Job changes into People roles** | First 100 days. Actively looking for initiatives to own |
| **Companies that posted about wellbeing in 30 days** | Budget is live and visible |
| **Headcount growth >20%** | Scaling pain is real and named internally |
| **Recent funding** | Budget exists and People functions get built out |
| **TeamLink to your network** | Warm path into a cold account |

**Past-customer filter is the highest-yield search you own.** Jamf and HashiCorp
accounts — 470+ logos, a 360-customer territory — many of those contacts are now
several years more senior. Build that list first.

### Weekly rhythm

- 25 new qualified prospects added
- 15 connection requests, personalised, no pitch
- Engage with 10 prospect posts before any outreach
- 5 accounts moved to sequence

## Channel 3 · Email

### Warm reactivation — fundamentally different, run it first

These people know you. Do not sequence them like strangers.

**One email. No follow-up sequence. No CTA beyond a reply.**

> Subject: Different thing entirely
>
> [Name] — we worked together when I was at [Jamf/HashiCorp] on [specific thing].
>
> I left enterprise software and spent the last year building something in an
> unrelated field: a nervous-system regulation platform. Thirty protocols, three
> tracks, and a corporate version that organisations licence for their whole
> population.
>
> Not pitching you. I'm reaching out to about forty people I respect from that
> period to ask what you think of the corporate side — you sat on the buying end
> of enough of these to have a view.
>
> Worth fifteen minutes?
>
> Andre

**Why this works:** it is true, it asks for judgement rather than money, and the
buying-side compliment is real. Expect a 30–40% reply rate against 2–5% cold.

### Cold sequence — four touches, 18 days

| # | Day | Angle | Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 | The failed rollout question. No pitch, no link | 60 words |
| 2 | 4 | The disclosure problem. One sentence on device-only records | 70 words |
| 3 | 10 | The short security review. Offer the one-pager | 60 words |
| 4 | 18 | Permission to close the file | 30 words |

**Rules**
- No attachments before a reply
- One link maximum, never in email one
- No calendar link until they express interest
- Plain text. HTML templates read as marketing and get filtered
- Break-up email gets the highest reply rate of the four — write it properly

## Channel 4 · Phone

You know this channel. Three constraints specific to this sale.

| Rule | Reason |
|---|---|
| **Never call before email engagement** | Cold-calling HR without a trigger is the lowest-yield activity available |
| **Call the day of an open or click** | Reference nothing about the tracking. Just call |
| **Voicemail plus email, same hour** | The voicemail makes the email get opened. Neither works alone |

**Opening that works:** "I sold employee experience software for a decade. I now
build something in wellbeing, and I'm calling because you've probably bought one
of these before and had it not land. I wanted to ask what happened."

That is a question from a peer, not a pitch from a vendor.

## Channel 5 · Social — B2C discovery

**Different audience, different motion, and it solves the distribution problem
the forecast depends on.**

⚠ **The site is noindex.** Until that changes there is no organic discovery at
all, and social plus YouTube are the *only* discovery layer. This is the single
highest-leverage fix available.

| Platform | Content | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **YouTube** | Full guided meditations, method explainers | Indexes. Becomes the search layer the site currently isn't |
| **Instagram / TikTok** | 30–60s protocol excerpts, in Andre's voice | Free-track signups. Volume channel |
| **Podcast** | The Disciplined Man, co-hosted with Dwight | Arrives with an audience attached |

**One rule for short-form:** never a claim, always a recognition. "The version of
you that edits every sentence before it leaves your mouth" outperforms any
promise, and stays inside the vocabulary rules.

## The 90-day campaign

| Phase | Days | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| **Foundation** | 1–14 | Profile rebuilt. Sales Navigator searches saved. Warm list of 40 built. Licence one-pager written. Site indexing decision made |
| **Warm wave** | 15–30 | 40 warm emails sent in batches of 10. LinkedIn posting begins at 3/week. YouTube channel opened |

| **Convert** | 60–90 | First pilot signed. Founding logo programme opened. Testimonials captured. Retreat offered to pilot accounts |

## What to measure

| Stage | Metric | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Warm | Reply rate | 30%+ |
| Cold | Reply rate | 5%+ |
| Cold | Meeting rate | 1.5–3% |
| LinkedIn | Profile views/week | Rising trend, not absolute |
| Lunch-and-learn | Booked to delivered | 70%+ |
| Lunch-and-learn | Delivered to pilot | 25%+ |
| Pilot | Pilot to licence | 50%+ |
| B2C | Free signups/month | The number that decides Years 2–3 |

**The one that matters most is free signups.** Every other metric here improves a
number the forecast already assumes. That one creates a number that does not yet
exist.
