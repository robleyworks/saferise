# Operating Note — 8 September 2026

## Principle

State precedes thought. Thought colours emotion. Emotion drives reaction.
Regulate the state first; everything downstream is cheaper.

Method: bring it all to the surface, then reverse-engineer the inquiry — what
sits under the layers of this state. Clarity arriving from that inquiry is
signal, not interference. Do not suppress it. Its job is to narrow the option
set to the right ones.

## Decisions taken

1. **Independent production.** Secure the capacity to produce everything the
   platform needs without waiting on anyone.
2. **Independent finance.** Fund what the platform needs without external
   dependency.
3. **Confirmed advisory + collaborators.** Named, active, proactively engaging —
   following up so roadmap items move.
4. **Boundaries.** Say no to people operating outside range of alignment.

### Test for (4)

Withdraw or decline where there is:
- no follow-through on stated commitments
- no demonstrated capacity to fulfil what they hold
- no accountability when it slips
- net load added rather than balance supported

character failure in them rather than a design failure in the arrangement.
