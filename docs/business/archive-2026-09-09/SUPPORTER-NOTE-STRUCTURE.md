# Supporter Funding Structure

*Companion to `GTM-FORECAST-60DAY.md` and `ENTITY-ROADMAP.md`.
Instrument, ladder, and the material to put in front of someone.*

> Not legal or financial advice. A convertible instrument is a security in most
> jurisdictions, including this one. The paper needs counsel before signature —
> already budgeted in the 60-day forecast.

---

## The idea in one line

**Not "back my project." Instead: "here is the smallest amount that proves
something, here is what it proves, here is the date you'll know."**

---

## Why tranching is the persuasion, not the paperwork

The fear for someone writing a $1,500 cheque to a friend's venture is not losing
$1,500. It is the silence afterwards — having backed something that quietly died
and became a subject neither party raises again.

Tranching removes that entirely:

| What tranching gives them | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| A decision point every ~6 weeks | Faith becomes a series of small verifiable steps |
| Evidence before each release | They are never asked to believe, only to read |
| A graceful exit | They stop releasing. No confrontation, no awkwardness |
| A capped worst case | Exposure is one tranche, not the full commitment |
| A reason to stay engaged | Each gate is news they're part of |

The structure is doing the emotional work. The terms are just how it's written down.

## Timing

| Tranche | Day | Release |
|---|---|---|
| T1 | 0 | Tracks 01–03 live at **day 45** |
| T2 | 45 | Three new tracks live at **~day 165** |
| T3 | 165 | Scale phase, ~10 weeks |
| T4 | 235 | Final two tracks live at **~day 325** |

**T1 is 45 days because nothing is being written.** All 31 protocols exist —
308 resources, 151,772 words. What remains is 29 posters against a verified
`mk_posters.py`, a removal pass on nine t3 covers, one new t3-06 cover, and the
Stage A–D code pass.

**T2 is ~4 months because three tracks is a large build.**

| Per track | Volume |
|---|---|
| Protocols | 10 |
| Resources | ~100 |
| Words | ~50,000 |
| Covers | 10 |
| Posters | 10 |
| Recordings + mastering | 10 |

Three tracks is 30 protocols and ~150,000 words — a duplicate of the entire
existing body of work, built while operating a live platform, running workshops
and delivering 1:1s. Four months assumes aggressive batching: all writing
front-loaded, covers and posters produced in one pass, recordings in blocks.

**Do not put 60 days in front of a supporter.** A missed schedule at the first
gate costs more credibility than the extra ten weeks costs in patience.

## The gates still point at retention, not output

Each tranche releases on **evidence people pay and return** — not on the previous
tranche having been spent.

This matters most at T2. If Tracks 01–03 launch and retention is weak, building
three more tracks is the wrong response and the gate should stop it. Content
volume was never the unproven thing: 151,772 words with zero paying members is
the current position, and production capacity is already established beyond
argument.

## The instrument

A simple convertible loan with a revenue-linked downside. Kept deliberately
plain — cap tables and pro-rata rights are overkill at this size and expensive
to draft.

### Upside — it works

| Term | Position |
|---|---|
| Conversion trigger | First qualifying raise (defined: ≥$100,000) |
| Conversion terms | Principal converts at a 20–25% discount to the round price |
| Entity | Converts into the entity holding SafeRise at conversion — **successor-entity clause required** so the right survives any restructure (see `ENTITY-ROADMAP.md`) |
| Interest | None, or nominal. Simplicity is worth more than a few dollars |

**Be straight about the size.** $5,000 at a 25% discount into a $1M pre-money is
roughly 0.65%. This is not a returns pitch, and presenting it as one damages
credibility. The honest framing is better: real paper, a defined floor, and no
possibility of being quietly forgotten.

### Downside — no qualifying raise by month 18

**Holder chooses at the trigger date.** Offering the choice costs almost nothing
and signals that the downside was designed for them, not around them.

| Option | Terms |
|---|---|
| **A — Revenue share** | 5% of gross monthly revenue until 1.4× principal is repaid. No fixed date |
| **B — Scheduled repayment** | Principal + 20%, repaid over 12 months from month 18 |

**Default to A if they don't choose.** A fixed repayment cliff on a pre-revenue
platform creates exactly the pressure the whole operating model is meant to
eliminate — and a holder can never ask for money the business doesn't have.

### Honest failure path

If the platform is wound down, remaining principal repays from whatever revenue
and realisable assets exist, and the holder is told directly rather than left to
work it out.

Write this clause in. Someone who reads it understands they are dealing with a
person who thought about how this ends badly, not only how it ends well. That is
disproportionately persuasive.

## Non-financial terms — costs nothing, matters more than the equity

| Offer | Marginal cost |
|---|---|
| Founding supporter — lifetime platform access, all tracks | $0 |
| Two seats at any group workshop | ~$0 |
| First look at the Elevation Series when it opens | $0 |
| Named in the founding record — **only if they want it** | $0 |
| Monthly written update, fixed date, whether news is good or bad | Time |

The last one is the real product. It's what makes T2 easy to release.

## Who to ask

**Profile, not a list.**

Ask someone who:
- would not notice $1,500 leaving for eighteen months
- has some proximity to the work — health, coaching, wellness, or has watched you build
- you can still have dinner with if this fails

Do not ask someone who:
- would feel the loss
- you'd avoid at a difficult moment
- is being asked because they're reachable rather than because they fit

**One person is enough.** A second holder of conversion rights triggers Gate C
and forces the entity question early. Keep it to one until the structure can
carry more.

### SafeRise Protocol — Founding Supporter Note

**What it is**
A self-guided nervous-system regulation platform. 31 protocols, 308 resources,
three tracks. Built on Polyvagal Theory, HeartMath's heart-brain coherence
research, and Compassionate Inquiry. Clinical review by Dr. Keny F. Bastien MD.

**What I'm asking**
$1,500 now, up to $5,000 total across four tranches. Each one releases a group of
tracks. You release the next only if I hit benchmarks written down before you
sign.

**What the first $1,500 does**
Puts all three existing tracks live — 31 protocols, already written, plus the
remaining image and build work. Legal review and site terms. Payment processing
setup. Runs the platform for 45 days.

**What it has to prove — by day 45**
- Payments working end to end
- Three tracks live at €19, €29 and €39
- 20+ people through a protocol
- Half of them finishing it
- A third coming back within a week

**Then**
$1,500 builds the next three tracks. $1,000 scales what's working — workshops and
corporate contracts. A final $1,000 completes the last two tracks. Eight tracks,
$5,000, roughly eleven months.

Each release is gated on paying members and retention, not on my having spent the
last tranche.

**If it works**
Your money converts to equity at the first real raise, at a 20–25% discount.
Being straight with you: on $5,000 that's well under one percent. This isn't a
returns play.

**If it doesn't**
You choose. Either 5% of monthly revenue until you're back to 1.4× what you put
in, or principal plus 20% repaid over a year. If I wind it down, you hear it from
me directly and I repay from whatever exists.

**What you get either way**
Lifetime access to everything. Two workshop seats. A written update on the 1st
of every month — good news or bad — for as long as this runs.

**What I need from you**
A conversation, and a lawyer's read of the paper before either of us signs.

## The approach — sequence, not a script

| Step | Do |
|---|---|
| 1 | Ask for a conversation, not money. No document attached |
| 2 | Show the platform. 31 protocols and 151,000 words is the argument; talking about it is not |
| 3 | Make the ask specific: **$1,500, first of four tranches, ceiling $5,000, eight tracks** |
| 4 | Send the one-pager after, not before |
| 5 | Say plainly it's small equity and you'd rather they knew that now |
| 6 | Give them a week and don't chase |

**Do not:** open with the deck, inflate the numbers, imply a raise is imminent,
or ask over text.

```

Gate status
[gate table: benchmark | target | actual | ●◑○]

Shipped
- [three lines maximum]

Missed
- [name it plainly, with what changed as a result]

Money
Tranche 1 used: $[x] of $1,500 — [one line on what for]

Next tranche
[Gate A/B] currently [n] of [n] met. On track / not on track for [date].

[Nothing, or one specific thing]
```

**Send it when things go badly too.** The month you go quiet is the month the
relationship changes.
