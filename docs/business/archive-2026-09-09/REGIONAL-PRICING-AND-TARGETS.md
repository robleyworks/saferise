## Why two price books

A 200-person resort in Sint Maarten and a 200-person SaaS company in Amsterdam
are the same headcount and entirely different buyers. The resort's annual
training budget may be smaller than the European licence fee alone.

**This is not a discount. It is regional pricing against market conditions** —
standard practice across software and media, and defensible in any room.

| | Caribbean | Europe |
|---|---|---|
| Currency | **USD** | **EUR** |
| Wellbeing as a budget line | Rarely exists | Established, often mandated |
| Procurement | Relationship-led, fast | Process-led, slow |
| Decision maker | Owner, GM, HR Manager | CHRO, People Director, committee |
| Typical cycle | 2–8 weeks | 3–9 months |
| Payer | Often not the employer | The employer |
| **Licence range** | **$1,500 – $9,000** | **€4,500 – €28,000** |

### ⚠ Keep the two price books apart

- Separate proposal templates and separate one-pagers
- Regional pricing never published on the public site
- No published price list that shows both
- If a European buyer asks about regional rates, the answer is that pricing
  reflects local market conditions — the same answer every software vendor gives

## Caribbean price book — USD

### Organisational licences

| Package | Headcount | Annual | Per employee |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pilot** | One department, capped at 50 | **$750 / 3 months** | — |
| **Foundation** | Under 100 | **$1,500** | $15+ |
| **Growth** | 100–500 | **$4,000** | $8–40 |
| **Scale** | 500–2,000 | **$9,000** | $4.50–18 |
| **Enterprise** | 2,000+ | **Custom, from $15,000** | Negotiated |

Roughly a third of European bands. Included content is identical — full library,
whole-population access, quarterly sessions, launch kit.

### Workshops and retreats

| Offer | Delivery | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Lunch-and-learn | Remote or local, 45 min | **Free** |
| Team session | Remote, 90 min | **$450** |
| Half-day programme | Remote | **$900** |
| **Half-day, in person — Sint Maarten** | On site | **$1,400** |
| **Full-day retreat — Sint Maarten** | On site | **$2,500** |
| Half-day, in person — other islands | On site | **$1,800** + travel |
| Full-day retreat — other islands | On site | **$3,200** + travel |
| Conference or festival | On site | **Custom, from $1,500** |

**The local in-person margin is the regional advantage.** Delivering on Sint
Maarten costs you a taxi. Everywhere else in the world, in-person carries flights
and lost days. Locally, in-person is your differentiated, high-margin product —
and it is exactly what regional buyers prefer.

## The payer is often not the employer

Regional organisations frequently cannot fund this from operating budget but can
access it through a third party. **Qualify for funding route, not just budget.**

| Source | Applies to |
|---|---|
| **Development finance** — CDB, IDB and similar | Workforce development, health, resilience programmes |
| **Health insurers** | Wellness provision that reduces claims. Insurer-funded, employer-delivered |
| **Government workforce funds** | Training levies and skills development schemes |
| **Sector associations** | Hospitality and trade bodies buying once for many members |
| **Grant programmes** | NGO and public-health funded, often multi-year |
| **Parent company** | Regional subsidiaries of international groups — the budget sits abroad, and **European pricing may apply** |

**Qualifying question, early:** *"Would this come out of your budget, or is there
a training fund or insurer programme it could sit under?"*

**⚠ The association route is the highest-leverage regional play.** One hospitality
association buying a member-wide licence reaches dozens of employers through a
single sale and a single relationship. Worth pursuing ahead of individual
employers.

# TARGET PROFILES · CARIBBEAN

## 1 · BPO and contact centres — **highest fit**

**Jamaica, Dominican Republic, Trinidad, Guyana.** Jamaica's sector alone employs
tens of thousands.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Why it fits** | Agents absorb customer hostility for eight hours a day. Attrition is the sector's defining problem, it is measured monthly, and it has a known cost per head |
| **Why they buy** | Attrition is already a line item with a budget attached. You are not creating a category, you are entering an existing one |
| **Buyer** | Site Director · HR Manager · Operations Director |
| **Trigger** | Monthly attrition reporting · client SLA pressure · new campaign ramp |
| **Protocols** | Conflict Navigation · Burnout & Overload · Decision Fatigue · Shutdown Recovery |
| **Scale** | 500–3,000 per site. Scale and Enterprise bands |

**This is the strongest regional profile you have.** The pain is quantified, the
budget exists, the population is large and concentrated, and English is the
operating language.

## 2 · Hospitality and resorts

**Sint Maarten, Aruba, Curaçao, Bahamas, Dominican Republic, Jamaica.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Why it fits** | Front-of-house absorb guest behaviour all day and are required to perform warmth while doing it. High turnover, seasonal intensity |
| **Buyer** | HR Director · Training Manager · GM |
| **Trigger** | Pre-season onboarding · post-season debrief · turnover spike |
| **Protocols** | Conflict Navigation · Emotional labour · Burnout · plus Track 01 |
| **Access route** | Hospitality and trade associations before individual properties |

**Home advantage.** In-person delivery on the island costs you nothing in travel
and is what this buyer prefers.

## 3 · Financial services, trust and legal

**Cayman, BVI, Bahamas, Curaçao, Sint Maarten, Barbados.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Why it fits** | Review culture, visible assessment, high stakes. The most direct Track 03 match in the region |
| **Why they buy** | Highest regional budgets and the most Europe-like procurement. Many are subsidiaries — **check for a parent company, which may put them on European pricing** |
| **Buyer** | Managing Partner · HR Director · Compliance |
| **Headcount** | 50–400. Foundation and Growth bands |

## 4 · Healthcare

**Regional hospitals, clinics, medical centres.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Why it fits** | Burnout is named, understood and unstigmatised in this sector |
| **Why they buy** | Retention of clinical staff is existential and expensive |
| **Buyer** | HR Director · Chief Nursing Officer · Medical Director |
| **Route** | Dr. Bastien's network is the credible introduction here |
| **Funding** | Frequently grant or insurer funded rather than operating budget |

## 5 · Energy — Guyana and Trinidad

| | |
|---|---|
| **Why it fits** | Guyana's oil sector is building corporate infrastructure at speed. New HR functions, new budgets, international standards being imported |
| **Why they buy** | Duty-of-care requirements arrive with international operators. Provision must be demonstrated |
| **Buyer** | HSE Director · HR Director |
| **Note** | **Fastest-growing corporate market in the region.** Worth early positioning |

## 6 · Government, utilities, ports and airports

| | |
|---|---|
| **Why it fits** | Large, stable populations. Shift work, hierarchy, safety-critical roles |
| **Why they are hard** | Slow procurement, tender requirements, political cycles |
| **Approach** | Start early, expect months, use the free lunch-and-learn to build familiarity |

## 7 · Cruise and maritime crew

| | |
|---|---|
| **Why it fits** | Long contracts, extreme isolation, no privacy, documented mental-health strain. Device-only records matter more here than anywhere |
| **Why it is hard** | Jurisdictionally complex, connectivity constrained, procurement centralised in Miami |
| **Verdict** | ⚠ High resonance, high complexity. Not a first market — but a large one once references exist |

## 8 · NGOs and development organisations

| | |
|---|---|
| **Why it fits** | Burnout is endemic and openly discussed |
| **Why they buy** | Grant-funded, so wellbeing provision can be written into programme budgets |
| **Note** | Smaller populations, but the funding route is unusually accessible |

# TARGET PROFILES · EUROPE

## 1 · Remote-first companies — **highest fit**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Why it fits** | They physically cannot provide office-based wellbeing. No on-site counsellor, no wellness room, no yoga. A device-based, remote-delivered, privacy-preserving platform is the *only* format that works |
| **Why they buy** | Isolation is their known cultural risk and they actively look for interventions that suit async, self-directed work |
| **Buyer** | Head of People · People Ops Lead |
| **Headcount** | 100–800. Growth band |

**This is the strongest European profile and the one nobody is serving properly.**

## 2 · Tech and SaaS scale-ups

| | |
|---|---|
| **Why it fits** | Your network, your language, your credibility. Burnout is discussed openly |
| **Why they buy** | Retention of engineers is expensive and visible |
| **Buyer** | CHRO · People Director |
| **Route** | **Warm reactivation** — Jamf and HashiCorp contacts now several years more senior |

## 3 · Professional services and consultancies

| | |
|---|---|
| **Why it fits** | Up-or-out review culture, billable-hour pressure, constant assessment. Track 03 written for exactly this |
| **Buyer** | Partner responsible for People · L&D Director |
| **Note** | Higher budgets, longer cycles, strong referral behaviour between firms |

## 4 · Healthcare and health tech

| | |
|---|---|
| **Why it fits** | Burnout literacy is high and the clinical framing is understood without explanation |

## Prioritisation

| Rank | Profile | Market | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **BPO / contact centres** | Caribbean | Quantified pain, existing budget, large populations |
| 2 | **Remote-first companies** | Europe | Only viable format for them, unserved |
| 3 | **Tech / SaaS scale-ups** | Europe | Warm network already exists |
| 4 | **Hospitality associations** | Caribbean | One sale reaches many employers |
| 5 | **Financial services** | Both | Closest Track 03 match, best budgets |
| 6 | **Healthcare** | Both | Clinical route via Bastien |
| 7 | **Energy — Guyana** | Caribbean | Fastest-growing corporate market |
| 8 | Government and utilities | Caribbean | Real budgets, slow cycles |
| 9 | Cruise and maritime | Caribbean | High resonance, high complexity, later |

**Run one regional and one European profile simultaneously.** They have different
cycle lengths — regional closes in weeks, European in months — so running both
means the pipeline does not go quiet while enterprise deals mature.
