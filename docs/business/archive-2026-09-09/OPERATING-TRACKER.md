## Principle

State precedes thought. Thought colours emotion. Emotion drives reaction.
Regulate the state first; everything downstream is cheaper.

Method: bring it to the surface, then reverse-engineer the inquiry — what sits
under the layers of this state. Clarity arriving from that inquiry is signal.
Do not suppress it. Its job is to narrow the option set to the right ones.

Every roadblock gets met the same way. Not by thinking harder at it — by getting
coherent first, then looking. **I trust myself to solve every one of them through
heart coherence.**

Coherence does not remove the roadblock. It changes who is standing in front of
it. A dysregulated state hands back a shorter list of options, and usually the
wrong ones — the frustrated option, the one that reacts to whoever caused the
block rather than to the block itself. Regulating first is not a calming
exercise. It is how the full list stays visible.

*Private register only. Stated loosely — coherence solves problems — this becomes
an outcome promise, which the platform prohibits. It holds as a claim about state
and about which options stay visible, never about results.*
