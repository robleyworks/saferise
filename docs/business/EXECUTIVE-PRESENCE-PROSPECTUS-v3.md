# SafeRise — Executive Presence
## Working Prospectus v3 · Provisioning Edition

**Supersedes v2 (8 Sep). Changes: *The Reach* added at position 1; *The Meeting
After the Meeting* retired and its territory distributed into Peer to Boss and
Who You're Becoming, both of which score higher for it.**

v1 was designed around leadership behaviour and averaged 15.8 of 25, with four
protocols failing on arrival. v2 redistributed around transition and reached
22.8. v3 adds the entry state and folds the weakest protocol into two stronger
ones. **Average 23.4, nothing below 21.**

---

## 01 · Track identity

The pair for Professional Performance. Track 03 works your state while you are
being watched and judged. **This works your state when other people's outcomes
depend on you, and when the identity that got you here stops fitting.**

> **Central track principle**
> At every step up, the thing that got you there stops being the thing that
> keeps you there. Nobody tells you, you cannot practise it, and the people who
> could explain it are now your reports or your bosses.

**On the name.** "Executive presence" as ordinarily sold is image management —
posture, voice, gravitas. That is the opposite of this track's argument. Used in
SafeRise's sense the term is reclaimed: **presence is not how you appear, it is
the state other people have to regulate around when you walk in.** That
definition appears early and plainly or the track is bought for image coaching
and disappoints.

**Developmental arc.** Reach for it → take on the weight of others → lose your
place → lose your craft → commit to a future you cannot see → ask for what you
are worth → end someone's employment → hold what they told you → count what it
cost at home → look at who it made you.

**Audience width is a feature and a risk.** The Reach is anyone below a step.
The First Hire is a founder. Peer to Boss is a team lead. The Currency Changed
is senior corporate. That lets someone enter at their own altitude and stay as
they climb — but no protocol may assume a corporate structure, or every founder
and every person running a three-person team is lost.

## 02 · Overlap control against Track 03

The sharpest boundary on the platform, because both tracks live at work.

| Question | Professional Performance | Executive Presence |
|---|---|---|
| Whose state is the subject? | Yours, under observation | Yours, and what it costs others |
| Who is in the room? | People evaluating you | People depending on you |
| `t3-01` High-Stakes Presence | Surviving being watched | — |
| `t3-03` Imposter Dissolution | Found out in a role you hold | Protocol 1: disqualifying yourself from one you don't |
| `t3-03` continued | *Were you ever good* | Protocol 4: you *were* good, at something that stopped counting |
| `t3-06` Belonging Gap | Not knowing the shorthand | Protocol 3: knowing it, and the decisions have moved again |
| `t3-07` Career Transition | Should I stay | Protocol 1: should I reach · Protocol 5: accountable for predictions |
| `t3-09` Burnout & Overload | Depletion through rest | Protocol 2: isolation · Protocol 9: division |

**Rule.** If a protocol works for someone with no reports, it belongs in Track
03. If it only works for someone with reports, it belongs here. Anything that
works for both stays in Track 03, because that audience is larger. **Protocol 1
is the deliberate exception** — it serves people with no reports yet, and it is
the track's entry point precisely because that population is the largest.

## 04 · Protocol scope

### 1 · The Reach

**State.** Unsettled and long-running. Not a spike — a standing gap between
where you are and where you believe you could be, carried for months or years.
Signature: the sink when a role you could do is announced with someone else's
name on it.

**Arrival.** Someone less capable gets the thing. Or the posting you read four
times and did not apply for.

**Territory.** Wanting more and not going for it. The self-assessment that runs
before any external one — you disqualify yourself in private and never find out
whether you were right. Waiting to be noticed as a strategy, and why it fails:
nobody is tracking your readiness, and being good at your job is not a signal,
it is a reason to leave you where you are. Visibility as distinct from
performance, and the discomfort of being seen wanting something. The fear of
asking and being told no, avoided by never asking. Leaving as a legitimate move
rather than a betrayal — your value inside your own organisation is anchored to
who you were when you joined, and the same skills often price differently across
the street. Holding ambition somewhere that is not rewarding it.

**The turn.** You are waiting for permission that is not coming, from people who
are not thinking about you nearly as much as you assume. The gap is not
capability. It is that pursuit has to be visible, and being visible about
wanting something is the exact state you have been avoiding.

**Boundaries.** `t3-03` Imposter is fear of being found out in a role you hold;
this is disqualifying yourself from one you do not. `t3-07` Career Transition is
*should I stay*, a live decision with a deadline; this is *should I reach*, with
no deadline and no forcing function, which is why it goes unaddressed for years.
Protocol 3 is the aftermath; this is the approach. Protocol 6 is the negotiation
once you are in the room; this is everything before the room exists.

**Cycle.** Recognise the private disqualification · Regulate the state that
arrives when you consider being visible about wanting it · Release the belief
that being good at the work is the mechanism · Rise into the specific move you
have not been making.

**Safety.** No career tactics. No CV advice, no networking method, no interview
technique. This works the state that stops the move. **That line will be under
more pressure here than on any other protocol on the track.**

### 2 · The First Hire

**State.** Sustained low activation with a nocturnal signature — waking at the
same hour, running the runway.

**Arrival.** Payroll. The first bad month. Realising you cannot simply stop.

**Territory.** Someone's rent is attached to a decision you make. Not a
management problem, a weight problem. Risking your own money versus risking
someone's mortgage. What it does to decision-making — the conservatism that
arrives, and whether it is wisdom or fear. Being the person who cannot show
doubt to the people who most need to see it.

**The turn.** You did not become responsible for their outcome. You became
responsible for the conditions, and those are different — but your nervous
system has not learned the difference.

**Boundaries.** Nothing on the platform touches employer weight. `t3-08`
Decision Fatigue is depleted closing capacity; this is intact capacity carrying
consequence.

**Cycle.** Recognise the weight distinct from the work · Regulate the nocturnal
run · Release responsibility for outcomes you do not control · Rise into what
you actually owe them.

---

### 3 · Peer to Boss

**Highest scorer on the track.** Absorbs the positional half of the retired
politics protocol.

**State.** Social exclusion and elevated status arriving together, which the
body reads as threat while you are being congratulated.

**Arrival.** The first lunch you are not invited to. The conversation that stops
when you approach.

**Territory.** Yesterday's colleagues, today's reports. You gained a role and
lost a table in the same week, and the loss is what nobody warned you about.
Friendships that cannot survive the asymmetry and the ones that can. The
temptation to stay one of them, and what it costs when you need not to be. Being
resented for something you were given. Managing someone who wanted your job.
**And the second table:** decisions land that you had no idea were being made,
because the room you thought you joined has a room behind it. Access as distinct
from title. Hypervigilance for signals nobody taught you to read.

**The turn.** The isolation is not a sign you are doing it badly. It is
structural, it arrives with the role, and treating it as personal failure is
what makes people over-correct into either false authority or false friendship.

**Boundaries.** `t3-06` Belonging Gap is not knowing how a room works; this is
knowing exactly how it works and being removed from it — twice, at two
altitudes.

**Cycle.** Recognise the loss inside the gain · Regulate the pull to close the
gap · Release the belief that you can be both · Rise into the relationship and
the access that are actually available.

### 4 · The Currency Changed

**State.** Unsettled and continuous. Not a spike — a standing sense of being
paid for something you are no longer doing.

**Arrival.** A week where you produced nothing you can point at.

**Territory.** Promoted for excellence at the work, and the work is no longer
what you do. Your expertise is context now, not output. What happens to identity
when the thing you were best at becomes a thing you supervise. Skills decaying
while you watch. The academic background and hard-won knowledge that got you
here and no longer maps. Delegation as identity loss rather than trust failure.
Being outperformed at your old craft by someone you hired.

**The turn.** You were good. You are still good. The market for what you are
good at has moved inside your own job, and grief is a reasonable response.

**Boundaries.** **Critical against `t3-03` Imposter Dissolution**, which is
*waiting to be found out* — a verdict on whether you were ever competent. This
is the exact inverse: you *were* competent, at something that stopped counting.

**Cycle.** Recognise the grief for the old work · Regulate the reflex to reclaim
it · Release the equation of worth with output · Rise into what the new work
actually is.

---

### 5 · Committing to a Future You Can't See

**State.** Maintained unsettledness from ongoing accountability for predictions.

**Arrival.** The number is due and you do not have grounds for it.

**Territory.** Forecasts, targets and commitments you will be held to about
conditions you do not control. The dread of putting a figure in writing. Longer
time horizons than you have worked to, and what that does to tolerance for
ambiguity. Individual hard decisions inside those commitments, including ones
whose cost falls on somebody else. Anticipated guilt as a load distinct from
anticipated failure.

**The turn.** You are not being asked to be right. You are being asked to commit
in public with incomplete information and stay functional while it resolves —
and nobody names that as a separate skill, so people experience it as a
character defect.

**Boundaries.** `t3-07` is one unresolved decision about your own life. `t3-08`
is depleted closing capacity. This is standing exposure to consequences you
predicted.

**Cycle.** Recognise the dread as separate from the analysis · Regulate the
scanning for certainty that will not arrive · Release the requirement to be
right · Rise into committing anyway.

---

### 6 · Asking for More

**State.** Mobilised, sharp somatic signature — heat, dry mouth, and the reflex
to discount before anyone else has spoken.

**Arrival.** The conversation is scheduled.

**Territory.** Negotiating your own worth. The dread of naming a number. The
reflex to soften, hedge or pre-concede. A sense of what you are worth set at an
earlier altitude and never updated. Repositioning earning potential as you move
up. What happens in your body when someone pauses after you say the figure.
Asking for resource and headcount, not only salary.

**The turn.** The discount happens before the conversation. By the time you are
in the room you have already negotiated with yourself and lost, and that is the
negotiation this works on.

**Boundaries.** **Nothing on the platform touches money.** `t2-01` Safe
Conversation is anticipated relational difficulty; this is anticipated
self-advocacy with a number attached.

**Cycle.** Recognise the pre-concession · Regulate the state before the ask ·
Release the valuation set at a previous altitude · Rise into naming it and
staying quiet after.

**Safety.** No negotiation tactics, no scripts, no figures.

**Note.** Highest reach on the list and the protocol most likely to make someone
recommend the track.

---

### 7 · The Person You Have to Let Go

**State.** Anticipatory mobilisation with guilt, under total power asymmetry.

**Arrival.** The decision is made and the conversation is not yet had.

**Territory.** Ending someone's employment. The days between deciding and doing.
Whether it is fair, and living with it when you believe it is. Their family,
which you know about. The performance of composure while delivering it. What
happens to you afterwards and why nobody asks. Doing it when the business
required it rather than the person deserving it — harder, not easier.

**The turn.** Doing it well does not mean feeling nothing. The composure is for
them; what you feel is yours and does not need to be argued away.

**Boundaries.** Nothing on the platform is close.

**Cycle.** Recognise the dread and the guilt as separate loads · Regulate the
days before · Release the requirement to feel resolved · Rise into carrying it
without hardening or collapsing.

**Safety.** No legal or HR procedure. State only.

---

### 8 · Carrying What They Told You

**State.** Sustained load with no discharge route. Intrusive recall of what they
said.

**Arrival.** They told you something heavy and you are still holding it.

**Territory.** A report discloses illness, bereavement, addiction, abuse,
financial collapse, or their own mental health. You are not trained, you have no
supervision, and you cannot repeat it. Confidentiality and its actual limits.
The pull to fix it. The pull to become their support. What carrying someone
else's state does to your own. Routing them without abandoning them.

**The turn.** Being trusted with something is not being responsible for it, and
the boundary is not coldness — it is the only thing that keeps you able to help
at all.

**Boundaries.** `t2-08` Appreciation & Support is relational and reduced. This
is a duty of care you did not train for.

**Cycle.** Recognise the load as yours to carry rather than solve · Regulate the
intrusive recall · Release responsibility for their outcome · Rise into routing
and staying present.

**Safety.** Pairs with *When It's Not Yours to Hold*, non-optional here. This
protocol is the platform's clearest opportunity to stop a manager doing
untrained clinical work on a report, and that should be explicit in the writing.

### 9 · What It's Costing at Home

**State.** Reduced with intermittent sharp guilt. Flattened at home while
functioning at work.

**Arrival.** The thing you missed.

**Territory.** Hours, travel, distance, the school event, the phone at dinner.
The role is being paid for by people who did not get promoted. Present in body,
absent in attention. The bargain you made without consulting anyone. Whether the
money justifies it, and who decides. Resentment arriving from people you are
doing it for. What happens to a relationship that reorganises around your
absence. Coming home unable to switch registers.

**The turn.** Nobody at work will tell you it is enough, and nobody at home will
tell you the money made it worth it. The scales are not commensurable, and
looking for the exchange rate is itself part of what is costing you.

**Boundaries.** Track 02 works the dynamic between two people. This works the
price a job charges a household. `t3-09` Burnout is depletion from output; this
is depletion from division.

**Cycle.** Recognise the cost without immediately defending it · Regulate the
guilt so it can be looked at · Release the arithmetic that will never balance ·
Rise into a decision about what you will actually change.

**Note.** Emotionally the heaviest on the track and unaddressed anywhere else on
the platform.

### 10 · Who You're Becoming

**Absorbs the comportment half of the retired politics protocol**, which fixed
its missing arrival: it now scores 4 on nervous system and cycle rather than 3.

**State.** Unsettled, low and continuous. The disquiet of not recognising your
own behaviour.

**Arrival.** You catch yourself performing a register that is not yours.
Somebody who knew you before says something. Or criticism arrives from below and
lands.

**Territory.** Dress, register, comportment — the unwritten code and the cost of
reading it wrong, and the fact that you adjusted to it without deciding to. You
speak differently, hold more back, are harder in meetings than you used to be.
Growth or drift, integrity or performance. Whether learning to play is integrity
loss or competence, and which rules you will not learn. The culture you absorbed
and whether you chose it. Criticism from people who report to you, the sharpest
available evidence that you have changed in ways you cannot see. Closes with the
doctrine's requirement: *is the pattern I am seeing in them coming from me?*

**The turn.** Some of the change is adaptation and some is erosion, and they
feel identical from inside. The only way to tell is to check them against
something you decided before the role — which is why this is last and not first.

**Boundaries.** `t2-07` Projection Clarity is one disproportionate reaction;
this is a standing question about identity drift. `t3-06` Belonging Gap is not
having the shorthand; this is having adopted it and wondering what it cost.

**Cycle.** Recognise the change without immediately grading it · Regulate the
defensiveness · Release the need to be the same person you were · Rise into
choosing which changes you keep.

## 05 · Resource architecture

Nine universal resources unchanged. Proximity Guide applies on 3 and 9;
Invitation to Repair on 3, 9 and 10; Raising It on 1, 5 and 6.

**Two new conditionals.**

**The Hard Message.** The structural mirror of Raising It — naming something
while holding the power rather than while subject to it. Separating the message
from the apology. What softening costs the person. Delivering from a regulated
state. Saying it once. Clarity versus bluntness. **Not a performance-management
method; carries no HR or legal procedure.** Attaches to 5, 7 and 10.

**When It's Not Yours to Hold.** Recognising that someone you are responsible
for needs more than you can give, and routing rather than absorbing. Signals
beyond a manager's role. Support versus untrained clinical work. What to say
when someone discloses something you are not equipped for. Confidentiality and
its limits. What carrying another person's state does to yours. **Non-optional
on 2, 7 and 8; available on all ten.** The most safety-relevant resource
proposed across the three new tracks.

## 06 · Safety and scope

- **Not a management course.** No performance-management method, no HR
  procedure, no legal guidance on dismissal, discipline or grievance.
- **Not a careers course.** Protocol 1 is where this is most at risk.
- **No diagnosing of reports.** Patterns are described about the member. This is
  the platform's hardest boundary and this track strains it most.
- **The manager is not a clinician.**
- **No implied outcomes.** Nothing suggests a team will improve, retention will
  rise, or anyone will be promoted.
- **No money advice on protocol 6.**
- **Protocol 10 must not become self-blame.** A team's state is neither proof of
  your failure nor of your innocence.

## 07 · Commercial fit and its risk

The only one of the three new tracks with an existing revenue path — corporate
retreats and B2B contracts have a buyer for this and none for intimacy or
elevation. It is the natural anchor for the corporate offer, since the person
signing is usually the person the track is about.

**The risk is writing it for the buyer.** A leadership track written to please a
company becomes a productivity product. Every protocol must remain useful to the
person running it even when it is inconvenient for their employer — protocols 1,
9 and 10 in particular, which are exactly the ones a corporate buyer would want
softened. Protocol 1 openly treats leaving as a legitimate move.

   definition, stated early. Alternatives to test: *Responsible for Others*,
   *The Weight of the Role*, or a two-level name with Executive Presence as
   descriptor. Note protocol 1 sits below the executive altitude, which the name
   should not exclude.
2. Track number, once Sex & Intimacy and Elevation are placed.
3. Whether *When It's Not Yours to Hold* goes platform-wide — obvious
   application in Track 02.
4. Whether the corporate offer launches with the track or after it.
5. Whether Philosophy §10's four figures appear here. They carry a standing
   verification requirement and a permanent safeguard, and a leadership track is
   exactly where someone would reach for them.
