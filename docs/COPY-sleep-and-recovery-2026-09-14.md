## Promise line

> Sleep is not something you do. It is something that arrives once you stop.

---

## Body copy

> The mind that will not close. The wake at three with nothing wrong and no route
> back. The hour taken back at the end of a day that took everything, because it
> was the only hour that was yours.
>
> Everyone has a rule for this too. Darker, cooler, earlier, no screens. None of
> it reaches the part that is still going — and *being told to relax is its own
> kind of pressure* when the body has already decided otherwise.
>
> This does not manage your sleep. It works the states that sit on either side of
> it: what is still running when you lie down, and what you wake into.

## Why this one earns the space

Worth having in mind while reviewing the copy.

**It is the strongest evidence on the platform.** Sleep loss measurably degrades
prefrontal–amygdala connectivity. No other substrate claim is as well supported,
and this track can say so without overclaiming.

**It is the least stigmatised entry point.** Someone who would never open an
anxiety protocol will open one about not sleeping. That makes it the best
recruiting track in the library.

**It is named in three of the six organisational sector bundles** — healthcare,
manufacturing and logistics, technology. Those bundles currently reference a track
that does not exist.

**And it ships without waiting on anyone.** Nutrition needs clinical review;
Intimacy and Addiction Recovery need governance. Sleep needs a routing note. That
makes it the right one to build first.
