Extends the existing image register. IMG-051–081 are the protocol posters (abstract, no

---

## 1 · What SafeRise is, in one paragraph

A nervous-system regulation platform. Thirty self-guided audio sessions for the moments
when anxiety, anger, overwhelm, conflict or pressure start taking over, plus workshops,
one-to-one coaching, retreats and programmes for organisations.

It is not therapy and does not present itself as clinical. It does not promise outcomes.
The register throughout is plain, warm and unsentimental — a capable adult talking to
another capable adult, not a wellness brand talking to a patient.

---

## 2 · Who these images are of — read this before generating anything

**Age 27–37.** Not students, not middle-aged. People established enough to have real
pressure on them.

**Caribbean and European.** Both, genuinely mixed across the set, not one token image.
Black, mixed-race, South Asian, White European, Latin. **No set of images should read as
one ethnicity with exceptions.**

**Upper middle class, and it should show** — in the rooms, the materials, the quality of
what they're wearing. Not wealth on display. The kind of money that buys good light and
a decent apartment.

**Lively, trendy, contemporary.** Colour, pattern, texture, jewellery, interesting
eyewear, good hair. Real personal style.

### What this is not

**Not minimalist wellness.** No beige linen, no oatmeal knitwear, no white loungewear, no
"quiet luxury". That aesthetic is the entire category's default and it is the opposite of
this audience.

**Not a meditation app.** No lotus positions, no mats, no candles, no incense, no hands
in prayer, no eyes closed in bliss, no sunrise, no mountain, no beach at golden hour.

**Not stock-photo happy.** No laughing at salad, no arms-outstretched freedom pose, no
group high-five. **Nobody is having a breakthrough.**

**Not clinical.** No clipboards, no consulting rooms, no white coats, no couches.

---

## 3 · The register of the people

Composed, not serene. Present, not transported. Someone who has had a difficult week and
is handling it — not someone who has transcended anything.

**Eyes open in most frames.** Closed eyes read as meditation stock and as absence. This
product is about attention, and attention looks like someone looking at something.

**Ordinary settings.** A good apartment, an office, a car, a street, a kitchen, a
stairwell. Real places at real times of day.

**Solitude reads as choice, not isolation.** A person alone in a frame should look
self-possessed, not abandoned.

## 4 · Technical specification

```
Aspect      16:9  · 2400 × 1350   (heroes, bands)
            4:5   · 1600 × 2000   (cards, portrait)
            1:1   · 1600 × 1600   (avatars, tiles)
Format      JPEG q94 master · WebP q82 for delivery
Colour      sRGB
Naming      img-200-hero-workshop-remote.jpg
```

**Grade to match the platform.** Dark ground `#0C0C12`, gold `#E0B658`, warm ink
`#EAE2CE`. Images sit on a near-black page — favour deep shadow, warm highlights, and
real contrast. Nothing washed out, nothing pastel, nothing cool-blue.

**Grain is welcome.** Slight, filmic. The platform is not glossy.

**Leave headroom for text.** Any image marked *hero* or *band* carries live copy over it.
Compose with a quiet third — usually left — where type can sit without a scrim.

**No text burned in, ever.** No wordmark, no captions. Titles are live text.

## 6 · Diversity, concretely

Across IMG-200 to 224 there are **21 images containing recognisable people.** The set
must land roughly:

- **7–8** Black or Caribbean
- **4–5** South Asian or East Asian
- **5–6** White European
- **4–5** mixed race or Latin

**Check the set, not the image.** Each one generated alone will drift toward the model's
default, and the default is White. Generate, lay them out together, and re-generate
whatever makes the set lopsided.

Gender roughly even. Body types not uniformly slim.

---

## 7 · Rejection criteria

Reject and regenerate if any of these appear, regardless of how good the image looks:

- Beige, oatmeal, cream or white loungewear
- Eyes closed in more than two images across the whole set
- Lotus position, mat, candle, incense, singing bowl
- Sunrise, sunset, mountain, beach, horizon silhouette
- Anyone mid-breakthrough, arms out, face upturned
- Clinical setting, clipboard, couch, consulting room
- Text of any kind burned into the frame
- A set that reads as predominantly one ethnicity
- Cool blue or washed-out grade — these sit on near-black and must hold contrast

One folder, flat, named as §4. JPEG q94 masters; WebP conversion happens in the build,
not here.

**Send the full set together, not one at a time.** Section 6 can only be judged across
