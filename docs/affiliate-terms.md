## 1 · What this is

SafeRise pays a commission when someone you refer becomes a paying member.

It is open to coaches, therapists, practitioners, writers and creators already
talking to people about nervous-system regulation. It is not an open sign-up
programme, and every application is reviewed by a person.

---

## 2 · Commission

| | |
|---|---|
| **Rate** | **30% of subscription revenue** |
| **Duration** | **12 months** from the referred member's first payment |
| **Applies to** | Subscription only — Personal, Relationship, Professional |
| **Does not apply to** | Premium 1:1 · workshops · retreats · corporate licences |

**Worked example.** A referral on the Relationship plan at €29/month who stays a
year returns **€104.40**. On Professional at €39, **€140.40**.

⚠ **DECISION — the rate.** 30% for 12 months is mid-market for a subscription at
this price. 20% lifetime and 40% first-year are both defensible alternatives. The
12-month cap is deliberate: an unbounded lifetime rate becomes an unpriceable
liability against a subscription with no churn history.

**Why 1:1 and corporate are excluded.** Those are delivered by the founder
personally and calendar-capped. A commission on a fixed number of hours reduces
the hourly rate rather than growing the business.

## 3 · Attribution

| | |
|---|---|
| **Cookie window** | **60 days** |
| **Attribution model** | Last click |
| **Cross-device** | Not tracked. A member who clicks on a phone and buys on a laptop may not attribute |
| **Self-referral** | Not permitted, and not paid |

⚠ **DECISION — the window.** 60 days is standard. This purchase is often
considered over weeks rather than minutes, which argues for 90. Longer windows
cost nothing directly but make attribution disputes more likely.

**Attribution is cookie-based, so it is imperfect and we say so.** A member using
private browsing, blocking cookies, or clearing them will not attribute. We will
not add fingerprinting or cross-device tracking to close that gap — it would
contradict the privacy position the product is sold on.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Threshold** | **€50** — balances below this roll forward |
| **Schedule** | Monthly, within 30 days of month end |
| **Method** | Bank transfer. You provide the details |

## 5 · Approval

Applications are reviewed manually. We are looking for people **already in
conversation with the people this is for** — not for volume.

**We will usually decline:** coupon and deal sites · cashback sites · sites whose
traffic is primarily search arbitrage · anyone we cannot identify · anyone whose
existing content makes health claims we would not make ourselves.

We do not have to give a reason, and we will where we can.

## 6 · What you may and may not say

**This section is the reason the programme is reviewed rather than open.** An
affiliate's claim is a claim about SafeRise, and Kenor International B.V. carries
it.

### You may

- Describe your own experience, as your experience
- Describe what the product contains — protocols, resources, the four steps
- Quote our published copy
- Say that it is self-guided nervous-system regulation grounded in
  peer-reviewed research

### You may not

- **Promise an outcome.** Not *"this will fix your anxiety"*, not *"this cured
  my burnout"*, not *"you will feel better"*
- **Present it as therapy, treatment or a substitute for either**
- **Describe it as clinically proven, clinically trialled or evidence-based
  treatment.** It is built on established frameworks. It has not been trialled
- **Make physiological claims we do not make** — nothing about rewiring,
  frequencies, energy fields or brain chemistry
- **Diagnose anyone**, or imply the product diagnoses
- **Use "quantum", "manifest", "rewire" or "frequency"** in connection with
  SafeRise. These are prohibited platform-wide and the rule extends to you
- **Bid on "SafeRise" or close variants** in paid search
- **Present yourself as SafeRise**, or as employed by or speaking for it
- **Send unsolicited email**, or promote in a way that would breach GDPR or
  local marketing law

### ⚠ If you are a practising clinician, therapist or coach

**Earning commission on a referral to a client is a conflict of interest**, and
most professional codes require it to be disclosed or prohibit it outright.

We will not knowingly pay commission on a referral from a practitioner to their
own client. **Promote to your audience, not to the people in your care** — and if
your professional body's rules differ from this, follow theirs.

### Breach

We may withhold payment, close the account, and require correction. For claims
that create risk for members, we will do so immediately and without notice.

## 7 · Termination

Either side, any time, in writing. Commission already earned on referrals already
converted is paid at the next scheduled payment. Attribution stops at
termination.

We may terminate immediately for a breach of section 6.

---

## 8 · Changes

We may change these terms with **30 days' notice** by email. Changes do not apply
retroactively to commission already earned. If a change is unacceptable, close
the account and take the earned balance.
